BARBARIAN ◆ DPS

BLADEMASTER

Rage ◆ Battlerage ◆ Hit Everything Very Hard

Overview Stats Gear Enchantments Artifacts Companions Mounts Powers & Feats Boons Rotation

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Blademaster is the damage-focused Barbarian paragon: a fast, aggressive melee DPS built around generating Rage, entering Battlerage and unloading encounters while Rage is active.

The basic idea is simple. Hit things to build Rage, activate Battlerage at the right moment, use your strongest encounters and keep attacking while they recharge.

The clever bit is deciding when to spend Rage and when to save it. For bosses, you want full Rage and your encounters ready when the group's next artifact call begins. Against packs, you can be much more aggressive and enter Battlerage whenever it becomes available.

THE BARBARIAN RULE: Rage is a resource, not a decorative red bar. Build it, spend it deliberately and make sure you've got it when the important damage window arrives.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Blademaster uses the standard offensive DPS stats, but the finished percentages matter more than raw ratings. Balance the whole toon after gear, companions, insignias, enchantments, consumables and group buffs are taken into account.

POWER

One of the primary offensive stats and a major priority for the finished build.

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

As valuable as Power while you are actually gaining Combat Advantage. Position yourself correctly around bosses and grouped enemies.

CRITICAL STRIKE

Build this alongside Critical Severity. The two stats depend on each other for consistent critical damage.

CRITICAL SEVERITY

Get Critical Strike and Critical Severity into a strong position together before pushing Severity toward its higher ceiling.

ACCURACY

Useful for reducing the impact of enemy deflects, but generally the last offensive stat to maximise.

ABILITY SCORES

Strength is particularly important to Blademaster because Barbarian deals physical damage. Build around Strength as your primary damage ability score.

DON'T CHASE ITEM LEVEL: Higher item level affects ratings as well as increasing your overall strength. Judge gear by its useful stats and bonuses, not simply by whichever number is biggest.

LOADOUT

GEAR

This build has a current Module 33.5 gear progression, so you can work toward the endgame setup rather than assuming every slot has to be completed immediately.

WEAPONS

Current artifact weapons are the progression target. If you don't want to upgrade them immediately, Voidtouched or Xaryxian weapons remain viable alternatives.

ARMS

Bindings of the Death Pact from Master Shackles of Divinity are particularly strong for multi-target damage.

SCALED CONTENT

Astral Raider's Gloves remain extremely useful in scaled content and can outperform higher-item-level arms there.

BOOTS

Celestial boots can provide a strong damage bonus, but only use them if you're comfortable trading a substantial amount of Health for that extra damage.

GEAR PROGRESSION: Build toward the endgame setup as you unlock content. A sensible progression path beats wearing terrible equipment purely because it appears on somebody's finished BIS sheet.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Use enchantments and reinforcements to finish your offensive stat balance. Check your actual percentages before deciding which rating each slot needs.

OFFENSIVE ENCHANTMENTS

Fill whichever of Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy your current setup is missing.

REINFORCEMENTS

Armour kits and jewels are useful for correcting smaller stat gaps without replacing otherwise excellent equipment.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Use a strong current damage or group-debuff artifact as your active artifact. Secondary artifacts should support your required stats and useful set bonuses.

Artifact calls are especially important to Blademaster because Rage needs to be prepared before the call begins. Going into a major group damage window with an empty Rage bar defeats half the point of having one.

BEFORE THE CALL: Have Rage ready and your encounters off cooldown. The artifact window is where you want Battlerage and your heavy encounters working together.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Use companion bonuses to improve personal damage, complete your offensive stats and contribute useful debuffs to organised groups.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Use a strong current damage companion suited to the content you are running.

PLAYER BONUSES

Select offensive bonuses according to the stats and damage effects your finished Blademaster needs.

COMPANION GEAR

Companion equipment is another major source of ratings. Use it to correct your final offensive stat balance.

GROUP DEBUFFS

Companion enhancements can reduce enemy defensive stats, but coordinate with the party because identical reductions are not designed to stack.

COORDINATE: If somebody else is already providing the same companion debuff, use a different one. Five people bringing the same thing does not magically make it five times better.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Your mount setup should provide useful combat damage, offensive stats, cooldown support and movement.

SINGLE-TARGET COMBAT POWER

For bosses, use a strong current single-target mount combat power. Save it for your major Battlerage and artifact-call damage window.

MULTI-TARGET COMBAT POWER

Against grouped enemies, switch to a multi-target mount power where appropriate. The Legendary Flying Carpet is one useful option.

INSIGNIA BONUSES

  • Tactician's Precision — using a Daily reduces remaining encounter cooldowns.
  • Artificer's Persuasion — using an artifact helps reduce remaining cooldowns.
  • Gladiator's Guile — useful movement and stamina support for melee combat.
  • Warlord's Motivation — increases summoned companion damage.
  • Executioner's Covenant — trades defensive ratings for additional offensive ratings.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Blademaster has three useful ways to configure its powers: straightforward sustained boss damage, harder burst-focused boss damage and multi-target damage.

SINGLE-TARGET SUSTAIN

This is the easier boss setup and is designed for fights where you can maintain high uptime on the target.

BRASH STRIKE

Your primary single-target damaging at-will.

RELENTLESS SLASH

Used to maintain its damage buff and can also contribute to faster Rage generation.

RELENTLESS BATTLERAGE

Provides much faster Rage generation, making the sustained setup easier to play and helping ensure Rage is ready for artifact calls.

IMPATIENCE

Optional class-feature choice if you want additional cooldown support and more frequent encounter casts.

SINGLE-TARGET BURST

The burst variation sacrifices some easy Rage generation in exchange for a higher damage ceiling during boss artifact calls.

ESCALATING RAGE

The centre of the burst setup. Rage generation becomes slower, so entering Battlerage at the correct moment becomes much more important.

OVERPENETRATION

Used for the higher-damage burst setup when your stat balance supports it.

BRUTAL CRITICAL

Can replace Overpenetration if you want to sacrifice some damage for easier Rage generation.

RAGING STRIKES

Damage-focused option, while Impatience can be substituted for a smoother rotation and stronger sustain between artifact calls.

MULTI-TARGET

RELENTLESS SLASH

Your primary multi-target at-will. Swap back to Brash Strike once only one enemy remains.

HIDDEN DAGGER

Useful multi-target encounter, but don't cast it back-to-back. Put another encounter or at-will between casts.

MIGHTY LEAP

Alternative AoE encounter if you prefer the mobility and area damage it provides.

ROAR

Another viable multi-target option depending on the content and your preferred setup.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise offensive boons that contribute to Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy according to the stats your finished build still needs.

Once your offensive choices are covered, use remaining points for survivability and utility. Blademaster lives in melee range, and being horizontal on the floor is generally considered a fairly poor DPS rotation.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

SINGLE-TARGET SUSTAIN

  1. RELENTLESS SLASH Establish and maintain the damage buff.
  2. BRASH STRIKE Use your primary at-will to generate Rage and deal damage.
  3. BUILD RAGE Continue attacking until you have enough Rage to enter Battlerage.
  4. ENTER BATTLERAGE Activate Battlerage and immediately begin your encounter sequence.
  5. USE ENCOUNTERS Fire your encounters during Battlerage.
  6. RETURN TO BRASH STRIKE Continue damage and rebuild Rage while encounters recharge.
  7. PREP FOR ARTIFACT CALL Make sure Rage is full and encounters are available before the next coordinated burst window.

SINGLE-TARGET BURST

The Escalating Rage version is less forgiving. Rage regenerates much more slowly, so you need to learn the timing of each boss and avoid wasting Battlerage immediately before the next artifact call.

  1. GENERATE RAGE Use Brash Strike and Relentless Slash while preparing the burst.
  2. WAIT FOR ESCALATING RAGE Make sure the buff and Rage are ready for the important damage window.
  3. ENTER BATTLERAGE Activate Rage during the artifact call.
  4. UNLOAD ENCOUNTERS Use your encounters aggressively while Rage is active.
  5. BRASH STRIKE BETWEEN CASTS Continue using your main at-will while maintaining the Relentless Slash buff.
  6. SECOND ENCOUNTER PASS Aim to use the encounters again before the Rage window ends.

MULTI-TARGET

  1. GENERATE RAGE Use encounters and Relentless Slash while engaging the pack.
  2. ENTER BATTLERAGE Against groups, activate Battlerage whenever practical rather than hoarding Rage unnecessarily.
  3. USE ENCOUNTERS Dump the available AoE encounters into the grouped enemies.
  4. RELENTLESS SLASH Use your main multi-target at-will while encounters recharge.
  5. DON'T DOUBLE HIDDEN DAGGER Put another encounter or at-will between Hidden Dagger casts.
  6. DAILY + MOUNT IN RAGE Enter Battlerage before using your Daily and multi-target mount combat power where possible.
THE DIFFERENCE: Sustain Blademaster spends Rage freely and rebuilds it quickly. Burst Blademaster treats Rage like the last biscuit in the packet and guards the bastard until the artifact call. 😂

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

RELENTLESS BATTLERAGE VS ESCALATING RAGE

Relentless Battlerage is the easier and more consistent option for sustained boss damage. Escalating Rage offers greater burst potential but demands much better Rage management and timing.

OVERPENETRATION VS BRUTAL CRITICAL

Overpenetration belongs in the harder maximum-burst setup. Brutal Critical can be used when you want easier Rage regeneration and a more forgiving rotation.

RAGING STRIKES VS IMPATIENCE

Raging Strikes pushes damage when your stats are already in a good place. Impatience gives additional cooldown support and can make the rotation noticeably smoother.

MULTI-TARGET OPTIONS

Blademaster has several viable AoE alternatives including Mighty Leap and Roar. Barbed Strikes can also be useful when the offensive stats it provides are not already capped.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Blademaster has a much more forgiving sustained setup and a harder maximum-burst setup. If you're learning the toon, start with the sustained version and get comfortable managing Rage before making life unnecessarily difficult.

Current guild variations, dungeon-specific swaps and screenshots will be added here as we collect them.

And if you've entered the artifact call with no Rage, we are all judging you quietly. 😂
