THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Vanguard is the Fighter's dedicated tank path: a heavily defensive front-line build designed to control threat, absorb incoming damage through its shield and keep bosses facing exactly where the party needs them.

The current build combines high defensive stats with aggressive threat generation. Shield Throw, Anvil of Doom, Retaliate and Tide of Iron form the heart of the single-target threat engine, while the multi-target setup swaps into stronger pack control and AoE threat tools.

Unlike a tank that simply hides behind its shield, Vanguard benefits from attacking deliberately. The aim is to create enough threat that the DPS can unload during artifact calls without suddenly discovering they've been promoted to tank.

THE JOB: Hold threat. Position the boss. Block the horrible shit. Give the group useful support. Preferably all at the same time. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Vanguard needs enough defensive percentage to survive the hardest mechanics while retaining enough offensive capability to generate reliable threat.

1 ◆ MAXIMUM HIT POINTS More health gives the healer a larger safety margin and helps you survive heavy tank-busters.
2 ◆ DEFENSE Core mitigation. Build it strongly but watch for overcapping once temporary buffs and gear effects activate.
3 ◆ AWARENESS Important protection against Combat Advantage damage and a major part of the current defensive setup.
4 ◆ CRITICAL AVOIDANCE Reduces the danger from critical hits and helps smooth incoming damage.
5 ◆ DEFLECT Adds another layer of mitigation once the core defensive stats are established.
6 ◆ DEFLECT SEVERITY Improves the value of successful Deflects and becomes increasingly useful as the rest of the defensive package comes together.
ABILITY SCORES: Constitution is the main defensive investment, with Charisma used alongside it in the current Module 33.5 setup.

LOADOUT

GEAR

The current Module 33.5 Vanguard setup is built for endgame tanking, with gear chosen around survivability, threat generation and useful party support rather than simply chasing the highest item level.

HEAD

Use a current tank headpiece providing useful defensive percentages or a reliable group-support effect.

ARMOUR

Prioritise strong defensive bonuses that remain useful during sustained boss encounters.

ARMS

Current tank arms should contribute to survivability, threat or group support without pushing already-capped defensive stats too far.

FEET

Use the slot to complete missing defensive percentages or gain a useful tanking effect.

WEAPONS

Current endgame weapons should support both the defensive build and the damage required for reliable threat generation.

RINGS

Balance defensive ratings against useful threat and support effects. Don't sacrifice a strong bonus just because another ring has a larger item-level number.

NECK & WAIST

Choose a current tank or support set that complements the rest of the defensive spread.

SHIRT & TROUSERS

Finish the remaining defensive ratings and use bonuses that actually activate reliably in the content you're running.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

The current build uses tank enchantments and modifications to fill whichever defensive percentages are still missing after gear, companions and temporary bonuses are included.

CELESTIAL RIME TEMPER

Recommended defensive combat enchantment in the current setup, providing maximum hit points and incoming-damage reduction.

CELESTIAL LIGHTNING FLASH

Alternative offensive combat enchantment when threat and damage are more valuable than additional mitigation.

Defensive enchantment slots should primarily finish Awareness, Defense, Critical Avoidance and other missing tank percentages. Offensive slots can then be used to support threat generation.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Vanguard is a support role, so the active artifact should normally contribute to the party's coordinated burst rather than simply providing selfish damage.

PRIMARY ARTIFACT

Use the group buff or enemy debuff assigned to you for the artifact call.

SECONDARY ARTIFACTS

Use secondary artifacts to complete defensive percentages, maximum hit points and useful set bonuses.

SUPPORT RULE: Mount first, artifact second. Some mount powers have an activation delay, so firing the artifact first can leave your support effects arriving after the DPS have already blown half their burst.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Vanguard companion choices can be split between personal survivability and group damage support. Strong groups generally let the tank lean further into support, while progression groups may need more defensive bonuses.

RAPTOR'S INSTINCTS

Strong coordinated Power bonus when the party is built around Raptor bonuses.

UNYIELDING OVERLORD

Defensive companion bonus used within the current tank setup.

JACOB BLACK'S INSIGHT

Offensive option that can help increase threat through stronger personal damage.

BRUENOR'S BULWARK

Defensive option for strengthening the tank package when extra survivability is needed.

SPITEFUL HEX

Utility and defensive option within the current setup.

COMPANION ENHANCEMENT

Armor Break is a strong group option. Dulled Senses and Vulnerability can also be coordinated across the party so the group covers multiple debuffs rather than stacking the same one.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Vanguard uses mount powers for both party support and tank utility. The current setup combines a support combat power with defensive and cooldown-focused insignia bonuses.

PERILOUS POLYMORPH POTION

Current support combat power that debuffs the enemy and helps increase party damage during coordinated calls.

PACK TACTICS

Current mount equip power used to support the party.

SWIFTSTRIDE

Movement option that makes repositioning and handling mechanics considerably less painful.

INSIGNIA BONUSES

  • Cavalry's Haste — reduces encounter cooldowns after using a mount combat power.
  • Gladiator's Guile — movement while stamina is high and stamina regeneration while it is low.
  • Tactician's Precision — cooldown reduction after using a daily.
  • Guardian's Spirit — increases Defense and maximum hit points.

MOUNT COLLARS

  • Practical Regal — Rough Astral Diamond bonus
  • Sturdy Crescent — Encounter Power damage
  • Unified Crescent — Movement Speed
  • Wayfaring Barbed — Critical Severity
  • Supportive Crescent — Stamina Gain

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Vanguard uses separate single-target and multi-target configurations. Boss tanking focuses on concentrated threat, while the pack setup adds stronger AoE control and threat generation.

SINGLE TARGET

BRAZEN SLASH

Main filler at-will between encounter cycles during normal single-target tanking.

TIDE OF IRON

Key part of the threat engine. Use it immediately before your heavy threat encounters to increase their effectiveness.

SHIELD THROW

One of the main threat encounters and an important part of the artifact-call sequence.

ANVIL OF DOOM

Heavy single-target threat tool. With Anvil of Challenge it can also be used to jump directly to the top of the target's threat list when required.

RETALIATE

Strong threat and damage tool. Use the double Retaliate whenever possible and try to place it inside the strongest part of the group's burst window.

STEEL RECOVERY

Restores stamina whenever an encounter or daily is used, helping maintain the shield throughout sustained fights.

ANVIL OF CHALLENGE

Useful threat feature when a boss must immediately be reclaimed or during tank-swap mechanics.

MULTI TARGET

Switch to the multi-target loadout for trash packs and encounters containing several dangerous enemies. The objective changes from concentrating threat on one boss to establishing control over the entire group quickly.

CLEAVE

Useful AoE at-will and the preferred option for the first boss of Master Jotunskar.

ENFORCED THREAT

Strong AoE threat tool and particularly useful for immediately reclaiming grouped enemies.

GREATER ENDURANCE

Useful multi-target class feature that improves movement while stamina is high.

STEEL RECOVERY

Remains valuable because encounter and daily use continually feeds stamina back into the shield.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise defensive boons first, then invest remaining points into utility and offensive bonuses that improve threat generation.

  • Maximum Hit Points
  • Defense
  • Awareness
  • Critical Avoidance
  • Deflect
  • Deflect Severity
  • Stamina regeneration
  • Recharge and Action Point gain where useful

The exact distribution can move around as gear changes. If a defensive percentage is already capped in combat, move those points somewhere that actually does something.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

SINGLE-TARGET THREAT FLOW

  1. ESTABLISH POSITION Get the boss where it belongs before the DPS begin unloading.
  2. TIDE OF IRON Apply the increased threat generation before your major threat encounters.
  3. SHIELD THROW Hit the first major threat encounter.
  4. ANVIL OF DOOM Follow with another heavy single-target threat hit.
  5. BLOCK THE IMPORTANT SHIT Threat means very little if the boss turns you into floor decoration.
  6. BRAZEN SLASH Use as filler while encounters return.

ARTIFACT CALL

The current threat rotation is tight. If mechanics interrupt the full sequence, prioritise the pieces that generate the largest amount of threat.

  1. MOUNT POWER Fire the support mount first so its activation lands before the party begins its main burst.
  2. ARTIFACT Follow with your assigned group artifact.
  3. TIDE OF IRON → SHIELD THROW Use Tide of Iron to strengthen the threat generated by Shield Throw.
  4. TIDE OF IRON → ANVIL OF DOOM Repeat the threat setup into Anvil.
  5. DAILY Use the daily inside the party's burst window.
  6. DRAGONFIRE Add the belt-item damage during the call.
  7. RETALIATE Use it when available, preferably around the middle of the call when party debuffs are strongest.
  8. SECOND SHIELD THROW Fit another Shield Throw into the call where cooldowns allow.
IMPORTANT: Don't hit Tide of Iron immediately after your daily. Wait for Tactician's Precision to activate first or you can accidentally cancel the daily/support activation.

MJS FIRST BOSS

Use Cleave instead of Brazen Slash. If a tank-buster removes you from the fight and you return to find the Remorhaz or Basilisks chasing somebody else, immediately use Enforced Threat or Anvil of Doom with the threat feat to reclaim them.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

MORE SURVIVABILITY

Swap offensive companion bonuses, combat enchantment and gear effects toward maximum hit points and defensive percentages.

MORE THREAT

Keep Anvil of Challenge and lean harder into Shield Throw, Anvil of Doom, Retaliate and offensive bonuses.

MORE GROUP SUPPORT

When survivability and threat are comfortable, use support companions, debuff enhancements, mount powers and artifacts to increase the party's damage.

MULTI TARGET

Switch to Cleave, Enforced Threat and the AoE configuration when controlling several enemies matters more than maximum boss threat.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Vanguard isn't just "hold shield and hope." The good ones are attacking, blocking, positioning, watching threat, timing support powers and quietly preventing three DPS from making extremely poor life choices. 😂

The Tide of Iron timing is worth practising. If you cancel it too quickly by blocking, the extra threat effect can fail to activate. Get the rhythm down somewhere harmless before trying to be clever while an angry master boss is attempting to rearrange your face.

And remember: mount before artifact. You're part of the support call. Don't be the bastard whose debuff finally arrives after everyone else's encounters are already on cooldown. 🤣
