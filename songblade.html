HOW IT WORKS

SONGBLADE EXPLAINED

Songblade combines encounter attacks, Performance songs, party support and extremely strong coordinated burst.

Preparation matters more than simply pressing whatever comes off cooldown first. Establish the correct songs, prepare the required buffs and organise encounters around Activate Exploit and the party's artifact call.

Bosses and mob packs use different songs, Dailies and potentially different encounters.

BOSS SETUP

HERO ◆ LORE ◆ BATTLE RESEARCH

Establish Tailwind Mambo before combat and follow it with Ballad of the Hero for the boss setup.

Where possible, enter the fight with five Battle Research stacks prepared for Lore and arrange your encounter cooldowns around Activate Exploit.

Avoid wasting important encounters immediately before the artifact call becomes available.

MOB SETUP

WITCH ◆ INSPIRATION ◆ ENCOUNTER SWAPS

Ballad of the Witch replaces Hero for mob packs. Inspiration replaces Lore as the multi-target Daily.

Duet and Volti Subito are useful mob-focused encounter swaps, with Contre available as another situational option.

Don't force the boss setup through every trash pack. Songblade has different tools because different fights require different bloody tools.

BURST

THE ARTIFACT WINDOW

Songblade damage is heavily concentrated into the coordinated artifact window.

Enter with the important encounters available, allow the group's support debuffs to land, use Lore and unload the prepared encounter sequence while those effects remain active.

With enough cooldown support, additional encounter uses can fit inside the same burst window. Outside the call, rebuild resources and organise cooldowns for the next one.

STATS

DON'T CHASE SOMEBODY ELSE'S SCREENSHOT

Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy should form a balanced offensive spread.

Party buffs and temporary effects continually change the final percentages, so being a few points away from another player's exact setup doesn't automatically mean anything is wrong.

Combat Advantage is only valuable when positioning actually gives you Combat Advantage, so pay attention to the fight rather than just the character sheet.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Songblade has one of those rotations where somebody watching from the outside thinks you're just running around playing a lute.

Then the artifact call happens and half the boss's health suddenly fucks off. 🤣

Preparation is the trick. Tailwind, the correct Ballad, Battle Research, Activate Exploit and your encounter cooldowns all need thinking about before the burst begins.

Missing that coordinated window hurts this build far more than slightly imperfect damage between calls.
