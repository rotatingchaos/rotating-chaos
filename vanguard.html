HOW IT WORKS

VANGUARD EXPLAINED

Vanguard is Fighter's dedicated tank paragon. The build combines mitigation, Guard management, deliberate threat generation and group support.

Blocking is only part of the job. You still need to attack aggressively enough that the DPS can unload during the group's burst window without inheriting the boss.

Position bosses away from the group and keep their movement predictable wherever mechanics allow.

GEAR LOGIC

WHY IS HALF THIS SHIT ANCIENT?

Because Vanguard is a support tank.

Several older pieces remain useful because their effects increase group damage or provide mechanics that newer raw-stat pieces do not replace.

The Bregan armor provides AP support. Drow Priest's Mitts can reduce enemy Awareness. Duergar Masterwork weapons provide party support. Apocalypse remains a useful debuff set.

Rugged Sabatons can provide a substantial temporary Maximum HP and Defense increase.

This is precisely why replacing gear according to item level alone is a terrible bloody idea. 😂

THREAT

TIDE OF IRON

Tide of Iron is woven into the heavy-threat encounter sequence.

Practise the Tide of Iron into Shield Throw and Anvil timing until the sequence is reliable.

Don't become so obsessed with cancelling animations quickly that you cancel the bloody thing you actually wanted to activate.

CLASS FEATURES

STEEL RECOVERY + ANVIL OF CHALLENGE

Steel Recovery restores 5% stamina whenever Vanguard uses an Encounter or Daily.

That makes the normal offensive/threat rotation part of Guard management as well.

Anvil of Challenge modifies Anvil of Doom so the normal cast puts Vanguard at the top of the target's threat list.

A fully charged Anvil behaves differently and does not provide that same threat-list effect.

ARTIFACT CALL

HOLDING AGAINST BURST

Boss mechanics won't always let you perform the entire ideal threat sequence.

When the call becomes messy, prioritise:

Shield Throw ×2 ◆ Anvil of Doom ◆ Daily ◆ Dragonfire ◆ Retaliate

Use Tide of Iron with those heavy-threat encounters wherever the timing permits.

SUPPORT

MOUNT BEFORE ARTIFACT

Vanguard is part of the party's support chain.

Activate the mount combat power before the artifact because mount effects can take time to fully apply.

The DPS want those enemy debuffs active before their strongest attacks land, not halfway through the aftermath.

COMPANIONS

GROUP DAMAGE

The current source build is designed around Spined Devil rather than Portobello Da'Vinci.

Portobello can provide useful Power and Combat Advantage support, but the build's recommendation is Spined Devil for the higher overall group-damage ceiling unless the party specifically depends on Porto's stats.

Companion enhancements such as Armor Break, Dulled Senses and Vulnerability should be coordinated across the party.

SHIELD

STAMINA MANAGEMENT

Don't burn Guard on harmless incoming damage simply because you can.

Save stamina for tank-busters and dangerous mechanics.

Steel Recovery, Gladiator's Guile and the rest of the build's stamina support help keep Guard available when you actually need the bastard.

MASTER JOTUNSKAR

FIRST BOSS

Use Cleave instead of Brazen Slash.

Tank-busters can temporarily remove you from the fight. If aggro changes while you're gone, immediately reclaim the target with Enforced Threat or Anvil of Doom using the appropriate threat setup.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Vanguard isn't "stand there holding block and contemplate life."

You're managing threat, Guard, positioning, encounters, support powers, debuffs and whatever fresh bullshit the boss has decided to throw at you. 😂

And this build is also a beautiful demonstration of why highest IL ≠ automatically best. Some of the old gear in here is practically collecting a pension and still hasn't been fired. 🤣
