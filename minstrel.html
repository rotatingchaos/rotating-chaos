HOW IT WORKS

MINSTREL EXPLAINED

Minstrel is the Bard's healing paragon, combining direct healing, healing-over-time effects, emergency recovery and party support.

The biggest difference is preparation. Instead of waiting for health bars to collapse and reacting afterwards, maintain important songs and keep the correct tools ready before incoming damage arrives.

Performance is the resource used to play songs. Maintain the important long-duration effects without spending so aggressively that nothing remains for an emergency.

IMPORTANT TOOLS

FLOURISH, SERENADE & DELAYED PLAY

Flourish is one of the most important encounters in the healing setup. Use it before important healing songs to strengthen their effect.

Serenade marks a target, normally the tank, and allows Defender's Minuet to reliably heal that marked player.

Delayed Play stores the next song rather than casting it immediately, allowing healing to be prepared for a dangerous mechanic or incoming damage phase.

THE SONGBOOK

CORE SONGS

Rejuvenating Carol provides the core long-duration healing-over-time effect. Sheltering Etude provides important emergency healing when party members fall into danger.

Defender's Minuet provides fast targeted healing and works particularly well with Serenade on the tank. Warding Carol provides a large-area cleanse when the encounter requires one.

Blaze Flamenco contributes group damage support when healing requirements allow. Aurora Fantasia is more situational and can be useful where its enemy interactions help generate Action Points.

DAILY

CURTAIN CALL

Curtain Call converts active songs into additional effects. Depending on the songs currently active, this can provide emergency healing, shielding, damage reduction or Action Point effects.

The trade-off is that Curtain Call consumes those active songs. Use it deliberately and re-establish anything important afterwards.

OTHER OPTIONS

CHANGE WITH THE FIGHT

If healing is easy, move more of the setup toward group damage support. There is no benefit in throwing unnecessary healing at a party that is already safe.

If the tank is taking sustained punishment, Serenade and Defender's Minuet become much more important. In cleanse-heavy encounters, keep Warding Carol readily available.

During progression, prioritise getting the important healing percentages into a healthy range before worrying about perfect optimisation.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Minstrel looks bloody complicated at first because apparently ordinary healing wasn't theatrical enough.

Once the important songs become muscle memory, it starts making considerably more sense. The real trick is having Rejuvenating Carol and Sheltering Etude doing their jobs before the damage arrives.

That leaves Defender's Minuet and the rest of the kit available for the bit where somebody ignores a mechanic, stands in something glowing red and suddenly develops a very urgent interest in your healing abilities. 😂
