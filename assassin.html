HOW IT WORKS

ASSASSIN EXPLAINED

Assassin is the classic close-range Rogue DPS paragon. Position for Combat Advantage, establish Duelist's Flurry bleeds, use Stealth deliberately and place the strongest encounter attacks inside useful damage windows.

Single-target damage revolves around maintaining the bleed while cycling Wicked Reminder, Assassinate and Lashing Blade. Against groups, the loadout shifts toward Path of the Blades, Blade Flurry and Smoke Bomb.

ALTERNATIVES

SWAPS & TRADE-OFFS

ASSASSINATE VS PATH OF THE BLADES

Assassinate belongs in the single-target setup where its heavy hit matters. Path of the Blades is more useful against groups where its damage can spread across multiple enemies.

BLADE FLURRY

Blade Flurry is a strong AoE option and can be combined intelligently with Stealth in the multi-target setup.

DAZING STRIKE

Dazing Strike is a situational alternative when extra AoE damage or control suits the encounter better.
