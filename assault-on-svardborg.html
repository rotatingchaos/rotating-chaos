ASSAULT ON SVARDBORG

Rotating Chaos Neverwinter Trial Guide

Overview Phase One Phase Two Manticores Final Phase Role Jobs Common Wipes Chaos Notes

THE TRIAL

OVERVIEW

Assault on Svardborg is built around shared-damage mechanics, ice control, positioning and add phases.

The most important mechanics throughout the encounter are breaking ice immediately, stacking correctly for Hypothermia, moving targeted red circles away from the group and reacting quickly to dangerous boss callouts.

OPENING PHASE

STAY TOGETHER

The group should stay relatively close together during the opening phase.

ICE

Players can become trapped in ice.

Break the ice immediately.

Failing to free a trapped player quickly can result in death.

HYPOTHERMIA

Hypothermia is a shared-damage mechanic.

The targeted player should bring the mechanic into the centre of the group.

Everyone stacks together to split the incoming damage.

Taking Hypothermia away from the group can kill the targeted player.

RED CIRCLES

Separate red circles can target players.

These should be moved away from the group.

If dropped on the party, they can knock multiple players down.

GET BEHIND SOMETHING

When the boss calls for the group to get behind something, move quickly to cover at the edge of the arena.

Use either side of the available cover to avoid the incoming attack.

SECOND PHASE

POSITIONING & DAMAGE

Continue moving targeted red circles away from the group.

Maintain clean positioning and focus damage on the active target.

Ice and other core mechanics continue to appear during later phases, so do not stop watching for them just because the fight changes.

ADD PHASE

MANTICORES

Two manticores become active during this section.

FOCUS ONE

Focus one manticore at a time where possible.

This reduces pressure on the group and avoids fighting both at full strength.

ICE STILL MATTERS

Ice mechanics continue during the manticore phase.

Free any trapped player immediately.

A player who dies inside the ice cannot be recovered normally, making this one of the most dangerous mechanics in the phase.

SPINE ATTACKS

Avoid the manticores' spine attacks.

TAIL WHIP

Avoid the tail whip.

It can apply a poison effect that deals heavy damage over time.

RED AOES

Red areas continue appearing around the arena.

Move out of them and keep dangerous telegraphs away from the group.

HYPOTHERMIA RETURNS

Hypothermia can return during this phase.

Bring it into the group and stack to share the damage.

FINAL PHASE

FINAL BURN

Core mechanics continue into the final phase.

Keep breaking ice immediately.

Move red AoEs away from the party.

Stack together for Hypothermia.

Stay close enough to the healer and maintain awareness of tank positioning.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANKS

Keep enemies positioned clearly so the group can react to ice, red AoEs and shared-damage mechanics without unnecessary chaos.

During manticore phases, control positioning and avoid pointing dangerous attacks through the group.

HEALERS

Stay central enough that players can remain within healing range while handling mechanics.

Be ready for heavy damage from Hypothermia, poison effects and mistakes involving ice.

DPS

Break trapped players out of ice immediately.

Focus one manticore at a time.

Move targeted red circles away from the group and stack correctly for Hypothermia.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Failing to break players out of ice quickly enough.

Taking Hypothermia away from the group instead of sharing the damage.

Dropping red circles on the party and knocking multiple players down.

Failing to reach cover when the boss calls for the group to get behind something.

Splitting damage between both manticores instead of focusing one.

Standing in tail attacks and taking heavy poison damage over time.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Svardborg is mostly about doing the simple things quickly.

Ice appears? Break it.

Hypothermia appears? Group cuddle.

Red circle appears? Take your personal problem away from everybody else.

Giant attack incoming? Get behind the bloody cover.
