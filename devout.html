HOW IT WORKS

DEVOUT EXPLAINED

Devout combines healing-over-time, emergency healing, Divinity management and group support with situational tools for difficult mechanics.

Healing Word forms the backbone of normal group healing. Keep it active, then use the rest of the toolkit to support damage, restore Divinity or react to incoming damage.

SWAPS

HEALING & SUPPORT OPTIONS

EXALTATION VS BASTION

Use Exaltation when healing is comfortable and you can support a strong DPS. Switch to Bastion when the group needs additional instant healing.

DIVINE GLOW VS BASTION

If the group must remain widely spread and Exaltation remains useful, Divine Glow can instead be replaced by Bastion.

ANOINTED ARMY VS HALLOWED GROUND

Anointed Army fits coordinated damage calls. Hallowed Ground becomes useful when its broader support and cooldown interaction are more valuable.

ANGEL OF LIFE

Use Angel of Life when raw healing matters more than squeezing additional damage support from the Devout.

MECHANICS

BRING THE RIGHT TOOL

Cleansing Light, Astral Shield, Geas and Sun Burst exist for specific jobs. Swap powers to solve the encounter rather than trying to force one loadout through everything.

Expanded Faith can be useful, but class features are not completely fixed either. Some encounters favour mobility while channeling Divinity or additional personal mitigation.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Devout becomes much more useful when you stop thinking only about repairing health bars and start controlling how safely and efficiently the whole group can play.

If nobody is dying, support damage, prepare for the next mechanic and recover Divinity.

If everybody IS dying... perhaps stop buffing Dave for five seconds and heal the bastards. 😂
