MINSTREL

Healing ◆ Buffs ◆ Musical Chaos

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Minstrel is the Bard's healing paragon, combining direct healing, healing-over-time effects and emergency recovery with some of the strongest group-support tools in the Bard kit.

Unlike a healer that simply waits for health bars to drop and then reacts, Minstrel rewards preparation. You maintain songs, manage Performance, strengthen important heals with Flourish and keep the correct tools ready before the group actually needs them.

Once the healing requirement is under control, the Bard can also contribute valuable party buffs rather than spending the entire fight desperately throwing heals at people who don't currently need them.

THE BASIC RULE: Keep your important songs running, keep enough Performance available for emergencies and don't blow the entire orchestra because somebody lost 4% health. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

The healing setup focuses first on improving the strength of every heal, then builds the critical stats that increase the size and frequency of critical healing.

1 ◆ POWER Primary healing stat and one of the first percentages to push.
2 ◆ OUTGOING HEALING Directly improves your healing output and should be pushed alongside Power.
3 ◆ CRITICAL SEVERITY Increases the strength of your critical heals and becomes a major part of the finished healing setup.
4 ◆ CRITICAL STRIKE Increases how often those critical heals occur. Build this after the core healing stats are in place.
5 ◆ RECHARGE / SUPPORT STATS Useful once the main healing percentages are healthy, particularly where they improve access to your encounters and support tools.
DON'T CHASE RATING FOR THE SAKE OF IT: What matters is the finished percentage. Use gear, enchantments and companion bonuses together rather than forcing every slot to increase the same stat.

LOADOUT

GEAR

The older Minstrel setup gives us the healing and support principles, but individual equipment pieces have moved on. For Module 33, build around current healer gear rather than copying obsolete armour simply because it appeared in an older loadout.

HEAD

Prioritise a current healer or group-support bonus that contributes to Power, healing or useful party utility.

ARMOUR

Look for reliable healing or support bonuses rather than effects dependent on unrealistic uptime.

ARMS

Use this slot to strengthen the healing percentages you're still missing or provide additional team support.

FEET

Healing, Power, movement and useful support effects are all valid depending on the finished stat balance.

WEAPONS

Use a current healer weapon set. In coordinated groups, group-support weapon bonuses may outperform a purely personal healing option once your healing is already comfortable.

RINGS & CLOTHING

Use these slots to complete Power, Outgoing Healing and critical percentages without unnecessarily overcapping one stat.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments are part of the overall stat puzzle rather than a fixed shopping list. Choose them after your main equipment and companion bonuses so they fill whatever percentages remain short.

  • Power where additional Power is required
  • Outgoing Healing where available and useful
  • Critical Severity to strengthen critical heals
  • Critical Strike once the earlier priorities are healthy
  • Recharge or defensive utility where the core healing stats are complete

Reinforcement kits should follow exactly the same logic: use them to finish the build rather than automatically stacking one stat into every available slot.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Healers are in an excellent position to carry group-support artifacts. Coordinate the active artifact with the party so the group's major debuffs land together during the DPS burst window.

ACTIVE ARTIFACT

Prefer a useful current group debuff or support artifact unless a specific encounter requires a defensive alternative.

SECONDARY ARTIFACTS

Use secondary artifacts to complete Power, Outgoing Healing, Critical Severity and Critical Strike.

ARTIFACT CALLS: You're part of the burst window too. Get the support artifact down with everyone else's instead of discovering it still sitting unused on your bar after the boss has wandered off. 😂

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Companion choices should either improve your healing percentages or contribute useful support to the whole party.

SUMMONED COMPANION

A current group-support companion is usually the strongest choice when your personal healing output is already sufficient.

PLAYER BONUSES

Prioritise bonuses supplying Power, Outgoing Healing, Critical Severity or Critical Strike according to what the finished build needs.

COMPANION EQUIPMENT

Use current companion gear to finish the same healing priorities. Don't overcap a rating simply because a higher item-level piece dropped.

ENHANCEMENT

Group debuff enhancements are excellent when healing is secure. Coordinate with the party so several players aren't wasting slots on the same non-stacking effect.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mounts give the Minstrel another place to balance healing stats, survivability and party support.

COMBAT POWER

Use a current group-support or debuff combat power where possible and coordinate it with artifact calls.

EQUIP POWER

Choose whichever equip bonus best completes the healing or support setup.

INSIGNIAS

Use insignias to finish missing percentages rather than copying a fixed spread from somebody else's character.

COLLARS

Recharge, movement and other useful support bonuses can all have value once the core healing setup is complete.

COMBAT

POWERS, SONGS & FEATS

CORE MECHANIC ◆ PERFORMANCE

Enter Performance mode to play songs using their note sequences. Performance is a resource, so the Minstrel is constantly balancing maintaining long-duration effects against keeping enough Performance available for immediate healing.

AT-WILLS

ARPEGGIO

Main general-purpose at-will for the healing setup and useful filler between the parts of the rotation that actually matter.

ENCOUNTERS

FLOURISH

One of the most important parts of the healing setup. Use Flourish before important healing songs to significantly strengthen their effect.

DELAYED PLAY

Stores the next song rather than immediately casting it, giving you another way to prepare healing for when the group actually needs it.

SERENADE

Marks a target — normally the tank — and allows Defender's Minuet to reliably heal that marked player.

CORE SONGS

BLAZE FLAMENCO

Group-support song used to increase party damage. It can also contribute useful movement support while travelling between encounters.

REJUVENATING CAROL

Core healing-over-time song. Keep this maintained rather than waiting for the entire party to lose health before remembering you own it.

DEFENDER'S MINUET

Fast targeted healing song. Without Serenade it heals the player with the lowest health; with Serenade active it reliably targets the marked player.

WARDING CAROL

Cleanse tool used to remove negative effects from the group over a large area.

AURORA FANTASIA

Situational song rather than part of the normal healing loop. Useful when its interaction with groups of enemies can help generate Action Points.

SHELTERING ETUDE

One of the Minstrel's bread-and-butter healing tools. Maintain its effect so injured party members can receive the large heal when their health falls into danger.

DAILY ◆ CURTAIN CALL

Curtain Call converts active songs into additional effects. This can produce powerful emergency healing, shielding, damage reduction or Action Point effects depending on which songs are active.

CURTAIN CALL WARNING: It consumes the songs you're currently running. Don't immediately delete a freshly cast long-duration song and then wonder where all your Performance went. 😂

FEATS

Build the feat setup around reliable Performance management, stronger healing and group support. Desperate Finale is particularly useful as an emergency Performance recovery tool, while Storyteller provides valuable role-based party support.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise boons that improve the same core healing percentages and then add utility once those are healthy.

  • Power
  • Outgoing Healing
  • Critical Severity
  • Critical Strike
  • Recharge Speed
  • Action Point Gain
  • Useful defensive or movement bonuses afterwards

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

HEALING FLOW

  1. SERENADE THE TANK Mark the tank when reliable single-target healing will be needed.
  2. FLOURISH Use Flourish before setting up your important healing songs.
  3. REJUVENATING CAROL Establish the long-duration heal-over-time and keep it maintained.
  4. SHELTERING ETUDE Keep the emergency healing effect available across the party.
  5. BLAZE FLAMENCO Maintain your group-damage support when Performance and healing requirements allow.
  6. DEFENDER'S MINUET Use for immediate targeted healing, especially on the Serenade target.
  7. WARDING CAROL Use when the encounter applies negative effects that need to be cleansed.
  8. DELAYED PLAY Prepare a song in advance when you know a dangerous mechanic or incoming damage phase is approaching.
  9. CURTAIN CALL Use deliberately when its converted song effects are worth losing the active songs. Re-establish anything important afterwards.
  10. MANAGE PERFORMANCE Don't spend yourself completely dry. A Bard with no Performance during the emergency is basically just somebody standing near a dragon holding an instrument.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

Minstrel should change with the encounter. If healing is easy, move more of the build toward group damage support. If the tank is taking sustained punishment, Serenade and Defender's Minuet become much more important.

HEALING HEAVY

Prioritise Performance efficiency, Flourish-enhanced healing, Rejuvenating Carol and reliable Defender's Minuet access.

SUPPORT HEAVY

When nobody is in danger, use the breathing room to maintain useful party buffs and coordinate group debuffs.

CLEANSE FIGHTS

Keep Warding Carol readily available when removing negative effects is part of the encounter mechanics.

PROGRESSION

Use whatever current healing gear gets the important percentages into a healthy range first. Perfect optimisation can come after the character actually keeps everybody alive.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Minstrel looks bloody complicated when you first see the song system because apparently ordinary healing wasn't theatrical enough. Once the core songs become muscle memory, though, the whole class starts making considerably more sense.

The biggest difference between a struggling Bard and a comfortable one is usually preparation. Rejuvenating Carol and Sheltering Etude should already be doing their jobs before the damage arrives, leaving Defender's Minuet and the rest of the kit available for the stuff that actually goes wrong.

And don't forget Flourish before the important healing songs. There is very little point owning a giant "make healing better" button and then politely leaving the bastard untouched. 😂
