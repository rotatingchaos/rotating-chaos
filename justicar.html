PALADIN ◆ TANK

JUSTICAR

Block ◆ Threat ◆ Holy Brick Wall

Overview Stats Gear Enchantments Artifacts Companions Mounts Powers & Feats Boons Rotation

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Justicar is the Paladin's dedicated tank paragon, built around controlling threat, blocking heavy incoming damage and keeping the boss facing somewhere that isn't directly into the DPS.

This setup is designed as a flexible general tank build. Start with survivability and reliable threat, then swap pieces and powers toward group support when the healer is comfortable and the fight doesn't require maximum defence.

The Paladin's Divinity mechanic gives Justicar access to powerful defensive and threat tools, while Divine Champion turns your own damage into a massive source of threat during important burst windows.

THE JOB: Keep the boss angry at you, keep your shield between your face and whatever nightmare is trying to remove it, and stop the DPS becoming an unexpected tank. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Justicar needs a balanced defensive spread rather than blindly stacking one stat. Maximum Hit Points are particularly valuable because more health also increases the amount of damage your block can absorb.

MAXIMUM HIT POINTS

One of the most valuable tank investments. More health improves raw survivability and gives your block a larger working pool.

DEFENSE

Core mitigation stat. Keep it strong without wasting excessive rating once the percentage is already where it needs to be.

AWARENESS

Major defensive priority for reducing the punishment from enemy Combat Advantage.

CRITICAL AVOIDANCE

Reduces the impact of enemy critical hits and becomes especially useful in harder boss content.

DEFLECT

Adds another layer of mitigation and works alongside Deflect Severity.

DEFLECT SEVERITY

Improves the value of successful deflects and becomes increasingly useful as the rest of the defensive build fills out.

ABILITY SCORES

Constitution is the main investment for the tank build. Secondary points can go into Charisma, Intelligence or Strength depending on what the finished setup needs.

LOADOUT

GEAR

Build the core loadout around survivability first, then keep a second set of useful support pieces available. A Justicar that comfortably survives the encounter can afford to sacrifice some personal defence to increase the group's damage.

HEAD

Use a defensive or utility option appropriate to the encounter. For AoE support, grouping effects can also be extremely useful.

ARMOUR

Prioritise strong tank bonuses, maximum health and defensive value. Support armour can be swapped in when survivability allows.

ARMS

Use defensive arms for progression and difficult fights, with debuff or group-support alternatives available for organised runs.

FEET

Choose reliable defensive bonuses rather than effects that only work under unrealistic conditions.

WEAPONS

Use a strong current tank weapon set. Masterwork becomes a useful group-support option when the party is coordinating those bonuses.

RINGS

Use rings to complete defensive percentages or swap into group-damage support when the encounter permits it.

GEAR MODIFICATIONS

  • 4 × Maximum Hit Points
  • 4 × Awareness
  • Swap excess Awareness into Stamina Regeneration when capped
BUILD FOR THE FIGHT: A tank doesn't need to wear every defensive piece they own just because it says "tank" on the character sheet. Survive comfortably first — then start helping the group kill the bastard faster.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Use enchantments to finish the defensive percentages your gear leaves short and adjust them whenever major equipment changes alter the balance.

COMBAT ENCHANTMENT

Flash Freeze and Fortified Nature are both viable defensive options for the general tank setup.

OVERLOADS

Enemy-specific damage-resistance overloads are extremely useful. Change them according to the content rather than leaving the same pair equipped forever.

UTILITY

Use remaining enchantment slots to correct Defense, Awareness, Critical Avoidance and the rest of the defensive spread.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

The current setup uses a mixture of tank-friendly stat artifacts and useful group options.

MYTHALLAR FRAGMENT

Useful secondary artifact within the current tank setup.

SCINTILLATING SYMBOL OF WATER

Another current secondary option contributing useful build stats.

HEART OF THE VOLCANO

Current artifact option used within the complete tank loadout.

TIAMAT'S ARCANE GLOBE

Can occupy the remaining artifact slot, with Sealing Parchment serving as another option.

For the active artifact, coordinate with the party and use whichever group debuff or support artifact the run needs. Let a DPS handle Demogorgon's Reach where appropriate.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

A summoned companion is normally best used to increase the group's damage rather than adding a tiny amount of personal tankiness.

GROUP SUPPORT

Drizzt, Devil, Porto, Tutor and Flapjack are examples of summoned companions that can contribute useful team support.

SURVIVABILITY OPTION

An augment such as Cat or Winter Fox can provide extra personal HP, Deflect or Forte if active companions behave badly in the content you're running.

HEALER OPTION

Linu, Dedicated Squire or Angel of Protection can provide healing, although their impact is generally less valuable than stronger support options.

ENHANCEMENT

Use a group-support enhancement such as Armor Break, Dulled Senses, Vulnerability or Slowed. Redemption is the stronger personal self-healing option when required.

COMPANION BONUSES

  • Tamed Velociraptor
  • Green Slime
  • Lich
  • Lysaera
  • Myconid

Duergar Guard, Captain Elaina Sartell and Golden Deep Crow are useful alternatives when additional Critical Avoidance, threat, healing or Awareness is required.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mount choices should reinforce survivability, stamina management and group support rather than being treated as a fixed list that never changes.

INSIGNIA BONUSES

  • Ally's Resilience
  • Tactician's Precision
  • Artificer's Enticement
  • Cavalry's Haste
  • Knight's Condemnation
  • Magistrate's Patience
  • Protector's Covenant
  • Shepherd's Devotion
  • Survivor's Blessing

INSIGNIAS

The defensive setup uses Fortitude insignias heavily for Maximum Hit Points. If this pushes Defense too far beyond what you need, rebalance the rest of the build rather than wasting more rating there.

MOUNT COLLARS

  • Stamina Gain
  • Movement Speed or Incoming Healing
  • Critical Severity or Recharge Speed
  • Encounter Power Damage
  • Rough Astral Diamonds for the utility slot

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

AT-WILLS

SHIELDING STRIKE

Useful filler that helps restore stamina while maintaining your normal attack sequence.

OATH STRIKE

Switch to this when you need stronger threat, particularly for AoE situations where you need enemies glued to you.

RADIANT SLAM

Useful mobility option for quickly closing the distance to a target.

ENCOUNTERS

ABSOLUTION

Defensive option for the standard setup when extra threat tools are not required.

VOW OF ENMITY

Emergency threat control. Save it for orange threat, recovering after death or tank swaps rather than wasting it unnecessarily.

SACRED WEAPON

Swap this in when additional threat through your at-will attacks is required.

RELENTLESS AVENGER

Fast gap closer that also provides useful burst threat.

SMITE

Useful with Divine Challenger when you need repeated single-target threat control.

BURNING LIGHT

Useful swap when AoE control and a stun are more important than the standard encounter.

CLASS FEATURES

AURA OF VALOR

Strong threat option. Use it when you need it — losing aggro is considerably worse for the group than sacrificing a support aura.

AURA OF WRATH

Group-support alternative when your threat is already secure.

AURA OF PROTECTION

Defensive team-support alternative for encounters where additional threat isn't required.

DIVINE CHALLENGER

Useful with Smite when reliable single-target threat control is required.

DAILIES

DIVINE JUDGEMENT

Major burst option used during the artifact call to generate effective damage and therefore additional threat.

SHIELD OF FAITH

Defensive and group-support option that can be combined with Sheltering Light when extra personal survivability isn't needed.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

After filling the essential defensive choices, prioritise boons that improve stamina management, cooldowns and the secondary defensive layers.

  • Stamina Regeneration
  • Forte
  • Action Point Gain
  • Recharge Speed
  • Deflect
  • Deflect Severity

Lingering Medicine is worth obtaining early if you want to use rejuvenation potions instead of relying entirely on Stones of Health.

GUILD BOON: Revive Sickness Reduction is particularly valuable for tanks because repeated deaths reduce Maximum Hit Points — exactly the stat you really don't want disappearing during a difficult run.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

BOSS ROTATION

  1. TAKE POSITION Establish the boss position and turn it away from the party before everyone starts unloading.
  2. DIVINE CHAMPION Activate your Tab mechanic to deal damage over time and massively increase the threat generated by your damage.
  3. BUILD & MAINTAIN THREAT Use your at-will as filler. Switch to Oath Strike when stronger threat is required and use Shielding Strike when stamina recovery is useful.
  4. ARTIFACT CALL Activate Divine Champion and prepare to deal as much effective damage as possible during the party's burst window.
  5. SACRED WEAPON Use Sacred Weapon when running the threat-focused encounter setup.
  6. BINDING OATH Continue the burst sequence while the group debuffs are active.
  7. DIVINE JUDGEMENT Roughly five seconds into the artifact call, use Divine Judgement for the major damage and threat burst.
  8. KEEP VOW IN RESERVE Save Vow of Enmity for orange threat, recovering after death or a required boss swap.
  9. BLOCK THE IMPORTANT SHIT Don't empty your stamina bar blocking harmless chip damage and then discover the boss has saved a small moon specifically for your forehead.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & GROUP SUPPORT

MORE THREAT

Switch to Oath Strike, Sacred Weapon, Relentless Avenger or the Smite + Divine Challenger combination depending on whether the problem is AoE threat, mobility or keeping one target permanently at the top of your threat list.

GROUP DAMAGE SUPPORT

Once survivability and threat are secure, Aura of Wrath, Masterwork weapons, support artifact sets, Feral Raptor bonuses and group-support companions can all move the build toward increasing party damage.

AOE CONTROL

Burning Light provides an AoE stun, while specialised head gear can also be used to help pull packs together for the DPS.

HEALER SUPPORT

Sheltering Light with Shield of Faith is useful when the healer needs support and your own survivability is already comfortable.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Tanking isn't just "equip defensive gear and hold shield until Tuesday." Threat is part of survivability because the boss can't murder the DPS if it remains obsessed with you.

Start defensive when learning a fight. Once you know which attacks actually hurt, when you need stamina and how much punishment the healer can comfortably handle, start swapping toward group support.

And if somebody complains about you using a threat option while simultaneously trying to rip aggro off you with nuclear-level DPS, they can either calm their rotation down or enjoy their exciting new career as temporary tank. 😂
