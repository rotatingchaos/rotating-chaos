HOW IT WORKS

BLADEMASTER EXPLAINED

Blademaster is the damage-focused Barbarian paragon, built around generating Rage, entering Battlerage and unloading encounters while Rage is active.

The important part is deciding when to spend Rage and when to save it. Against packs you can use Battlerage aggressively. Against bosses, you want Rage and encounters ready for coordinated artifact calls.

ALTERNATIVES

SWAPS & TRADE-OFFS

RELENTLESS BATTLERAGE VS ESCALATING RAGE

Relentless Battlerage is easier and more consistent for sustained boss damage. Escalating Rage offers greater burst potential but requires much better Rage management.

OVERPENETRATION VS BRUTAL CRITICAL

Overpenetration belongs in the harder maximum-burst setup. Brutal Critical is the more forgiving alternative when easier Rage generation is useful.

RAGING STRIKES VS IMPATIENCE

Raging Strikes pushes damage. Impatience adds cooldown support and can make the rotation noticeably smoother.

MULTI-TARGET OPTIONS

Mighty Leap and Roar are viable AoE alternatives. Barbed Strikes can also be useful when the offensive stats it provides are not already capped.

GEAR PROGRESSION

BUILD TOWARD IT

This build has a current Module 33.5 progression path. You do not need every finished endgame piece immediately.

Build toward the final setup as you unlock content. Wearing terrible equipment purely because it appears on somebody's finished BIS sheet rather defeats the point.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

If you're learning Blademaster, start with the sustained version and get comfortable managing Rage before making life unnecessarily difficult with the maximum-burst setup.

Current guild variations, dungeon-specific swaps and screenshots will be added as we collect them.

And if you've entered the artifact call with no Rage, we are all judging you quietly. 😂
