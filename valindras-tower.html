VALINDRA'S TOWER

Rotating Chaos Neverwinter Dungeon Guide

THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Valindra's Tower is largely about controlling add-heavy rooms before the final encounter against Valindra Shadowmantle.

The main mechanics to watch for are summoning portals, dangerous add buildup, Shatterspine's sustained add pressure, Valindra's ghost phases, Phantom Claw and Soul Caskets.

DUNGEON MECHANIC

SUMMONING PORTALS

Several rooms contain summoning portals that continuously produce additional enemies.

Prioritise the portals before dealing with the surrounding adds.

PORTAL WAVES

Destroying one portal can cause another to appear.

Continue focusing the active portal until the sequence is finished.

LEGION DEVILS

Legion Devils can build up quickly if the portals are left active.

Large numbers of them can overwhelm the group, so do not allow the portal rooms to drag on unnecessarily.

DREAD HOUNDS

Dread Hounds leap onto players and can create messy positioning.

Group them together and use AoE damage where possible.

IRON GOLEMS

Iron Golems can hit hard.

Keep them controlled and avoid unnecessary frontal damage.

FIRST BOSS

SHATTERSPINE

Shatterspine is surrounded by heavy add pressure.

STAY OUT OF FRONT

Shatterspine can hit hard directly in front of him.

DPS should avoid standing in front where possible.

DREAD PROTECTORS

Dread Protectors spawn during the fight.

The longer the encounter lasts, the more adds the group may need to handle.

ADD CONTROL

Group the adds tightly and use AoE damage.

A group relying heavily on single-target damage can struggle if the adds begin accumulating.

BURN THE BOSS

Control the adds while continuing to damage Shatterspine.

Ending the fight quickly reduces the total number of adds the group has to manage.

FINAL BOSS

VALINDRA SHADOWMANTLE

Valindra becomes increasingly dangerous the longer the fight continues.

LIFT & THROW

Valindra can lift a player into the air, choke them and throw them in a random direction.

Be ready to recover positioning after being thrown.

ARENA-WIDE GHOST PHASE

At certain health thresholds, Valindra returns to the centre and releases energy across the arena.

Move toward the outer edge.

GHOSTS

Ghosts move around the arena and chase players.

The group should all move in the same direction, either clockwise or counterclockwise.

Keeping everyone moving together helps control the ghosts and prevents players from running into each other.

Contact with the ghosts causes heavy damage and can drain Action Points.

RED ENERGY STRIPS

Red strips of damage appear across the arena during the ghost phase.

Avoid them while continuing to move with the group.

ENERGY HANDS

Large energy hands sweep across the arena.

Dodge out of their path.

Being hit can cause heavy damage or knock a player down.

PHANTOM CLAW

A Phantom Claw can grab and restrain a player.

The trapped player takes continuous damage and cannot act.

The rest of the group must attack the Phantom Claw to free them.

SOUL CASKETS

Soul Caskets can appear around the edge of the arena.

If opened, they release powerful adds.

Players can interact with the caskets to remove them before they open.

SECOND GHOST PHASE

The second arena-wide ghost phase lasts longer than the first.

Continue moving together around the arena and avoid ghosts, red strips and energy hands.

FINAL CASKET PHASE

Near the end of the fight, large numbers of Soul Caskets can appear.

If Valindra is already low enough, focusing damage on her can be more effective than attempting to clear every casket.

Once Valindra dies, the caskets and their threat are removed.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Group dungeon adds together so the party can use AoE damage efficiently.

During Shatterspine, keep the boss controlled while gathering Dread Protectors and other adds.

During Valindra, maintain clean positioning so the group has room to move during ghost phases.

HEALER

Be ready for burst damage when players are grabbed, thrown or caught by arena mechanics.

During ghost phases, stay with the group's movement direction so everyone remains within healing range.

DPS

Prioritise summoning portals in the dungeon rooms.

Use AoE damage to control Shatterspine's adds.

During Valindra, immediately attack Phantom Claws to free trapped players.

During ghost phases, move with the group rather than improvising your own scenic route around the arena.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Ignoring summoning portals and allowing adds to stack up.

Standing directly in front of Shatterspine.

Allowing Shatterspine's adds to accumulate faster than they are killed.

Players running in different directions during Valindra's ghost phases.

Running into ghosts and taking heavy damage or losing Action Points.

Failing to destroy Phantom Claws quickly enough.

Standing in red energy strips or being hit by sweeping energy hands.

Letting too many Soul Caskets open at once.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Valindra's Tower is mostly an exercise in not allowing the dungeon to manufacture twelve thousand additional problems.

Portal room: kill the things making more things.

Shatterspine: control the adds before the room becomes a convention.

Valindra: everyone runs the same bloody direction, free anyone grabbed by the giant hand, and try not to personally introduce yourself to every ghost.
