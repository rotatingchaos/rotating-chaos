DREADNOUGHT

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Dreadnought is the Fighter's damage paragon, combining heavy physical hits with one of the more unusual DPS mechanics in Neverwinter: Vengeance.

Unlike most damage dealers, the Fighter still has access to its shield. Blocking through Seethe can convert Stamina into Vengeance, giving the Dreadnought another resource to actively manage rather than simply standing behind the boss and hitting whatever comes off cooldown.

Single-target Dreadnought is built around preparing Vengeance, maintaining its damage effects and lining up powerful encounters and Dailies inside the group's main artifact window.

Multi-target changes the toolkit considerably, replacing several boss powers with faster area attacks designed to clear packs.

THE FIGHTER DIFFERENCE: That shield isn't there because the Dreadnought secretly wants to be a tank. Used correctly, blocking and Seethe are part of the DPS toolkit because they help you manage Vengeance.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Dreadnought deals physical damage, so build around the standard offensive DPS stats and balance them according to the bonuses your complete toon receives in combat.

POWER

Core offensive stat and one of the primary targets for the finished build.

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

Extremely valuable when properly positioned around enemies. Stay on the correct side of bosses so you're actually receiving the benefit.

CRITICAL STRIKE

Important both for damage and for Dreadnought mechanics that interact with critical attacks.

CRITICAL SEVERITY

Build alongside Critical Strike rather than maxing one while neglecting the other.

ACCURACY

Still useful, but generally the offensive stat you finish after the stronger priorities are in place.

ABILITY SCORES

Strength is particularly valuable because Dreadnought deals physical damage. Dexterity is also useful for supporting critical damage.

CHECK THE FINAL PERCENTAGES: Gear, enchantments, insignias, companions, boons, consumables and party buffs all contribute to the finished build. Don't cap something twice just because two different pieces of gear looked shiny.

LOADOUT

GEAR

Dreadnought gear should improve physical damage while completing the offensive stat spread. Damage bonuses are often more valuable than simply equipping the highest item-level piece available.

HEAD

Look for useful offensive percentages or damage bonuses that are reliable in the content you're running.

ARMOUR

Prioritise a strong damage effect while keeping enough useful ratings to maintain the overall stat balance.

ARMS

Current damage-focused arms are preferred. Keep useful scaled-content alternatives available rather than assuming one pair must be worn everywhere.

FEET

Damage-bonus boots are strong when their conditions are practical. Don't sacrifice survivability for a theoretical damage increase if you're still learning the content.

WEAPONS

Use a strong current artifact or damage weapon set appropriate to the content you're running.

RINGS

Use rings to finish missing offensive stats or add reliable damage bonuses without pushing ratings unnecessarily over cap.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Use enchantments to complete the offensive stat spread left by your equipment rather than treating one exact enchantment layout as mandatory for every Dreadnought.

OFFENSIVE RATINGS

Fill missing Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity or Accuracy according to your actual build.

REINFORCEMENTS

Armour kits and jewels are excellent for correcting smaller stat gaps without replacing otherwise strong equipment.

STRIKE & GUARD

Take advantage of the modern Strike and Guard enchantment slots as part of the complete build rather than judging the old enchantment system in isolation.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Use a strong current damage or debuff artifact as your active artifact and select secondary artifacts according to stats and useful set bonuses.

The important part for Dreadnought is timing. Your artifact belongs inside a prepared burst sequence with Vengeance available, encounters ready and your mount and Daily lined up.

DON'T WING THE CALL: If the group is about to artifact, prepare first. Dreadnought gets far more from a coordinated burst than from pressing everything in whichever order your thumb discovers it.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Companion choices should improve your personal damage while also contributing to the party where possible.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Use a strong current single-target companion for bosses and consider a useful AoE or grouping companion for packs.

PLAYER BONUSES

Choose damage bonuses and offensive stats according to what the finished Fighter still needs.

COMPANION GEAR

Use companion equipment to correct ratings and help complete the offensive stat spread.

GROUP DEBUFFS

In organised dungeon groups, use a useful debuff companion enhancement and coordinate it with the other players.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Use a hard-hitting single-target combat power for bosses and swap to an effective multi-target mount power for packs when appropriate.

SINGLE TARGET

Use a strong current single-target mount combat power and line it up with the artifact burst window.

MULTI TARGET

A wide-area mount combat power is more useful when several enemies can be hit together.

USEFUL INSIGNIA BONUSES

  • TACTICIAN'S PRECISION — useful for reducing encounter cooldowns after using a Daily.
  • ARTIFICER'S PERSUASION — provides useful cooldown support around artifact use.
  • GLADIATOR'S GUILE — movement and stamina utility are particularly useful on a melee class.
  • WARLORD'S MOTIVATION — improves the damage of your summoned companion.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

SINGLE-TARGET POWERS

HEAVY SLASH

Primary single-target at-will and an important part of the Dreadnought's damage setup.

COMMANDER'S STRIKE

Strong single-target encounter used as part of the main boss burst sequence.

BULL CHARGE

Strong boss encounter. Momentum improves its damage and removes the unwanted knock effect.

ANVIL OF DOOM

Heavy single-target encounter and one of the core hits in the boss rotation.

MOW DOWN

Main single-target Daily and an important part of the artifact burst window.

MULTI-TARGET POWERS

REAVE

Fast ranged-line at-will useful for hitting multiple enemies.

ONSLAUGHT

Multi-target encounter used in place of the single-target Commander’s Strike setup.

SHIELD SLAM

Useful AoE encounter for hitting enemies grouped in front of the Fighter.

TREMOR

Multi-target alternative to Anvil of Doom for clearing packs.

EARTHSHAKER

AoE Daily option when several targets can be hit together.

CLASS FEATURES

ENDURING VENGEANCE

Rewards keeping the Vengeance Gauge high, making resource management part of sustained damage.

MOMENTUM

Strong single-target choice that improves Bull Charge and also provides useful movement.

CORE FEATS

WEIGHT OF VENGEANCE

Supports the Vengeance-focused Dreadnought playstyle.

PREPARED SLAM

Adds another interaction between your at-will setup and damaging encounters.

CRUSHING BLOWS

Gives attacks a chance to consume Vengeance for an additional damage hit.

LAND WASTER

Useful single-target feat option for boss damage.

ROILING HATRED

Critical attacks can help restore Vengeance while the gauge is sufficiently full.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise offensive boon choices according to the stats the finished Fighter actually needs.

  • Power
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Combat Advantage
  • Accuracy
  • Forte where useful
  • Recharge Speed
  • Action Point Gain

Movement speed is also useful because Dreadnought is a melee DPS. Damage done while running toward the boss is generally still zero.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

SINGLE TARGET

  1. PREPARE VENGEANCE Make sure the gauge is in a strong position before the main damage window begins.
  2. HEAVY SLASH Begin with your main single-target at-will and establish the effects your setup requires.
  3. ARTIFACT Begin the coordinated burst window.
  4. COMMANDER'S STRIKE Start the encounter sequence.
  5. BULL CHARGE Follow with the Momentum-enhanced heavy hit.
  6. ANVIL OF DOOM Land another major single-target encounter while the artifact bonuses are active.
  7. MOUNT POWER Fit your single-target mount attack into the coordinated damage window.
  8. MOW DOWN Use the single-target Daily while the major buffs and debuffs are still active.
  9. HEAVY SLASH Return to your at-will while encounters recharge and rebuild anything needed for the next sequence.

MULTI TARGET

  1. GROUP THE ENEMIES Let the tank establish the pack so your AoE attacks actually hit as many targets as possible.
  2. SHIELD SLAM Open with area damage against the grouped pack.
  3. TREMOR Continue the AoE encounter sequence.
  4. ONSLAUGHT Hit the pack with another multi-target encounter.
  5. REAVE Use Reave as filler while your encounters recover.
  6. EARTHSHAKER Use your AoE Daily when the pack will survive long enough for it to matter.
REMEMBER THE SHIELD: You're DPS, yes. But you're Fighter DPS. Seethe lets you turn that massive dinner plate strapped to your arm into Vengeance, and Vengeance is damage. Use the bloody thing. 😂

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

SINGLE TARGET VS AOE

Don't force the boss setup into every pack. Dreadnought has specific multi-target alternatives including Reave, Onslaught, Shield Slam, Tremor and Earthshaker.

DAMAGE VS SURVIVAL

The Fighter's shield gives Dreadnought more defensive flexibility than many DPS classes. Use it when a mechanic requires it rather than dying because blocking might interrupt your beautiful parse.

STAT SWAPS

When group buffs push an offensive stat beyond its useful target, move excess rating into another offensive stat or a useful damage bonus.

GEAR SWAPS

Keep alternatives for scaled content, AoE and boss fights. One equipment set doesn't automatically perform best in every situation.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Dreadnought looks straightforward until you realise you're juggling Vengeance, positioning, cooldowns, artifact timing and a shield while standing approximately six inches from whatever giant bastard the dungeon has decided to throw at you.

Learn the single-target rotation first. Once that becomes automatic, start concentrating on preparing Vengeance before calls instead of desperately trying to generate it halfway through one.
