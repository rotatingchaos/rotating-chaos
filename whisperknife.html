WHISPERKNIFE

Stealth ◆ Knives ◆ Questionable Personal Space

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Whisperknife is a fast Rogue DPS path built around Stealth, positional damage, damage-over-time effects and explosive burst during coordinated artifact calls.

Single target revolves around maintaining Disheartening Strike, staying in Combat Advantage and combining Stealth with Shadow Strike, Lashing Blade and Impact Shot. Multi target changes the rhythm completely, throwing you directly into the middle of enemy packs with Blade Flurry, Smoke Bomb and Path of the Blade.

The build can be used throughout the game, although its maximum-DPS configuration is particularly optimised for boss encounters and assumes useful support from the rest of the party.

THE IMPORTANT BIT: Whisperknife isn't "stand somewhere vaguely nearby and throw knives." Positioning, Stealth timing and the ten-second artifact window matter enormously.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Build toward strong total offensive percentages rather than chasing ratings or item level alone. The finished build should account for party buffs and temporary effects that only appear during combat.

1 ◆ DAMAGE & MAGNITUDE Item level contributes to base Damage, but scaled content limits its value. Strong damage bonuses can therefore outperform raw item level.
2 ◆ POWER One of the main offensive percentages and a core part of the finished damage build.
3 ◆ COMBAT ADVANTAGE Extremely important. Whisperknife should flank whenever possible so Combat Advantage remains active.
4 ◆ CRITICAL STRIKE Build toward 90% total percentage alongside Critical Severity.
5 ◆ CRITICAL SEVERITY Build alongside Critical Strike. Critical Severity can continue beyond 90% toward 120%.
6 ◆ ACCURACY Useful, but the lowest offensive priority. Finish it after the stronger damage percentages.
ABILITY SCORES: Strength and Dexterity are the standard offensive choices. If your Critical Severity is already pushing beyond 120%, move some Dexterity into Charisma instead.

LOADOUT

GEAR

The build has a full progression route from easily accessible level 20 gear through advanced and master-content equipment. Don't assume the newest or highest-item-level piece automatically wins, particularly in scaled content.

WEAPONS

Current artifact weapons are the long-term progression target. Voidtouched and Xaryxian weapons remain strong alternatives and can perform extremely well in scaled single-target fights.

HEAD

Prioritise a reliable offensive effect and the stats needed by the finished build rather than item level by itself.

ARMOUR

Use a current damage piece with a consistently useful offensive bonus.

ARMS ◆ SCALED CONTENT

Astral Raider's Gloves are extremely strong whenever the content scales your item level down and can outperform newer, higher-item-level arms.

ARMS ◆ MULTI TARGET

Bindings of the Death Pact from Master Shackles of Divinity are the preferred multi-target arms.

FEET

Celestial boots can provide a large damage bonus but cost 20% of your health. Don't use them while learning difficult content if that trade makes you unnecessarily squishy.

RINGS

Choose rings around useful damage bonuses and whatever offensive percentages still need completing.

ARMOUR KITS & JEWELS

The source setup uses four Critical Severity and four Combat Advantage modifications, adjusted if your own stat spread needs something different.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments should finish the offensive spread rather than blindly copying another Rogue's exact distribution.

COMBAT ENCHANTMENT

Lightning Flash is the main combat enchantment in this build. Swift Synergy can technically be used, but requires rebuilding around it and offers limited benefit for the additional cost.

OVERLOADS

Rage of Flames can be replaced by enemy-specific or zone-specific overloads when the content makes those stronger.

DEFENSIVE SLOTS

Garnets are preferred for Defense, although spare high-rank defensive enchantments are perfectly usable while progressing.

OFFENSIVE SLOTS

Fill whichever of Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity or Accuracy your completed build still needs.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Whisperknife's damage spikes dramatically during the party's coordinated artifact window, making artifact timing one of the most important parts of the build.

NIGHTFLAME CENSER

One of the secondary artifacts used in the capped DPS setup.

DEMON SKULL

Strong secondary artifact option within the endgame build.

BROKEN HALO

Another current secondary option obtained from Shackles of Divinity content.

ARMA-EGG-ON

Additional secondary artifact option used to complete the offensive setup.

PRIMARY ARTIFACT

Use the group-support artifact assigned to you for the party's coordinated call. The important part is stacking complementary buffs and debuffs together.

WHY THE CALL MATTERS: A properly supported ten-second artifact window can produce roughly three to four times your normal damage. Missing it is therefore not a tiny optimisation problem. It's a rather large "where the fuck did my damage go?" problem. 😂

COMPANION ENHANCEMENT

Coordinate enemy-stat-reduction enhancements across the party. They are not intended to stack with identical copies, so don't have everyone bring the same bloody one.

COMPANION GEAR

Current companion gear can be obtained through Red Harvest or the relevant companion lockbox and should be used to finish the offensive stat spread.

INSIGNIAS & COLLARS

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Whisperknife uses noticeably different single-target and multi-target power setups. Don't try to make one bar do both jobs badly when loadouts exist.

SINGLE TARGET

DISHEARTENING STRIKE

Maintain its damage-over-time effect and damage buff by refreshing it roughly every ten seconds.

CLOUD OF STEEL

Main filler attack. It also helps maintain continual Action Point generation between encounter cycles.

SHADOW STRIKE

Core part of the normal single-target sequence and used immediately after entering Stealth.

LASHING BLADE

Heavy single-target encounter used immediately after Shadow Strike.

IMPACT SHOT

Standard final encounter in the single-target sequence.

SMOKE BOMB

Swap this in for Impact Shot when a boss encounter contains adds or when extra crowd control and multi-target damage are useful.

MULTI TARGET

BLADE FLURRY

Main AoE burst encounter. Normally use it once during Stealth rather than immediately spending both charges.

SMOKE BOMB

Drop it into the pack after the opening Blade Flurry.

PATH OF THE BLADE

Adds sustained multi-target damage while you're fighting inside the enemy pack.

POSITIONING FEATURES

Dagger Threat is useful when you can maintain normal boss flanking. Swap to Advantageous Position when boss mechanics or spawned mobs make reliable flanking difficult.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise boons that increase the offensive percentages the build needs, followed by bonuses against the enemy types you're actually fighting.

  • Power
  • Combat Advantage
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Accuracy
  • Enemy-specific damage bonuses
  • Recharge and Action Point utility where useful

Blessed boons are particularly useful when running with a healer who regularly maintains heal-over-time effects.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

SINGLE TARGET ◆ NORMAL COMBAT

  1. FLANK THE BOSS Position with an ally on the opposite side so Combat Advantage remains active.
  2. DISHEARTENING STRIKE Apply the damage-over-time effect and maintain it approximately every ten seconds.
  3. MOVE NEXT TO THE TARGET Be in position before committing Stealth.
  4. STEALTH → SHADOW STRIKE Enter Stealth and immediately use Shadow Strike.
  5. LASHING BLADE Follow Shadow Strike with your heavy single-target hit.
  6. IMPACT SHOT Finish the normal encounter sequence with Impact Shot.
  7. CLOUD OF STEEL Use as filler while encounters recover and continue building Action Points.

MULTI TARGET

  1. GET INTO THE MIDDLE OF THE PACK Yes, you're a Rogue. No, apparently personal space isn't part of the build. 😂
  2. ENTER STEALTH Prepare the opening AoE burst.
  3. BLADE FLURRY ◆ ONCE Use one Blade Flurry while Stealthed.
  4. SMOKE BOMB + PATH OF THE BLADE Layer the AoE effects over the enemy pack.
  5. WAIT FOR STEALTH When Stealth returns, use it with Blade Flurry again.
DON'T SPAM BOTH BLADE FLURRIES: Keeping one available lets you pair the next use with Stealth instead of wasting the stronger interaction.

ARTIFACT CALL

Enter the call with encounters ready, maintain Combat Advantage and stack your strongest attacks inside the party's roughly ten-second support window.

Cavalry's Haste and Tactician's Precision are important to the full cooldown sequence. Without them, perform the opening burst through Hateful Knives, then use Cloud of Steel until your encounters return rather than forcing a rotation your cooldowns cannot support.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

SMOKE BOMB ◆ ST

Replace Impact Shot when a boss fight regularly spawns adds or when crowd control is valuable.

ADVANTAGEOUS POSITION

Replace Dagger Threat when normal flanking is unreliable or when dealing with mobs during boss encounters.

VOIDTOUCHED / XARYXIAN

Strong weapon alternatives if you don't yet want to invest in the newest artifact set.

SAFER BOOTS

Skip the high-damage Celestial boots while learning content if losing 20% maximum health is turning every mechanic into a near-death experience.

SCALED CONTENT

Keep Astral Raider's Gloves available. Their damage bonus can be extremely valuable once the queue scales your item level.

STAT FLEXIBILITY

Don't panic over a few missing percentage points. Real groups aren't always perfectly optimised and leaving a little spare room can actually make the build more consistent.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Whisperknife has a wonderfully Rogue approach to AoE: walk directly into the middle of everybody, disappear, then start throwing sharp objects around. 🤣

On bosses, don't underestimate Disheartening Strike. Refreshing it every ten seconds and maintaining Combat Advantage aren't glamorous, but they're part of what makes the flashy burst actually work.

And the artifact call matters massively. This isn't the toon where you can miss the call, throw a few knives afterwards and go "eh, close enough." Your ten-second murder window is where a ridiculous chunk of your damage lives. 😂
