MASTER DUNGEON ◆ MECHANICS

DEMONWEB PITS

Dodge the lasers. Control the demons. Try not to get webbed into oblivion.

OVERVIEW

DEMONWEB PITS

Master Demonweb Pits is built around heavy tank pressure, add control, spread and stack mechanics, crowd control and timed damage checks.

The most dangerous sections are the first boss laser phases, the second boss add pressure and tank busters, and Lolth's chained-player and weapon phases.

BOSS ONE

MARILITH

  • The boss uses strong frontal attacks. DPS should avoid standing directly in front of her.
  • Tanks need to maintain stamina and mitigation because her direct attacks can hit hard.
  • She periodically creates clones of herself.
  • The real boss must be identified and damaged to end the clone phase.
  • During clone phases, the copies can also attack, increasing pressure on the group.

KEY MECHANIC

ROTATING LASER

  • The boss creates a rotating laser that sweeps around the arena.
  • Contact with the laser can kill a player outright.
  • Watch the boss and clone positioning before the laser begins.
  • Rotate around the arena with the beam and stay ahead of it.
  • During later phases, meteors and fireballs can appear at the same time, making movement more difficult.
  • Avoid getting trapped against the outer edge with no safe route around the laser.

AVOIDABLE DAMAGE

METEORS & FIREBALLS

  • Large red meteor areas appear around the arena.
  • Move out of them before they land.
  • Being hit can apply a debuff that causes you to take increased damage.
  • Avoid repeated hits because the extra damage becomes increasingly dangerous.
  • Fireballs can follow players and deal damage over time.
  • Keep moving and avoid allowing fireballs and meteors to trap you during laser phases.

THROUGH THE DUNGEON

DEMON WAVES

  • Enemy groups can deal heavy damage if allowed to gain combat advantage.
  • Group enemies together and use crowd control where possible.
  • Some demon enemies can grab players with pincers and hold them in place for several seconds.
  • Prioritise these enemies before they disable a tank, healer or DPS during another mechanic.

BOSS TWO

GRUMPH BA'RAN

  • The boss repeatedly shields herself during the encounter.
  • Burn through the shield before meaningful damage can be dealt to her health.
  • Additional demons continually spawn throughout the fight.
  • Keep on top of the adds rather than allowing them to accumulate.
  • When the boss is shielded, use the downtime to kill dangerous adds.
  • When the shield drops, switch back and maximise damage to the boss.

TANK MECHANIC

TANK BUSTER

  • The boss periodically charges a large orb and throws it at the tank.
  • The attack deals extremely heavy damage.
  • Tanks should use strong mitigation before the orb lands.
  • The attack also deals area damage.
  • The tank should move away from the rest of the group before resolving it.
  • Be especially careful if a demon has grabbed or stunned the tank when the buster begins.

SUMMONED BOSS

ORCUS

  • Orcus spawns during the second boss encounter.
  • He uses a large cone attack capable of pushing players back.
  • Move outside the cone rather than attempting to absorb it.
  • Orcus also uses Power Word Kill, targeting a player with a pointing-finger mechanic followed by heavy damage.
  • His direct attacks against the tank are also dangerous.
  • Continue managing the other demon adds while Orcus is active.

FINAL BURN

SECOND BOSS DPS CHECK

  • Near the end of the encounter, the boss enters a timed damage phase.
  • The group takes increasing damage over time.
  • Burn the boss quickly before the incoming damage overwhelms the group.

FINAL BOSS

LOLTH

  • Lolth uses heavy direct attacks and can crowd control or knock down the tank.
  • Tanks should block dangerous attacks where possible to avoid being disabled during other mechanics.
  • Phase spiders periodically enter the arena.
  • Kill them before they become an additional threat during more important mechanics.

SPREAD MECHANIC

RED AREAS

  • Players can receive large red circles.
  • As soon as the circle appears, move away from your current position.
  • Do not move into another player's marked area.
  • Remaining in the original impact area when it resolves can kill you.

PAIR MECHANIC

RED CHAINS

  • Two players can become linked together by a red chain.
  • Unlike some other chain mechanics, these players must stay close together.
  • Do not try to break the chain by running apart.
  • Remain together until the mechanic resolves.

SHARED DAMAGE

HYPOTHERMIA

  • A player can receive the shared-damage Hypothermia marker.
  • Group around the marked player to split the incoming damage.
  • If the tank is able to mitigate the attack safely, the mechanic may be survivable without the whole group, but the safest approach is to share it.
  • Avoid being crowd controlled when Hypothermia resolves, as this can prevent correct positioning or blocking.

INTERMISSION

TILTING PLATFORM

  • At approximately 50% health, Lolth becomes immune and moves to another platform.
  • The platform begins tilting and rocks fall from above.
  • Move out of the marked impact areas.
  • Falling rocks deal heavy damage.
  • Be prepared for additional knock-ups as the platform shifts.

INTERMISSION TARGET

GODLY WEAPON

  • During the intermission, damage the Godly Weapon embedded in the platform.
  • Spiders spawn on both sides of the arena.
  • These spiders can channel a green beam into the weapon and heal it.
  • Crowd control or kill the spiders quickly to stop the healing.
  • Pushing spiders off the platform does not permanently remove them because they can return.
  • Maintain damage on the weapon while controlling the spiders.
  • The group also takes ongoing area damage during this phase, so healers need to maintain strong group healing.

FINAL PHASE

LOLTH BURN

  • After the weapon phase, Lolth returns and can shield herself.
  • Previous mechanics continue, including red areas, chains, Hypothermia and spiders.
  • Near the end of the fight, Lolth enters a timed final damage check.
  • The group has roughly one minute to kill her.
  • Save enough burst damage to finish the boss before the timer expires.

QUICK REFERENCE

DEMONWEB SURVIVAL NOTES

  • Rotating laser → move with it, never through it.
  • Meteor → move out and avoid stacking debuffs.
  • Pincer demon → kill before it grabs someone important.
  • Second boss shield → kill adds while shielded, burst boss when open.
  • Tank buster → mitigate and move away from the group.
  • Orcus cone → get out of it.
  • Red circle → move away from your original position.
  • Red chain → stay together.
  • Hypothermia → stack and share.
  • Weapon phase → stop spiders healing the weapon.
  • Final timer → burn Lolth before time runs out.
