CRAGMIRE CRYPTS

Dodge the traps. Control the adds. Try not to set the entire room on fire.

OVERVIEW

CRAGMIRE CRYPTS

Cragmire Crypts is heavily filled with traps, enemy ambushes and additional enemies. The final encounter against Blackdagger requires the group to manage both adds and persistent fire across the arena.

THROUGHOUT THE DUNGEON

WATCH THE FLOOR

  • Cragmire Crypts contains numerous spike and blade traps across floors and corridors.
  • Watch the ground carefully while moving through the dungeon rather than running blindly through rooms.
  • Some rooms contain several traps across the centre. Moving around the edges can avoid triggering them.
  • Later sections contain spike pits capable of instantly killing players who fall into them.
  • Activate the nearby mechanism and wait for the bridge before attempting to cross a spike pit.

MINI BOSS

CALLOS TAM

  • Callos Tam deals strong single-target damage and also has area-of-effect attacks.
  • His main mechanic is summoning groups of undead adds.
  • Group the adds together and use area damage to hit them alongside Callos Tam.
  • Do not allow the summoned enemies to overwhelm an individual player.

MINI BOSS

RIVAL ADVENTURING PARTY

  • The opposing adventuring party contains its own tank, damage dealers and healer.
  • Prioritise their healer first to prevent the enemy group sustaining itself.
  • Once the healer is dead, focus the damage dealers.
  • Leave the enemy tank until last where possible.
  • Reducing the number of enemies quickly makes the fight considerably easier.

DUNGEON MECHANIC

PHASE SPIDERS

  • Phase spiders appear during the deeper sections of the crypt.
  • Lower-damage groups should focus one phase spider at a time rather than fighting several simultaneously.
  • Spiders can create webbed areas that reduce player movement.

FINAL BOSS

BLACKDAGGER

  • Blackdagger deals heavy damage to players standing directly in front of him.
  • The tank should keep Blackdagger's attention while the rest of the group attacks from safer positions.
  • Blackdagger uses a repeated frontal slash attack. Avoid standing in front of him when this begins.
  • He regularly throws bombs around the arena.
  • Each bomb leaves behind a persistent circle of fire.
  • The fire deals heavy damage and remains until the water barrel mechanic is used.
  • Keep repositioning as fire spreads across the arena and avoid allowing the group to become trapped.

KEY MECHANIC

WATER BARREL

  • A water barrel at the end of the arena can clear all existing fire from the floor.
  • Use the barrel when the amount of fire leaves the group with too little safe space to continue fighting.
  • Activating the barrel causes Blackdagger to become enraged and rapidly attack players around the room.
  • Be prepared to dodge or mitigate his attacks immediately after the barrel is activated.
  • Each additional use of the barrel causes Blackdagger's retaliation to hit harder.
  • Use the barrel only when necessary rather than clearing manageable fire unnecessarily.

ADD CONTROL

DON'T LET THEM BUILD UP

  • Blackdagger summons additional enemies throughout the encounter.
  • DPS should switch to the adds when they appear and remove them quickly.
  • Snipers have relatively low health but deal high damage, making them priority targets.
  • Stronger enemies begin appearing as Blackdagger's health decreases.
  • If adds are allowed to accumulate while the arena fills with fire, the group can quickly become overwhelmed.
  • When Blackdagger dies, surviving adds remain active. Kill them before considering the encounter finished.

QUICK REFERENCE

DON'T GET CREMATED

  • Watch for traps throughout the dungeon.
  • Rival party → healer first, DPS second, tank last.
  • Blackdagger → stay away from his front.
  • Bombs → leave persistent fire.
  • Adds → kill them before they accumulate.
  • Snipers → priority targets.
  • Arena running out of space → use the water barrel.
  • Barrel used → prepare for Blackdagger's retaliation.
  • Don't spam the barrel — his retaliation becomes stronger.
  • Blackdagger dead → finish any remaining adds.
