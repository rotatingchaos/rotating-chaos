HOW IT WORKS

VANGUARD EXPLAINED

Vanguard is Fighter's dedicated tank paragon. The build combines defensive percentages, shield management, deliberate threat generation and party support.

Blocking is only part of the job. Vanguard still needs to attack deliberately so high-output DPS players can unload during artifact calls without taking threat.

Positioning matters too. Keep bosses facing away from the group and maintain predictable positioning wherever mechanics allow it.

THREAT

TIDE OF IRON

Tide of Iron is a major part of Vanguard's threat engine and should be used immediately before the heavy-threat encounters.

The Tide of Iron into Shield Throw / Anvil of Doom animation-cancel rhythm is worth practising.

If you hit block too quickly after Tide of Iron, the animation can cancel before the additional threat effect activates.

Practise the timing until the threat proc is reliable rather than simply trying to execute the cancel as quickly as possible.

ARTIFACT CALL

HOLDING AGAINST BURST

The full threat rotation is tight and boss mechanics can interrupt it.

When the call gets disrupted, the important core is:

Two Shield Throws ◆ Anvil of Doom ◆ Daily ◆ Dragonfire ◆ Retaliate

Use those with Tide of Iron's increased threat generation wherever possible.

This concentrated sequence is designed to establish enough threat for the DPS to use their strongest burst safely.

RETALIATE

USE THE DOUBLE

Retaliate won't always be available at exactly the same point in every artifact call.

Use it whenever the opportunity appears, particularly around the middle of the call when the group's enemy debuffs are strongest.

Activate the double Retaliate as often as possible.

BETWEEN CALLS

BRAZEN SLASH

Use Brazen Slash while waiting for encounters to return between artifact calls.

Continue using Tide of Iron into Shield Throw and Anvil of Doom for maximum threat generation when those encounters become available.

MJS

FIRST BOSS

Cleave is recommended instead of Brazen Slash for Master Jotunskar first boss.

Following the first Basilisk eyes, a quick artifact call should give you the opportunity to establish threat with encounters, Dragonfire and the Daily before mechanics become messy.

Tank-busters can remove you from the fight. If the Remorhaz or Basilisks switch targets while you're gone, immediately use Enforced Threat or Anvil of Doom with the relevant threat setup when you return.

SUPPORT

MOUNT BEFORE ARTIFACT

As a support role, activate the mount before the artifact.

Some mount combat powers have an activation period and some artifacts also take time to apply their effect.

Using them in the wrong order can leave the party's strongest damage landing before your support effects are fully active.

SHIELD

STAMINA MANAGEMENT

Don't burn the entire shield on harmless damage.

Preserve stamina for tank-busters and dangerous mechanics while using the build's stamina and cooldown support to keep the shield available.

Steel Recovery supports stamina management as encounters and Dailies cycle.

FLEXIBILITY

DEFENSE VS THREAT

If survivability is the problem, move the build further toward Maximum HP, defensive percentages, defensive companions and Rime Temper.

If threat is the problem, lean harder into Shield Throw, Anvil of Doom, Retaliate and offensive/threat support.

Once both survivability and threat are comfortable, use the remaining flexibility to increase party support through companions, enhancements, artifacts and mount powers.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Vanguard isn't "hold shield and hope."

You're attacking, blocking, positioning, watching threat, timing support powers and preventing the DPS from accidentally discovering what a tank-buster feels like. 😂

Learn the Tide of Iron timing. If you cancel it too quickly, you've performed a very impressive animation cancel that also cancelled the fucking thing you wanted. 🤣

And remember: MOUNT BEFORE ARTIFACT. Nobody wants your debuff arriving after the boss has already been mugged.
