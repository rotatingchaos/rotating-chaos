THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Hellbringer is the Warlock's damage paragon, built around magical damage, Curse management, Soul Sparks and fitting as much damage as possible into the group's main burst windows.

The build changes noticeably between boss fights and packs. Single-target focuses on maximising damage against one enemy, while the multi-target setup repeatedly applies and consumes Curse to tear through groups.

Soul Sparks are another major part of the class. Building and spending them properly improves both damage and the speed at which your encounters return.

THE BASIC IDEA: Curse things. Consume the Curse. Curse them again. Build Soul Sparks. Spend Soul Sparks. Repeat until whatever offended you regrets spawning.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Hellbringer deals magical damage, so the finished build should be balanced around the core offensive DPS percentages while also taking advantage of Intelligence.

POWER

Core offensive stat and one of the first percentages to build toward its useful limit.

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

A major damage source whenever you have proper positioning around the target.

CRITICAL STRIKE

Build alongside Critical Severity for reliable critical damage.

CRITICAL SEVERITY

Important for maximising the value of those critical hits.

ACCURACY

Useful offensive stat, normally completed after the stronger damage priorities are where they need to be.

RECHARGE

Faster encounter recovery can make the entire Hellbringer rotation considerably smoother.

ABILITY SCORES

Intelligence is particularly valuable because Hellbringer's attacks are magical. Your second ability investment can be adjusted around the final stat balance of the toon.

DON'T BUILD FROM THE CHARACTER SHEET ALONE: Check your percentages while actually attacking. Companion bonuses, consumables, equipment effects and party buffs can completely change what still needs rating.

LOADOUT

GEAR

Module 33.5 gives Hellbringer several viable ways to finish the build rather than one compulsory equipment configuration.

LAUGHING VOID + LIGHTNING FLASH

Endgame configuration using the Laughing Void set with Lightning Flash.

LAUGHING VOID + SWIFT SYNERGY

Alternative endgame configuration using Swift Synergy to change how the remaining stats are balanced.

SCINTILLANT + SWIFT SYNERGY

A further endgame option retaining the Scintillant set and building around Swift Synergy.

MIDGAME

A strong setup can be assembled from campaign and advanced content without requiring Master gear.

STARTER

Campaign equipment provides a realistic route into the build before expensive enchantments and insignias are available.

SCALED CONTENT

Keep Astral Raider Arms from Wildspace available for scaled content rather than automatically wearing the highest item-level arms.

THERE ISN'T ONE SACRED LOADOUT: The current build has multiple endgame versions because changing a set or bonus changes the stats required elsewhere. Build the whole toon, not twelve unrelated equipment slots.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments should finish the offensive stat distribution created by your chosen gear configuration.

The exact mix changes between the Laughing Void, Scintillant, Lightning Flash and Swift Synergy variants, so copying another player's enchantments without copying the rest of their build can leave several stats badly balanced.

OFFENSIVE ENCHANTMENTS

Fill whichever offensive ratings remain short after equipment, companion gear, insignias and boons are included.

STRIKE & GUARD

Include the modern Strike and Guard slots when assessing the complete build.

REINFORCEMENTS

Use armour kits and jewels to make smaller rating corrections without sacrificing stronger equipment bonuses.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Use a strong current active artifact suited to the group's burst window and choose secondary artifacts for their stats and useful set bonuses.

Hellbringer benefits heavily from coordinated artifact calls. Prepare Soul Sparks and encounter cooldowns before the call rather than starting the burst window with half the build unavailable.

BE READY BEFORE THE CALL: Artifact ready, mount ready, encounters ready, Soul Sparks ready. The boss isn't going to politely extend everybody's debuffs because your Soul Scorch wasn't prepared.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Companion choices should provide useful damage bonuses, complete missing offensive stats and contribute to group damage where possible.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Use a strong current single-target companion for boss fights and consider an AoE or utility summon when clearing packs.

COMPANION BONUSES

Select bonuses around your remaining stats and useful personal damage effects rather than blindly copying five names.

COMPANION GEAR

One of the easiest places to correct offensive ratings when the rest of the equipment is already providing strong bonuses.

DEBUFF ENHANCEMENT

In organised dungeon groups, coordinate a useful debuff enhancement with the rest of the party instead of everybody stacking the same effect.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Choose the mount combat power around the type of fight. Bosses favour a strong single-target hit, while packs give wide-area mount powers considerably more value.

SINGLE TARGET

Use a strong current boss mount and place it inside the main artifact call.

MULTI TARGET

Swap to an AoE combat power when several enemies can be hit together.

INSIGNIAS & BONUSES

Build the stable around useful damage, cooldown, movement and companion bonuses while using the individual insignias themselves to help finish missing ratings.

  • TACTICIAN'S PRECISION — useful encounter cooldown support around Daily use.
  • ARTIFICER'S PERSUASION — useful cooldown support around artifact activation.
  • GLADIATOR'S GUILE — useful movement and stamina utility.
  • WARLORD'S MOTIVATION — useful when building around summoned companion damage.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

CORE MECHANICS

CURSE

Several Hellbringer powers apply, consume or interact with Curse. In AoE the cycle of applying and removing Curse becomes a central part of the rotation.

SOUL SPARKS

Build Soul Sparks through combat and spend them deliberately. They are a resource, not decorative orange dots.

SOUL SCORCH

Spend Soul Sparks for damage and cooldown reduction. In the multi-target setup, use Soul Scorch once three pips are ready.

SOUL PUPPET

Hellbringer mechanics and feats can use the Soul Puppet as an additional source of pressure and damage.

MULTI-TARGET SETUP

The current AoE setup is built around repeatedly applying Curse, consuming it with Curse Bite and immediately applying Curse again.

CURSE BITE

Consumes Curse from affected enemies for multi-target damage. Reapply Curse after consuming it.

SOUL SCORCH

Use at three Soul Spark pips for damage and to reduce encounter cooldowns.

DARK HELIX

Important at-will for damage, Action Point generation and Soul Spark generation.

SINGLE-TARGET SETUP

The boss loadout switches away from the dedicated pack-clearing setup and concentrates damage into one target. Keep Curse active, manage Soul Sparks and prepare the full encounter sequence for the artifact call.

ANIMATION CANCELLING

Hellbringer can cancel the ending animation of certain powers with its tactical movement. Done correctly, the effect still fires but the Warlock can begin the next action sooner.

Dark Helix is the main power worth learning this with. Faster casts improve damage, Action Point generation and Soul Spark generation. Hellish Rebuke can also be cancelled quickly when reapplying Curse during tightly optimised boss rotations.

THIS IS OPTIONAL MIN-MAXING: Learn the normal rotation first. Animation cancelling is only an improvement if you're still actually landing the powers and keeping the burst inside the artifact window.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise offensive boons and use them to complement the final build rather than overfilling ratings already supplied elsewhere.

  • Power
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Combat Advantage
  • Accuracy
  • Forte where useful
  • Recharge Speed
  • Action Point Gain

Use the guild boons to fill whatever the current loadout actually needs. Changing gear configuration may mean changing the guild boon with it.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

MULTI TARGET

  1. APPLY CURSE Get Curse onto the pack before consuming it.
  2. CURSE BITE Consume Curse for AoE damage.
  3. REAPPLY CURSE Immediately prepare the enemies for the next Curse interaction.
  4. BUILD SOUL SPARKS Continue attacking and generate Soul Sparks.
  5. SOUL SCORCH AT THREE PIPS Spend the resource for damage and encounter cooldown reduction.
  6. REPEAT Curse, consume, reapply and spend Soul Sparks while the pack remains alive.

SINGLE TARGET

  1. PREPARE SOUL SPARKS Don't walk into the artifact call with an empty resource bar.
  2. APPLY CURSE Establish Curse before beginning the main damage sequence.
  3. ARTIFACT CALL Begin the coordinated boss burst.
  4. USE YOUR ENCOUNTERS Fire the single-target encounter sequence while the group's buffs and debuffs are active.
  5. SOUL SCORCH Spend accumulated Soul Sparks for damage and cooldown reduction.
  6. MOUNT & DAILY Fit the major burst powers inside the remaining artifact window.
  7. REAPPLY CURSE Maintain Curse as required while continuing the rotation.
  8. BUILD BACK UP Use your at-wills between calls to rebuild Soul Sparks and Action Points and prepare for the next burst.
HELLBRINGER IN ONE SENTENCE: You have Curse, Soul Sparks, encounters, a puppet, an artifact, a mount and animation cancelling to think about — and somehow you're expected to notice the giant red circle under your feet too. 😂

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

LAUGHING VOID OR SCINTILLANT

Both appear in the current endgame configurations. Changing the set changes the rest of the stat puzzle, so rebalance the toon rather than swapping only those three pieces.

LIGHTNING FLASH OR SWIFT SYNERGY

Both are viable foundations for the current build and require different supporting choices.

STARTER BUILD

You do not need a finished endgame account before playing Hellbringer. A campaign-based setup can be assembled without the expensive enchantment and insignia investment.

MIDGAME BUILD

Campaign and advanced-content equipment can bridge the gap between the starter setup and the Master-content endgame version.

SCALED CONTENT

Keep Astral Raider Arms from Wildspace available. Their usefulness in scaled content can outweigh simply equipping a newer, higher-item-level pair of arms.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Hellbringer is one of those classes that looks like random purple shit flying everywhere until you understand what the Warlock is actually doing. Then you realise there is an alarming amount of resource management happening underneath all the demonic fireworks.

Learn Curse and Soul Spark management before worrying about animation cancelling. Once the normal rotation is automatic, shaving animation time becomes useful instead of just making your hands perform interpretive dance on the controller.
