BARBARIAN ◆ TANK

SENTINEL

Aggro ◆ Survival ◆ Group Support

Overview Stats Gear Enchantments Artifacts Companions Mounts Powers & Feats Boons Rotation

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Sentinel turns the Barbarian into a highly mobile tank built around threat generation, Rage, blocking and aggressive group support. Rather than standing still and simply absorbing damage, Sentinel actively attacks to maintain control of the fight.

This setup is built for current Module 33.5 endgame play, where the tank isn't only expected to survive. You're also helping the party deal more damage through support artifacts, mount powers, companion debuffs and properly timed artifact calls.

The introduction of Strike and Guard enchantment slots has made Sentinel threat generation considerably easier, allowing experienced tanks to trade some additional threat support for movement and defensive utility.

THE SENTINEL JOB: Make everything hate you, survive the consequences and somehow still find time to debuff the boss for everybody else. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Sentinel needs enough defensive strength to comfortably survive the content first. Once that baseline is secure, the remaining build can be pushed toward group support and reliable threat generation.

1 ◆ DEFENSE Core mitigation against incoming damage and one of the first defensive percentages to establish.
2 ◆ AWARENESS Extremely important for reducing the impact of Combat Advantage damage, particularly in difficult group content.
3 ◆ CRITICAL AVOIDANCE Reduces the danger from enemy critical hits and strengthens your overall defensive profile.
4 ◆ DEFLECT Adds another layer of mitigation and works particularly well with the Sentinel's defensive toolkit.
5 ◆ MAXIMUM HIT POINTS Your defensive percentages reduce the incoming hit; your health pool determines whether the bit that gets through actually kills you.
TANK RULE: Don't sacrifice the ability to survive purely to make a spreadsheet prettier. Dead tanks generate approximately fuck-all threat. 😂

LOADOUT

GEAR

The current Sentinel setup combines defensive equipment with pieces that improve group damage. Build enough survivability for the content you're running, then introduce support gear wherever it doesn't make the toon unnecessarily fragile.

HEAD

Use a current tank or support helm that contributes useful defensive percentages or a reliable party benefit.

ARMOUR

Prioritise a strong defensive or group-support bonus. The best choice depends on whether survival or party damage is currently the limiting factor.

ARMS

Defensive arms are perfectly valid while progressing. Stronger groups can move toward enemy-debuff or team-support effects.

FEET

Use this slot to complete missing defensive percentages, movement or another useful support effect.

WEAPONS

Current support-oriented weapons are ideal when your defenses are established, particularly where their bonuses benefit the whole party.

RINGS & CLOTHING

Balance Defense, Awareness, Critical Avoidance, Deflect and survivability. Don't keep stacking rating into a percentage that's already comfortably covered.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Module 33.5's Strike and Guard enchantment slots are particularly useful to Sentinel because they make maintaining threat easier than before.

  • Guard support for stronger tanking and threat control
  • Strike support to improve reliable aggro generation
  • Defense where your mitigation needs help
  • Awareness against heavy Combat Advantage damage
  • Critical Avoidance and Deflect to complete the defensive spread
  • Hit Points and stamina-related utility where required

Reinforcements should fill the gaps left by the rest of your equipment rather than automatically copying somebody else's exact combination.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Sentinel is a support role, so the primary artifact should contribute to the group's burst rather than simply giving the tank another personal toy.

PRIMARY ARTIFACT

Use a current enemy-debuff or group-support artifact and coordinate it with the party's artifact call.

SECONDARIES

Use secondary artifacts to complete defensive percentages, maximum health and useful set bonuses.

SUPPORT TIMING: Mount first, artifact second. Some mount effects need time to activate, so firing the artifact first can leave the DPS halfway through their burst before your mount debuff even arrives.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

The current Sentinel setup leans heavily into group-support companions rather than purely personal defensive bonuses once the tank is capable of surviving the content.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Prefer a current companion that improves total party damage while still allowing you to maintain the defensive stats required for the encounter.

SPINED DEVIL

Strong group-damage option in the current setup and preferred when the party isn't relying on another companion to supply missing offensive percentages.

BLACK DEATH SCORPION

Valuable group-support option where its positioning requirements can be maintained reliably.

ENHANCEMENTS

Armor Break, Dulled Senses and Vulnerability are excellent dungeon support options. Coordinate them across the party rather than duplicating effects that don't need duplicating.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

The mount setup should support both survival and the party's burst windows. Your combat power is part of the group's debuff rotation, not simply something to press whenever it lights up.

COMBAT POWER

Use a strong current group-support or enemy-debuff mount power appropriate to the rest of the party.

EQUIP POWER

Choose defensive percentages, health or whatever stat is needed to finish the tank setup.

INSIGNIA BONUSES

Prioritise useful defensive, stamina and cooldown-support bonuses rather than chasing item level alone.

COLLARS

Defensive utility, stamina, movement and encounter support can all be useful depending on the final stat balance.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Sentinel's combat loop revolves around maintaining threat while using Rage and encounters aggressively. The current setup uses Sentinel's Slash as an important threat-generation tool around encounter and Daily usage.

CORE AT-WILLS

SENTINEL'S SLASH

Central threat-generation tool. Use it to strengthen the threat generated by the attacks that follow, particularly during burst windows.

SURE STRIKE

Useful filler between encounter cooldowns and part of sustained threat generation on a single target.

ENCOUNTERS

TAKEDOWN

Important burst-window encounter. Where possible, have Takedown ready at the beginning of an artifact call rather than wasting it immediately beforehand.

SINGLE TARGET

Build the encounter bar around strong threat generation and reliable boss control, using Sentinel's Slash before the major threat-producing attacks.

MULTI-TARGET

Switch toward powers capable of controlling several enemies and distributing threat quickly across the pack.

RAGE

Use Rage for sustained aggro whenever it's available. Only hold it when an artifact call is about to begin and saving it will improve your burst threat.

DAILY ◆ SPINNING STRIKE

Spinning Strike is particularly useful in multi-target situations. It distributes substantial threat across multiple enemies and also provides control immunity during its animation.

CLASS FEATURES

THREATENING PRESENCE

Recommended while learning Sentinel or whenever maintaining aggro is difficult. The additional threat makes the whole rotation more forgiving.

BRAVERY

Experienced Sentinels who no longer require the extra threat can switch to Bravery for additional movement speed and Deflect.

DON'T GET COCKY TOO EARLY: Threatening Presence isn't training wheels if you still need it. Losing the boss because Dave's DPS toon sneezed particularly hard is considerably less impressive than keeping the extra threat feature. 😂

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Build boons around the defensive percentages required for the content, then use remaining choices for stamina, movement and useful support.

  • Defense
  • Awareness
  • Critical Avoidance
  • Deflect
  • Maximum Hit Points
  • Stamina regeneration
  • Movement and cooldown utility where appropriate

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ARTIFACT CALL & AGGRO FLOW

  1. HOLD TAKEDOWN Where possible, keep Takedown ready as the group approaches the next artifact call.
  2. MOUNT POWER Fire the support mount first so its activation lines up properly with the DPS burst.
  3. ARTIFACT Apply the group's artifact debuff immediately after the mount.
  4. SENTINEL'S SLASH Establish the increased threat generation before dumping your major attacks.
  5. ENCOUNTERS Get at least one complete encounter pass into the artifact window, beginning with Takedown where possible.
  6. DAILY Use the appropriate Daily inside the burst window while Sentinel's Slash threat generation is active.
  7. SECOND ENCOUNTER PASS If cooldown support returns encounters during the call, use them again while the group debuffs are still active.
  8. SURE STRIKE / SENTINEL'S SLASH Use your at-wills while waiting for encounters to return between burst windows.
  9. RAGE Spend Rage for sustained threat rather than hoarding it forever. Save it only when the next artifact call is seconds away.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

THREATENING PRESENCE

Use this whenever aggro isn't completely reliable. It's the safer option while learning the current rotation.

BRAVERY

Once threat is comfortable, switch for movement speed and Deflect to gain more utility from the class-feature slot.

SPINNING STRIKE

Particularly strong for multi-target encounters where several enemies need to remain glued to you simultaneously.

MORE DEFENSE

If you're progressing new content, sacrifice support before sacrificing survival. Add the aggressive debuff pieces back once the encounter becomes comfortable.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Sentinel is not supposed to spend the entire fight hiding behind the block button. You're a Barbarian. Apparently even tanking has to be done aggressively. 😂

The big habit to learn is Sentinel's Slash before the important threat-generating attacks. Inside an artifact call, even one proper round of encounters plus your Daily with that increased threat is enough to hold against the majority of DPS players.

And remember the support order: mount before artifact. If your mount finally applies its effect after the DPS have already unloaded half their rotation, congratulations — you've successfully debuffed the boss for the quiet bit. 🤣
