HOW IT WORKS

ARCANIST EXPLAINED

Arcanist is the lightning and arcane side of the Wizard. This setup is built around aggressive multi-target damage, combining persistent area damage, control and lightning effects against tightly grouped enemies.

Despite being a Wizard, this setup is not designed to spend the entire fight hiding at the other end of the room. Several of its important powers are centred on the caster, so positioning close enough to the pack matters.

ALTERNATIVES

SWAPS & TRADE-OFFS

ARCANE SINGULARITY VS OPPRESSIVE FORCE

Oppressive Force is the immediate option when enemies are already grouped and you want damage and control. Arcane Singularity is useful when pulling the pack together is more valuable.

ELEMENTAL REINFORCEMENT VS STEP ABOVE MASTERY

Elemental Reinforcement is the more reliable AoE option for this setup because the rotation naturally uses Cold, Arcane and Lightning spells. Step Above Mastery relies more heavily on maintaining Arcane stacks.

STAYING AT RANGE

This setup deliberately plays fairly close to enemies. A ranged Arcanist is possible, but changing the powers to support that becomes a different variation rather than simply doing this rotation from further away.
