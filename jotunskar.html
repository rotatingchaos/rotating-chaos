JOTUNSKAR

THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

This guide currently covers the Master version of Jotunskar. The dungeon is heavily focused on frost management, group cleansing, tank positioning and handling mechanics cleanly rather than simply brute-forcing damage.

All three boss encounters use the same core frost-stack mechanic, so the group needs to stay aware of stacks and coordinate cleanses throughout the run.

CORE MECHANIC

FROST STACKS

Frost stacks build during the boss encounters.

At 26 stacks, the player dies.

The group should cleanse together at a brazier rather than taking cleanses individually and leaving other players stranded.

Group timing matters, especially for tanks who may need a brazier available for positioning during later encounters.

FIRST ENCOUNTER

BOSS ONE

HOLD ALL THREE ENEMIES

The tank should take aggro of the boss and both basilisks.

BASILISK GAZE

Dodge, block or turn away from the basilisk gaze.

Failing to react can result in heavy damage and a stun.

BASILISK HEALTH

Bring both basilisks down to roughly 20% health before focusing the boss.

When a basilisk reaches 0 HP it becomes enraged and deals more damage, so the cleanest strategy is to finish everything at roughly the same time.

SUPPORT DAMAGE

Multi-target artifacts and support mounts are useful during this fight, while DPS can still focus on single-target damage where appropriate.

TANK SKULL

When the tank receives the skull marker, they are grabbed and eaten.

This becomes a healer check, so the healer needs to keep the tank alive through the mechanic.

OTHER ATTACKS

Watch for basilisk tail swipes and move out of red areas.

SECOND ENCOUNTER

BOSS TWO

POSITIONING

The tank should bring the boss to the side of the arena furthest from the entrance and exit.

BRAZIER CONTROL

The tank rotates between two braziers.

Other players should not use those braziers without coordinating with the tank, because doing so can interfere with the tank's frost management.

KILL ADDS IMMEDIATELY

Adds must be killed as soon as they spawn.

If they reach the boss, they buff him and can trigger massive area damage.

Make sure the group has Combat Advantage on the adds to kill them quickly.

LARGE CONES

The boss uses large cone attacks that cannot simply be dodged through.

Tank positioning is therefore critical so the cones do not hit the rest of the group.

TANK SKULL

When the tank receives the skull marker, they must block the attack.

Failing to block it results in death.

FINAL ENCOUNTER

BOSS THREE

SKULL JUMP

When a player receives the skull red area, move it away from the group.

Once the red area stops following you, move out of it. The boss then jumps to that location.

Use the jump to break ice on nearby totems.

TANK SKULL

When the tank receives the skull marker, they must block the attack or they will die.

TOTEMS

The boss can be charged into iced totems.

Once a totem is exposed, DPS should attack and destroy it.

Active totems buff the boss.

If six totems become active, the group has only a short time before the encounter wipes.

The increasing blizzard effect on screen gives a visual warning, but the boss's buff icons are the safest way to track the totem count.

POISON RED AREA

When the poison red area appears, pass it between players to reduce its damage.

The damage reduction only works three times, so the group needs to coordinate the passes rather than letting one player hold it.

HYPO

On Master, Hypo should be shared between three to four players.

The damage is a flat amount that ignores defenses, so maximum HP is what matters.

Everyone hit by Hypo must then cleanse in a golden area.

Golden areas are limited, and players need to remain inside for roughly three seconds to complete the cleanse.

Failing to cleanse results in the group being killed by the follow-up tentacle mechanic.

ADD PHASE

When the boss phases and enemies spawn, the tank should take the mobs while the group focuses on destroying the ice chunks.

Starting together and rotating as a group makes this phase easier.

BLIZZARD AREAS

The boss creates blizzard areas during the fight.

These can be dealt with using the tankbuster/skull interaction to destroy or clear the affected areas.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Hold all required enemies, manage boss positioning carefully and keep control of brazier access where needed.

Skull mechanics on Boss Two and Boss Three must be blocked.

During the final boss, use positioning and boss charges to help expose or clear totems and blizzard mechanics.

HEALER

Be prepared for the Boss One skull mechanic, where the tank is grabbed and requires sustained healing.

Keep an eye on group frost stacks and coordinate cleanses rather than allowing players to scatter across braziers.

DPS

Prioritise adds when required, especially during Boss Two.

On the final boss, destroy exposed totems quickly and coordinate Hypo, poison passes and golden-area cleanses with the group.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Reaching 26 frost stacks without cleansing.

Using a brazier the tank needs during Boss Two.

Allowing adds to reach Boss Two and buff him.

Failing to block tank skull attacks.

Leaving too many active totems during the final encounter.

Mishandling the poison pass mechanic.

Failing to share Hypo or failing to cleanse afterwards.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Jotunskar rewards coordination much more than panic movement. Frost-stack control, brazier discipline and clean positioning make the dungeon dramatically easier.

On the final boss, do not tunnel the boss and ignore totems. Totem count, Hypo, poison passing and cleanses are the mechanics most likely to turn a good run into a very expensive floor inspection.
