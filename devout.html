CLERIC ◆ HEALER

DEVOUT

Healing ◆ Divinity ◆ Group Support

Overview Stats Gear Enchantments Artifacts Companions Mounts Powers & Feats Boons Rotation

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Devout is the Cleric's dedicated healing paragon. It combines strong healing-over-time, emergency healing, Divinity management and group support with several situational tools for difficult mechanics.

Healing Word forms the backbone of normal group healing. Keep it active across the party and then use your remaining powers to support damage, restore Divinity or react to incoming damage.

A good Devout isn't simply waiting for health bars to move. When the group is safe you can support their damage, prepare for upcoming mechanics and manage Divinity so you're ready when everything inevitably goes to shit.

THE BIG RULE: Keep Healing Word active. Your other powers can then be used proactively instead of constantly trying to recover from healing you could already have had ticking in the background.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Devout should be balanced around healing strength first while still taking advantage of useful support stats and effects. Check your actual percentages with the full build active rather than judging everything from raw ratings.

POWER

One of the core healing stats and a major priority when building the Devout.

OUTGOING HEALING

Directly supports the job you're here to do: keeping everybody alive.

CRITICAL STRIKE

Helps your heals critically hit and works alongside Critical Severity.

CRITICAL SEVERITY

Improves the strength of critical healing and should be balanced with Critical Strike.

RECHARGE & AP

Recharge speed and Action Point generation can become valuable once your core healing stats are in a strong position.

ABILITY SCORES

Wisdom and Dexterity form the main ability-score setup. If your Critical Severity is already pushed beyond the useful ceiling, some Dexterity can be moved into Charisma instead.

LOADOUT

GEAR

Build your Devout around healing first, then use alternative pieces to increase party damage support when the content and group allow it.

WEAPONS

Work toward a current support-friendly artifact weapon set. Voidtouched and Xaryxian remain useful alternatives if your current artifact weapons aren't ready.

HEALING GEAR

Prioritise equipment that improves your useful healing stats, Divinity management and ability to sustain the party.

SUPPORT GEAR

Once healing is comfortable, selected slots can be changed to equipment that improves the damage output of the group.

PROGRESSION

Don't wait for a complete endgame setup. Build the toon in stages and correct your stats as each new piece is added.

HEALING VS SUPPORT: More healing isn't automatically better once nobody is dying. That's the point where Devout can start trading unnecessary overhealing for useful group support.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments should support your healing stats while also helping with Action Point generation and recharge where your setup needs it.

HEALING STATS

Use enchantments and reinforcements to fill missing Power, Outgoing Healing, Critical Strike or Critical Severity.

ACTION POINT GAIN

Additional AP generation can be valuable when your setup relies heavily on maintaining useful Daily effects.

RECHARGE SPEED

If AP generation is already comfortable, recharge can improve uptime on powers such as Divine Glow and Exaltation.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Devout can use support artifacts rather than treating the active artifact slot as a personal damage button.

In organised groups, coordinate your artifact with the rest of the party so its buff or debuff contributes to the group's main artifact call. Secondary artifacts should support your healing stats and any useful set bonuses.

SUPPORT FIRST: Your job isn't to win the Cleric damage leaderboard. A well-timed support artifact can contribute far more to the run than a random personal damage hit.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Companion choices can strengthen your healing, fill missing stats and provide useful group support.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Use a current companion that contributes useful healing, support or group damage effects.

PLAYER BONUSES

Prioritise bonuses that improve your healing stats or provide worthwhile support effects.

COMPANION GEAR

Use companion equipment to correct whichever ratings the rest of the Devout setup leaves short.

SUPPORT ENHANCEMENTS

Enemy stat reductions and other group-support effects can be valuable, but coordinate them with the rest of the party.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mount combat powers can provide another layer of party support. Equip powers and insignia bonuses should then be selected around healing, recharge, Action Point generation, movement and survivability.

COMBAT POWER

In organised content, use a useful group-support mount power and coordinate it with the party's main damage window.

INSIGNIAS

Use insignias to finish missing ratings rather than copying somebody else's exact distribution blindly.

COOLDOWN SUPPORT

Insignia bonuses that help artifact or encounter cooldowns can fit naturally into a support-oriented Devout.

MOVEMENT

Movement speed is underrated on a healer. Getting into position before the mechanic happens is considerably easier than healing everybody from the other side of the postcode.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Devout has a flexible toolkit. There is a standard setup for normal healing and support, but several powers should be swapped depending on the encounter.

CORE POWERS

HEALING WORD

Your main heal-over-time. Keep it active across the party and extend its duration with healing Dailies and Encounter Powers.

DIVINE GLOW

Provides Divinity and, following the M32.5 changes, becomes a useful damage-support power for the group.

EXALTATION

Can boost a strong DPS player. Positioning matters because you need to ensure the intended player is the one receiving it.

BASTION OF HEALTH

Strong instant-healing option when Healing Word alone isn't enough. It can be used frequently when the group needs heavier direct healing.

DAILY POWERS

ANOINTED ARMY

Useful damage-support Daily. Use it at the beginning of the group's artifact call when the damage buff is wanted.

HALLOWED GROUND

Useful when you want its wider support effects and the cooldown reduction interaction from Sanctified Ground.

ANGEL OF LIFE

Healing-focused Daily option when maximum healing is more important than additional damage support.

SITUATIONAL POWERS

CLEANSING LIGHT

Bring it when the encounter requires a cleanse rather than stubbornly pretending mechanics don't exist.

ASTRAL SHIELD

Useful for group mitigation checks and heavy incoming damage, including situations where the tank needs additional protection.

GEAS

Provides additional mitigation by reducing the damage dealt by the affected target.

SUN BURST

Situational utility when pushing enemies is actually useful to the mechanic.

CLASS FEATURES

Class Features aren't completely fixed either. Expanded Faith can be useful, but there are encounters where additional mobility while channeling Divinity or extra personal mitigation is more valuable.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise boon choices that strengthen the healing stats your current build needs. Power, Outgoing Healing and your critical healing stats take priority over random defensive ratings you already have in abundance.

Once the core healing setup is comfortable, remaining choices can support Action Point generation, survivability and other useful utility.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

Healers don't have a rigid DPS-style rotation because incoming damage and mechanics change constantly. Think of Devout as a priority system instead.

STANDARD GROUP PLAY

  1. KEEP HEALING WORD ACTIVE Maintain the heal-over-time across the group before people actually need emergency healing.
  2. MANAGE DIVINITY Don't empty the bar simply because healing powers are available. Keep enough Divinity to respond to incoming damage.
  3. USE DIVINE GLOW Generate Divinity and provide useful damage support when the group is stable.
  4. USE EXALTATION Boost the intended DPS when the group is healthy enough that additional direct healing isn't required.
  5. BASTION WHEN NEEDED If somebody takes significant immediate damage, use Bastion of Health rather than waiting for Healing Word to slowly repair the disaster.
  6. PREPARE THE ARTIFACT CALL Make sure your support artifact, mount power and appropriate Daily are ready for the group's coordinated damage window.
  7. ANOINTED ARMY Use it at the start of the artifact call when the group wants the additional damage support.

HEAVY HEALING

  1. HEALING WORD Keep the party-wide HoT running.
  2. SWAP EXALTATION Replace Exaltation with Bastion of Health when additional instant healing is more important than DPS support.
  3. BASTION OF HEALTH Use direct healing to recover players from large incoming hits.
  4. ANGEL OF LIFE Switch to the healing-focused Daily when the encounter demands maximum healing rather than group damage support.
  5. USE MITIGATION Astral Shield or Geas can prevent damage before it becomes another health bar you have to repair.
DEVOUT IN ONE SENTENCE: Healing Word keeps everybody ticking, Bastion fixes the "OH FUCK" moments, and everything between those two is where you turn from a health-bar babysitter into an actual support player. 😂

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

EXALTATION VS BASTION

Use Exaltation when healing is comfortable and you can support a strong DPS. Switch it for Bastion when the group needs additional instant healing.

DIVINE GLOW VS BASTION

If the group must remain widely spread and Exaltation remains useful, Divine Glow can instead be replaced by Bastion.

ANOINTED ARMY VS HALLOWED GROUND

Anointed Army fits coordinated damage calls. Hallowed Ground becomes useful when its broader support and cooldown interaction are more valuable.

ANGEL OF LIFE

Use Angel of Life when raw healing matters more than squeezing additional damage support from the Devout.

MECHANIC-SPECIFIC POWERS

Cleansing Light, Astral Shield, Geas and Sun Burst exist for a reason. Swap powers to solve the encounter instead of trying to make one loadout do absolutely everything.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Devout is strongest when you stop thinking of it as "the person who heals" and start thinking of it as the person controlling how safely and efficiently the entire group can play.

If nobody is dying, don't keep throwing every resource into overhealing them. That's your opportunity to support damage, prepare the next mechanic and recover Divinity.

If everybody IS dying... perhaps stop buffing Dave for five seconds and heal the bastards. 😂
