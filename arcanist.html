ARCANIST

Lightning ◆ Control ◆ Close-Range AoE Chaos

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Arcanist is the lightning and arcane side of the Wizard and this setup is built around aggressive multi-target damage. It combines persistent area damage, control and lightning procs to chew through tightly grouped enemies.

Despite being a Wizard, this particular setup is not designed to spend the entire fight hiding at the other end of the room. Several of its strongest powers are centred on the caster, so positioning close enough to the pack is a major part of playing it properly.

Get into position, drop the damaging zones, control the pack and let the lightning do its thing.

PLAYSTYLE: Arcanist AoE works best when enemies are grouped. You need to be close enough to land Icy Terrain, Steal Time and Arcane Tempest effectively rather than firing everything into three different postcodes.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Build around the core offensive DPS stats. Your final distribution will depend on your current gear, companions, enchantments, insignias and group buffs, so use these as priorities rather than blindly stacking one stat.

POWER

One of the core damage stats and a priority when balancing the finished build.

CRITICAL STRIKE

Important for consistent damage and particularly useful alongside effects that trigger from critical hits.

CRITICAL SEVERITY

Works alongside Critical Strike to increase the value of your critical hits.

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

Extremely valuable for AoE damage. Nightmare Wizardry can help Arcanist gain Combat Advantage against groups rather than relying entirely on positioning.

ACCURACY

Still useful, but generally comes after getting the major offensive stats into a healthy position.

REMEMBER: Balance your toon while fully buffed in combat. Your standing character sheet does not tell the whole story once companion bonuses, consumables and combat effects start firing.

LOADOUT

GEAR

The original Arcanist setup predates Module 33, so treat its old equipment as a build foundation rather than a shopping list. The important part is preserving the offensive stat balance and bonuses that support the Arcanist playstyle while using the strongest current Wizard DPS equipment available to you.

WEAPONS

Use a current Wizard weapon set with a strong damage-oriented set bonus. Prioritise the bonus and finished stat balance over item level alone.

ARMOUR

Look for current DPS armour with reliable offensive effects that work during normal dungeon combat rather than bonuses that only look impressive on the character sheet.

RINGS

Use rings to support Power, Critical Strike, Critical Severity, Combat Advantage and Accuracy according to whichever stats your finished toon still needs.

SHIRT & TROUSERS

Current offensive pieces can replace the older recommendations without changing the basic Arcanist power setup.

CURRENT-GEAR RULE: This is one of the builds we're modernising. Keep the Arcanist mechanics and replace superseded equipment with Module 33 options as they become available.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Use enchantments and reinforcement kits to finish the stats your current gear leaves short. There is no benefit in blindly stacking ratings that are already effectively capped once combat buffs are active.

OFFENSIVE ENCHANTMENTS

Fill whichever offensive ratings are actually missing from the finished build.

REINFORCEMENTS

Use kits and jewels as fine tuning. They are useful for correcting a stat imbalance without changing an otherwise strong gear piece.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Use a strong current DPS or group-debuff artifact as your primary artifact and use the remaining artifact slots to support the overall stat balance.

In organised groups, artifact calls are more valuable when the party coordinates them. Drop your encounters into the group's debuff and artifact window rather than firing the artifact randomly between packs.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Companion choices should support personal damage, useful offensive stats and group debuffs. The exact combination can change as gear changes, so use companion bonuses to finish the build rather than treating them as five permanently locked slots.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Use a strong current damage companion that performs well in the content you are running.

OFFENSIVE BONUSES

Prioritise bonuses that increase useful damage or complete Power, Critical Strike, Critical Severity, Combat Advantage and Accuracy.

GROUP DEBUFFS

In coordinated content, companion enhancements that reduce enemy defensive stats can improve the damage of the whole party.

COMPANION GEAR

Use companion gear to correct your remaining offensive ratings. Check the finished combat stats before deciding which pieces are best.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mount choices should provide a useful combat power, the stats your toon needs and insignia bonuses that contribute to damage, cooldowns or movement.

INSIGNIA BONUS OPTIONS

  • Artificer's Persuasion — casting your artifact reduces remaining cooldowns.
  • Tactician's Precision — using a Daily reduces remaining encounter cooldowns.
  • Gladiator's Guile — useful movement speed and stamina regeneration.
  • Warlord's Motivation — increases the damage of your summoned companion.
  • Executioner's Covenant — trades defensive ratings for offensive ratings.

Insignia bonuses are another balancing tool. Pick combinations that support your actual setup rather than forcing a bonus that sends an already-capped stat even higher.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

This Arcanist setup is primarily an AoE build. Its encounters work together at close range, combining persistent damage, control and lightning attacks.

AT-WILLS

RAY OF FROST

Useful single-target at-will that applies Chill while encounters are unavailable.

STORM PILLAR

The main lightning-flavoured AoE at-will for the setup and works particularly well with the build's lightning-focused feat choices.

ENCOUNTERS

ICY TERRAIN

Persistent area damage centred around the Wizard. Drop it where the enemies are being grouped.

STEAL TIME

Large close-range AoE that damages and controls enemies around you.

ARCANE TEMPEST

Run on Spell Mastery in this setup. Its area is fairly small, so it works best when the pack is tightly grouped.

LIGHTNING BOLT

Adds another strong lightning attack to the AoE encounter sequence.

DAILIES

OPPRESSIVE FORCE

Good when you want immediate AoE damage and control without waiting for enemies to be pulled together.

ARCANE SINGULARITY

Useful when grouping enemies is more valuable than immediately dumping the damage.

CLASS FEATURES

EVOCATION

Supports the build's focus on area damage.

STORM SPELL

Adds additional lightning damage and fits naturally with the rapid-hitting AoE setup.

IMPORTANT FEATS

ICE LIGHTNING

A reliable damage choice for the setup, supporting Storm Pillar and Lightning Bolt.

NIGHTMARE WIZARDRY

Critical hits can give Combat Advantage against enemies, making this particularly valuable when fighting groups.

ELEMENTAL REINFORCEMENT

Provides a reliable damage increase when using Arcane, Cold or Lightning Wizard spells and fits naturally with this mixed elemental rotation.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise offensive boons that contribute to the finished DPS stat balance. Power, Critical Strike, Critical Severity, Combat Advantage and Accuracy are the main areas to consider.

Once the important offensive choices are covered, remaining boon points can go into survivability and utility. A dead Wizard does approximately fuck-all DPS.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

Arcanist AoE is less about memorising a twenty-button boss rotation and more about positioning properly, dropping your damaging zones and getting the whole pack inside them.

STANDARD AOE ENGAGE

  1. GET INTO RANGE Move close enough for your centred AoE powers to hit the whole group.
  2. ICY TERRAIN Drop the persistent Cold damage underneath the pack.
  3. STEAL TIME Hit the grouped enemies with AoE damage and control.
  4. ARCANE TEMPEST Use the Spell Mastery encounter while the enemies are grouped inside its smaller radius.
  5. LIGHTNING BOLT Follow with the lightning encounter for additional AoE damage.
  6. STORM PILLAR Use your at-will while encounters recharge and continue feeding the lightning side of the build.
  7. DAILY WHEN NEEDED Use Oppressive Force for immediate damage/control or Arcane Singularity when pulling the pack together is more useful.
POSITIONING MATTERS: Icy Terrain, Steal Time and Arcane Tempest all reward being close enough to the enemies. If Arcane Tempest hits two mobs while the other six are wandering around behind you, the rotation isn't the problem. 😂

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

ARCANE SINGULARITY VS OPPRESSIVE FORCE

Oppressive Force is the more immediate option when enemies are already grouped and you simply want them dead. Arcane Singularity becomes useful when controlling and pulling the pack together is the bigger priority.

ELEMENTAL REINFORCEMENT VS STEP ABOVE MASTERY

Elemental Reinforcement is the more reliable AoE option in this setup because the rotation naturally uses Cold, Arcane and Lightning spells. Step Above Mastery relies more heavily on maintaining Arcane stacks, which is less convenient during fast AoE packs.

STAYING AT RANGE

This particular setup deliberately plays fairly close to the enemies. If you strongly prefer a ranged Wizard playstyle, the power setup can be changed, but that becomes a different variation rather than simply moving backwards while using the same rotation.

CURRENT GEAR

Replace old equipment recommendations with current Module 33 gear as you acquire it. Preserve the useful stat balance and power mechanics rather than keeping an obsolete item simply because it appeared in the original build.
