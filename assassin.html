THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Assassin is the classic close-range Rogue DPS paragon: get behind the target, establish your bleeds, use Stealth intelligently and unload your hardest-hitting encounters during the best damage windows.

Single-target damage revolves heavily around maintaining Duelist's Flurry bleed stacks while fitting Wicked Reminder and your burst encounters around them. Against groups, the setup changes toward area damage with powers such as Path of the Blades, Blade Flurry and Smoke Bomb.

Assassin rewards active play. You are constantly watching your bleed stacks, cooldowns, Stealth and positioning rather than standing still hammering one button until the boss develops a restraining order.

THE BIG RULE: Keep your Duelist's Flurry bleeds running on bosses. Letting the stacks fall off and rebuilding them repeatedly throws away damage.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Assassin wants a balanced offensive stat sheet. Your exact ratings will change with gear, companions, insignias, consumables and group buffs, so always check the toon in combat rather than judging the build entirely from idle stats.

POWER

Core offensive stat and one of the main priorities when balancing the finished build.

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

Extremely important for Rogue damage. Positioning and party play make this a major part of the Assassin's damage output.

CRITICAL SEVERITY

Increases the value of your critical hits and remains one of the main offensive stats to build around.

CRITICAL STRIKE

Works alongside Critical Severity to keep your damage consistent.

ACCURACY

Useful once the other major offensive stats are in a strong position.

ABILITY SCORES

Strength and Dexterity are the traditional offensive focus for this Assassin foundation.

STAT CHECK: Group buffs can significantly change your final percentages. Don't waste equipment slots massively overcapping one stat while another useful offensive stat is starving.

LOADOUT

GEAR

The Assassin mechanical foundation comes from an older build, so its original gear list should not be treated as current Module 33 best-in-slot. Keep the useful Assassin mechanics and replace old equipment with current Rogue DPS pieces.

WEAPONS

Use a current Rogue weapon set with a strong DPS set bonus. Evaluate the bonus as well as the raw item level.

ARMOUR

Prioritise current offensive effects that remain active during the content you actually run.

RINGS

Use your ring slots to add useful damage effects and correct whichever offensive ratings the rest of the build leaves short.

SHIRT & TROUSERS

Current offensive pieces can replace the older recommendations without changing the Assassin rotation itself.

UPGRADE RULE: Higher item level is useful, but don't automatically throw away a strong damage bonus for a bigger number. Look at what the item actually does for the finished build.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments, armour kits and jewels should finish the offensive ratings your equipment does not already provide.

OFFENSIVE ENCHANTMENTS

Choose them according to your missing Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy.

REINFORCEMENTS

Use reinforcement kits and jewels to fine-tune the finished stat sheet without replacing otherwise strong equipment.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Use a strong current DPS or debuff artifact as your primary and use secondary artifacts to support your offensive stats and set bonuses.

For boss fights, coordinate artifact calls with the group whenever possible. Assassin has strong burst encounters, so stacking those attacks inside the party's artifact and debuff window is considerably better than pressing everything whenever it lights up.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Companion choices should support personal damage, useful offensive ratings and group damage where appropriate.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Use a strong current damage companion suited to the content you are running.

PLAYER BONUSES

Use companion bonuses to add useful damage effects or fill missing offensive stats.

GROUP SUPPORT

In organised groups, useful debuff companion effects can improve overall party damage as well as your own.

COMPANION GEAR

Balance companion equipment around the stats your finished Assassin actually needs.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mount combat powers should contribute meaningful damage or group debuffs. Mount equip powers and insignias can then be used to finish your offensive stat balance.

STRONG DPS INSIGNIA BONUS OPTIONS

  • Artificer's Persuasion — casting your artifact reduces remaining cooldowns.
  • Executioner's Covenant — trades defensive ratings for additional offensive ratings.
  • Assassin's Covenant — another offensive-rating option at the cost of defensive stats.
  • Tactician's Precision — using a Daily reduces your remaining encounter cooldowns.
  • Warlord's Motivation — increases the damage of your summoned companion.
  • Gladiator's Guile — useful movement speed and stamina regeneration for a melee DPS.
DON'T COPY BLINDLY: Insignia bonuses need to work with the rest of your toon. Use them to solve problems in the build rather than stacking five bonuses because somebody else's screenshot had them.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Assassin uses different encounter choices for bosses and groups. The single-target setup focuses on maintaining bleeds and delivering strong encounter hits, while AoE swaps toward powers capable of damaging an entire pack.

AT-WILLS

DUELIST'S FLURRY

Core single-target at-will. Its bleed is a major part of the boss rotation, so build and maintain the full bleed stack.

SINGLE-TARGET ENCOUNTERS

WICKED REMINDER

Fits into the boss rotation alongside your bleeds and burst encounters.

ASSASSINATE

Heavy single-target burst and one of the main boss-damage encounters.

LASHING BLADE

Strong single-target damage option and the sensible replacement for the obsolete Toxic Blades setup from the older build.

MULTI-TARGET ENCOUNTERS

PATH OF THE BLADES

Useful sustained AoE damage and one of the main encounter swaps when moving from bosses to packs.

BLADE FLURRY

Strong option for attacking grouped enemies and particularly useful when combined intelligently with Stealth.

SMOKE BOMB

Area damage and control for groups. The old Toxic Blades interaction is not part of this build.

DAZING STRIKE

Situational alternative when additional AoE damage or control fits the encounter better.

STEALTH

Stealth is not simply an emergency invisibility button. It changes how Rogue powers behave and should be deliberately incorporated into the rotation to strengthen the encounters that benefit most from it.

OLD-BUILD WARNING: An older version of this Assassin setup revolved around an abnormal Toxic Blades and Smoke Bomb interaction. That interaction was already expected to be fixed, so it is deliberately excluded here.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise boons that improve your useful offensive stats and damage. Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy should be balanced around the rest of the toon.

Once the meaningful offensive choices are covered, remaining points can go into survivability and utility. Assassin fights in melee range, so being able to survive long enough to stab things remains somewhat important.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

SINGLE-TARGET / BOSS

The important part of the boss rotation is establishing Duelist's Flurry bleeds quickly and then maintaining them while cycling your encounters.

  1. POSITION FOR COMBAT ADVANTAGE Get into the correct melee position before dumping your damage.
  2. DUELIST'S FLURRY Use two full Duelist's Flurry sequences to establish the full bleed stack.
  3. WICKED REMINDER Work Wicked Reminder into the encounter sequence once your bleeds are established.
  4. ASSASSINATE Deliver your heavy single-target burst during the damage window.
  5. LASHING BLADE Follow with your other major single-target encounter.
  6. MAINTAIN BLEEDS Return to Duelist's Flurry before the bleed stacks expire. Do not repeatedly rebuild them from nothing.
  7. REPEAT AROUND COOLDOWNS Cycle encounters while keeping the bleed running and save your strongest burst for useful artifact and group debuff windows.

MULTI-TARGET / TRASH

  1. GROUP THE PACK Let the tank establish the enemies before unloading everything.
  2. PATH OF THE BLADES Start the sustained AoE damage.
  3. STEALTH Use Stealth deliberately before the encounter you want to empower.
  4. BLADE FLURRY Hit the grouped enemies with your AoE attack.
  5. SMOKE BOMB Add further area damage and control.
  6. CLEAN UP Use your at-wills and returning encounters to finish anything unfortunate enough to still be alive.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

ASSASSINATE VS PATH OF THE BLADES

Assassinate belongs in the single-target setup where its heavy hit matters. Path of the Blades becomes more useful when fighting groups and you want damage spread across multiple enemies.

BLADE FLURRY

Blade Flurry is a strong AoE option and can be used with Stealth to improve the multi-target setup.

DAZING STRIKE

Dazing Strike is another situational AoE option if it fits the pack or encounter better than your standard setup.

CURRENT EQUIPMENT

The original Assassin gear is old. Replace superseded pieces with current Module 33 Rogue DPS equipment while preserving the stat balance and core Assassin rotation.
