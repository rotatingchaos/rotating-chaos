SOULWEAVER

Healing ◆ Soulweave ◆ Dark Support Magic

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Soulweaver is the Warlock's dedicated healer path, built around maintaining healing-over-time effects, managing Soulweave and responding to incoming damage before the party turns into five corpses arguing about whose fault it was.

Unlike a purely reactive healer, Soulweaver works best when its healing is already established before the damage arrives. Keep the group covered, maintain your resources and use your larger heals when mechanics actually require them rather than panic-spamming everything at once.

Once the group's healing needs are under control, the build can also contribute useful support to the party instead of stacking more healing simply for the sake of producing bigger green numbers.

SOULWEAVER RULE: Prevent the emergency where possible. Fix the emergency when the DPS inevitably stands in the giant glowing circle anyway. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Soulweaver wants enough healing strength to comfortably handle the content without sacrificing every other useful part of the build.

1 ◆ POWER One of the main foundations of your healing output.
2 ◆ OUTGOING HEALING Directly supports the job you're here to do: keeping everyone alive.
3 ◆ CRITICAL STRIKE Important for stronger critical heals and should be balanced alongside Critical Severity.
4 ◆ CRITICAL SEVERITY Improves the strength of your critical healing. Don't overfill it at the expense of more useful percentages.
5 ◆ RECHARGE / AP Useful once your core healing percentages are healthy, improving access to encounters and Dailies.
6 ◆ SURVIVABILITY A dead healer has remarkably poor healing output. Add defensive stats where the content demands them.

LOADOUT

GEAR

Build around current healing and support gear rather than chasing item level alone. Once your healing output comfortably covers the mechanics, pieces that improve party performance become increasingly valuable.

HEAD

Prioritise a useful healing or party-support effect with ratings that complement the rest of the build.

ARMOUR

Look for reliable healing, support or survivability rather than effects that only look good on a character sheet.

ARMS

Current healing or support arms are preferred. Keep useful scaled alternatives available where their bonuses outperform raw item level.

FEET

Use the slot to complete healing percentages, support the group or add survivability where required.

WEAPONS

Current artifact weapons are the endgame target, with recent alternatives perfectly usable while progressing.

NECK & WAIST

Choose a set that supports healing, cooldowns or party utility rather than automatically selecting the highest item level.

RINGS

Excellent slots for finishing Power, healing and critical percentages or adding useful support effects.

SHIRT & TROUSERS

Fill remaining stat gaps and prioritise bonuses that remain useful during actual group content.

DON'T BUILD FOR OVERHEALING: Once everyone survives comfortably, another support bonus can be worth more than healing people for health they don't actually have.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Use enchantments to complete whichever percentages your gear leaves short. There is no reason to blindly duplicate somebody else's exact enchantment spread when your equipment is different.

  • Power where required
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Defensive ratings where survivability needs improving
  • Recharge where encounter availability is the priority
  • Action Point gain where stronger Daily uptime is more valuable

Reinforcement kits and jewels should follow the same principle: inspect the finished percentages and patch the gaps.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

A healer's primary artifact does not need to be selfish. In organised groups, a support or debuff artifact used during the party's artifact call can improve everyone's damage while your normal healing toolkit continues doing its job.

PRIMARY

Use a current group-support artifact appropriate to the party and coordinate it with the other support players.

SECONDARIES

Choose artifacts for useful healing, Power, critical percentages, survivability and set bonuses.

GROUP FIRST: Don't duplicate a debuff somebody else is already supplying if a different artifact gives the party something it doesn't have.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Soulweaver can use the companion system to increase personal healing, improve survivability or contribute more directly to group damage. The best choice depends on how much healing the content actually requires.

SUMMONED COMPANION

A current support companion is ideal for organised endgame groups. Pure healing companions are an option when additional recovery is genuinely needed.

PLAYER BONUSES

Prioritise healing percentages and Power first, then use remaining slots for critical healing, support or survivability.

COMPANION GEAR

Use companion gear to finish ratings without pushing ratings unnecessarily beyond what the build can use.

ENHANCEMENT

Group debuff enhancements are excellent when coordinated with the rest of the party. Avoid duplicating the same effect where it won't stack.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mount choices should reinforce the same priorities as the rest of the build: reliable healing, support, resource availability and enough survivability to remain upright during mechanics.

COMBAT POWER

Use a useful current support power for organised groups, or a defensive/healing option when the encounter specifically needs it.

INSIGNIAS

Balance insignias around the ratings your finished build still needs rather than chasing a fixed pattern.

COOLDOWN SUPPORT

Insignia bonuses that improve cooldown availability can be very useful when your important healing encounters need higher uptime.

MOVEMENT

Movement remains useful for a healer. Getting into position before the mechanic lands is considerably better than healing everybody from the floor.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Soulweaver revolves around proactive healing, Soulweave management and choosing the right tool for the damage pattern rather than following a rigid DPS-style rotation.

CORE HEALING

SOULSTORM

A major part of the healing-over-time playstyle. Place it where the party can actually benefit from it and use it proactively around predictable group damage.

REVITALIZE

Reliable direct healing for recovering players when passive healing isn't enough.

PILLAR OF POWER

Useful group utility when the party can remain positioned to benefit from it.

RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

Soulweave is not infinite. Maintain enough resource to answer the next mechanic rather than repeatedly spending it because somebody lost three percent of their health.

HEALING-OVER-TIME

The HoT setup is strongest when applied before predictable incoming damage. Establish the healing, maintain it and then layer direct healing on top only when necessary.

FEATS & CLASS FEATURES

Prioritise choices that strengthen the HoT-focused healing style, improve Soulweave management and support reliable group recovery. Situational options can be swapped when an encounter demands stronger emergency healing or additional utility.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Build boons around healing first, then resource availability and survivability.

  • Power
  • Outgoing Healing
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Recharge Speed
  • Action Point Gain
  • Maximum Hit Points and defensive options where needed

Once your healing comfortably handles the content, extra utility and survivability can provide more practical value than continually increasing overhealing.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

HEALING PRIORITY

Soulweaver is better treated as a priority system than a fixed rotation. What you cast next depends on what the encounter is about to do.

STANDARD GROUP FLOW

  1. ESTABLISH YOUR HOT Get the group's ongoing healing working before significant incoming damage begins.
  2. POSITION SOULSTORM Place it where the group will actually fight rather than where they were standing five seconds ago.
  3. MAINTAIN SOULWEAVE Don't exhaust the resource healing damage that your existing HoTs will comfortably recover.
  4. USE REVITALIZE WHEN NEEDED Layer direct healing onto players who need immediate recovery.
  5. SUPPORT THE GROUP Use Pillar of Power and your support tools when healing pressure allows.
  6. PREPARE FOR THE NEXT MECHANIC Re-establish HoTs and resources before the next major damage event rather than reacting after everybody gets smashed.

HEAVY DAMAGE

  1. PRE-HOT Get healing-over-time running before the mechanic lands.
  2. SOULSTORM Establish the healing area around the group.
  3. DIRECT HEALING Use Revitalize to recover anyone dropping faster than the HoTs can handle.
  4. DAILY / EMERGENCY TOOL Layer stronger recovery over the existing healing when the encounter requires it.
  5. RECOVER RESOURCES Once the danger passes, rebuild rather than continuing to dump Soulweave into already-full health bars.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

MAX HEALING

Lean harder into healing percentages for progression groups or encounters with extremely high incoming damage.

GROUP SUPPORT

Once healing is comfortable, exchange unnecessary overhealing for gear, companions and artifacts that increase party performance.

RECHARGE

Increase Recharge when encounter availability is proving more useful than additional raw healing.

AP GAIN

Increase Action Point generation when the encounter benefits more from frequent Daily usage.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

This is the HoT-style Soulweaver setup: establish the healing before people need it, keep Soulstorm somewhere useful and don't burn the entire Soulweave bar because Dave stood in something stupid for half a second. 😂

The better the group becomes, the less you need to build exclusively for maximum healing. If everybody is already surviving, helping the party kill the boss faster is also healer support.

And if everything suddenly goes completely to shit: forget the elegant support plan, keep the tank alive, recover the group and resume pretending the run was under control the entire time.
