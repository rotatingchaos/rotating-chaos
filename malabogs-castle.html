THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Malabog's Castle is mostly about add control, positioning and reacting quickly to Valindra during the final encounter.

The bosses themselves are fairly straightforward, but ignored adds can quickly overwhelm the group.

BOSS ONE

CROLL NIGHTFORGE

Croll begins the fight with several Redcap enemies nearby.

Additional adds spawn throughout the encounter.

Keep the adds grouped and use AoE damage to clear them quickly.

SHAMANS

Shamans can appear among the adds and should be prioritised.

Do not allow enemy waves to build up around the healer or damage dealers.

BOSS TWO

MALABOG

Malabog begins the fight with multiple adds.

Keep them controlled and clear them before they overwhelm the group.

IMMUNITY PHASE

Malabog periodically becomes immune to damage.

When this happens, stop attacking him and focus the spawned enemies instead.

WITCHES

Witches can polymorph players and should be killed quickly.

Other summoned enemies can create additional skeletons if left alive.

Kill priority adds before returning damage to Malabog.

TRANSITION

DRAGON ENCOUNTER

After Malabog, the group encounters the dragon before the final fight.

You do not need to kill it during this section.

Deal enough damage to force the dragon to retreat.

Once it flies away, continue toward the final encounter.

FINAL BOSS

FULMINAX

Fulminax is the main target during the final fight.

Tanks should keep the dragon facing away from the group.

DPS should avoid standing directly behind the dragon.

Fulminax uses a tail swipe that can knock players away.

Position around the sides where possible rather than directly in front or behind.

LIGHTNING BREATH

Fulminax uses a frontal lightning breath attack.

Move out of the red telegraph before it fires.

Tanks can block or mitigate the attack if necessary.

Keep the breath directed away from the rest of the group.

VALINDRA

Valindra appears at health thresholds during the dragon fight.

When she appears, switch damage to her immediately.

She can target and drain a player.

Damage Valindra quickly to force her away and free the affected player.

Once Valindra retreats, return damage to Fulminax.

ACTION POINT DRAIN

Large red areas appear during the fight.

Move out immediately.

Standing inside drains Action Points and leaves the player exposed to additional damage.

Later versions of the mechanic can remain active longer, requiring continued movement until the area disappears.

VALINDRA WINDOWS

Around 75% Fulminax health, the dragon phases and Valindra becomes active.

Move out of the draining red area and damage Valindra enough to end the phase.

Fulminax then returns and normal damage resumes.

Similar Valindra phases repeat later in the fight.

Near approximately 25% dragon health, the red-area phase lasts longer.

Continue moving until the danger zone resolves, then return to the dragon.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Keep large enemy packs under control throughout the dungeon.

Keep Fulminax facing away from the group.

Block or move out of Lightning Breath.

HEALER

Watch players who are surrounded by adds.

Be ready for heavy damage during Valindra drain mechanics.

Keep players alive while the group switches targets.

DPS

Clear priority adds quickly.

Kill witches and summoning enemies before they create additional problems.

Switch immediately to Valindra when she appears.

Avoid standing directly behind Fulminax.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Allowing Croll's add waves to build up instead of clearing them.

Ignoring Shamans among the first-boss adds.

Continuing to attack Malabog while he is immune.

Ignoring Witches and allowing players to be polymorphed.

Leaving summoning enemies alive long enough to create additional skeletons.

Standing directly behind Fulminax and being hit by the tail swipe.

Standing in front of Fulminax's Lightning Breath.

Continuing to attack Fulminax when Valindra appears.

Failing to damage Valindra quickly enough while she drains a player.

Standing inside the Action Point draining red area.

Stopping too early during the longer late-fight red-area phase.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Most of this dungeon is add management. Tank gathers them. DPS murders them. Everyone resists the urge to create seventeen separate fights.

Malabog immune? Stop hitting him. His health bar has quite literally informed you that your services are no longer required.

Witches polymorph people. Therefore witches die first. Nobody queued for this dungeon to spend the boss fight unexpectedly becoming livestock.

At Fulminax, front contains lightning breath and back contains angry dragon tail. The sides are your friends.

Valindra appears? Everyone switches to Valindra. She leaves? Back to dragon. This is not a complicated relationship.
