THE IMPORTANT BIT: Whisperknife isn't "stand somewhere vaguely nearby and throw knives." Positioning, Stealth timing and the artifact window matter enormously.

STAT PRIORITIES

Build around your actual combat percentages, not whichever number looks prettiest on the character sheet.

1 ◆ DAMAGE & MAGNITUDE

Strong damage bonuses can outperform raw item level, particularly in scaled content.

2 ◆ POWER

Core offensive percentage.

3 ◆ COMBAT ADVANTAGE

Extremely important. Flank whenever possible and keep it active.

4 ◆ CRITICAL STRIKE

Build toward 90% total alongside Critical Severity.

5 ◆ CRITICAL SEVERITY

Build alongside Critical Strike and continue toward 120%.

6 ◆ ACCURACY

Useful, but the lowest offensive priority.

ABILITY SCORES: Strength + Dexterity. If Critical Severity is already pushing beyond 120%, move some Dexterity into Charisma instead.

HOW IT WORKS

WHISPERKNIFE EXPLAINED

Whisperknife is a fast Rogue DPS path built around Stealth, positional damage, damage-over-time effects and explosive burst during coordinated artifact calls.

Single target revolves around maintaining Disheartening Strike, keeping Combat Advantage active and combining Stealth with Shadow Strike, Lashing Blade and Impact Shot.

Multi target changes the rhythm completely. Blade Flurry, Smoke Bomb and Path of the Blade take you directly into the enemy pack for close-range AoE.

SINGLE TARGET

DISHEARTENING STRIKE

Maintain Disheartening Strike's damage-over-time effect and damage buff by refreshing it roughly every ten seconds.

It is easy to focus on the larger encounter hits and forget the maintenance power, but the rest of the burst performs better when this effect is already established.

POSITIONING

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

Flank the boss whenever possible so Combat Advantage remains active.

Dagger Threat is useful when normal boss flanking can be maintained. Swap to Advantageous Position when mechanics or spawned enemies make reliable flanking difficult.

Get into position before entering Stealth rather than spending part of the burst window running around the boss.

STEALTH

SINGLE-TARGET BURST

Enter Stealth and immediately use Shadow Strike. Follow with Lashing Blade and then Impact Shot.

Cloud of Steel fills the downtime while encounters recover and continues generating Action Points.

Smoke Bomb can replace Impact Shot when the boss encounter contains adds or additional crowd control and multi-target damage are useful.

MULTI TARGET

BLADE FLURRY

Enter the enemy pack, activate Stealth and use Blade Flurry once.

Follow with Smoke Bomb and Path of the Blade, then keep the second Blade Flurry available until Stealth returns.

Spending both Blade Flurry charges immediately loses the stronger interaction available when the second charge is paired with Stealth.

BURST

THE ARTIFACT WINDOW

Whisperknife's damage increases dramatically during the party's coordinated artifact window.

Enter the call with encounters ready and Combat Advantage active, then stack the strongest attacks inside the roughly ten-second support window.

Cavalry's Haste and Tactician's Precision are important to the full cooldown sequence. Without them, perform the opening burst through Hateful Knives and use Cloud of Steel until the encounters recover.

GEAR

SCALED CONTENT & SWAPS

Don't assume the newest or highest-item-level piece automatically wins, particularly in scaled content.

Astral Raider's Gloves remain extremely strong when item level is scaled down and can outperform newer higher-item-level arms.

Bindings of the Death Pact from Master Shackles of Divinity are the preferred multi-target arms.

Voidtouched and Xaryxian weapons remain strong alternatives and can perform extremely well in scaled single-target fights.

SURVIVAL

CELESTIAL BOOTS

Celestial boots can provide a large damage bonus, but the tradeoff is 20% maximum health.

While learning difficult content, a safer option can be more useful than turning every mechanic into an unexpected meeting with the floor.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Whisperknife has a wonderfully Rogue approach to AoE: walk directly into the middle of everybody, disappear, then start throwing sharp objects around. 🤣

On bosses, don't underestimate Disheartening Strike. Refreshing it every ten seconds and maintaining Combat Advantage aren't the glamorous bits, but they're part of what makes the flashy burst actually work.

And the artifact call matters massively. This isn't the toon where you can miss the call, throw a few knives afterwards and go "eh, close enough."

Your ten-second murder window is where a ridiculous chunk of the damage lives. 😂
