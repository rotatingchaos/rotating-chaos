TOMB OF THE NINE GODS

Rotating Chaos Neverwinter Dungeon Guide

THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Tomb of the Nine Gods combines environmental puzzles, traps and three major boss encounters.

The dungeon begins with several puzzle rooms before moving into encounters built around role-specific buffs, environmental hazards and damage checks.

The final encounter against Ras Nsi requires the group to manage positioning, adds and Soulmonger Charge while avoiding being thrown from the platform.

BEFORE THE BOSSES

TOMB PUZZLES

FINDING THE FIRST PATH

At the beginning of the dungeon there are three possible pathways.

Look for the stone mask on the wall. The pathway marked by the stone mask is the correct route.

TRICKSTER GODS SARCOPHAGUS

In the large hall, inspect the symbol at the base of the central sarcophagus.

Memorise the symbol and move in that direction.

Interact with the sarcophagus displaying the matching symbol.

Repeat the process for all three symbols.

Once the third correct sarcophagus is activated, the exit opens.

GHOST CORRIDOR

Ghosts beyond the rotating doorway can stun players and drain health over time.

The tank should ideally enter first while DPS kill the ghosts quickly.

POTION & ANIMAL PUZZLE

One player drinks one of the coloured potions.

The potion transforms that player into an animal, but the transformed player needs their teammates to identify which animal or colour they have become.

The transformed player then stands on the matching animal platform.

Repeat the process until all potions have been used.

SKIPPING THE PUZZLE

The group can instead pass through the doorway and trigger a trap.

Possible trap sequences include rooms containing enemies, hidden teleporting masks or a collapsed ceiling.

ARTIFACT SEQUENCE PUZZLE

Six artifacts appear on pedestals and must be activated in the correct order.

Artifacts marked Hidden come first in the sequence.

The Manipulate artifacts are the final two.

Determine the order of the four Hidden artifacts first, then determine the order of the two Manipulate artifacts.

HIDDEN MASK TRAP

Some trap rooms contain invisible masks.

Two of the three routes contain glowing masks that teleport players back to the beginning of the room.

Find the safe path through each room and repeat until the group reaches the exit.

COLLAPSED CEILING TRAP

The exit can become blocked by fallen stones.

Pick up each stone and carry it to the stone pile.

Movement is heavily reduced while carrying a stone.

Continue until enough debris has been removed to open the exit.

FIRST BOSS

CURSES ◆ ORBS ◆ ZOMBIES

Before entering the fight, the group must activate three curse pedestals.

Different players must take the different curses.

TEMPORARY HIT POINTS

The left pedestal grants a very large amount of temporary hit points.

The player carrying this effect cannot be healed.

This player has an important job during the boss fight: intercepting incoming orbs.

ZOMBIE DAMAGE

The middle pedestal increases damage against the zombies that spawn around the outside of the arena.

It also reduces damage against the boss.

The healer is a suitable choice for this curse because the group's primary DPS can remain focused on the boss.

BOSS DAMAGE

The right pedestal increases damage against the boss.

It also applies damage over time to the player carrying it.

The group's strongest DPS is a good choice for this effect, provided the healer keeps them alive.

RED CONE

The boss places a red cone attack on the arena.

Being hit can knock the player into the outer gutter, potentially causing nearby zombies to aggro.

Avoid the cone.

ZOMBIES

Zombies spawn around the outside of the arena.

Kill them if they are pulled into the fight.

ORBS

Orbs periodically spawn around the outside of the arena and travel toward party members.

The player carrying the temporary-hit-point curse must intercept these orbs.

If an orb reaches another player who does not have the temporary-hit-point protection, it can instantly kill them.

More orbs appear as the encounter progresses, so the designated interceptor must continually watch the arena.

SECOND BOSS

WITHERS' CONTROL STATION

The central objective is Withers' Control Station.

During the encounter, multiple environmental traps can activate around the arena.

CENTRAL RED AREA

A red area can appear around the control station.

Move out of it or be pushed away from the centre.

HAND ATTACK

A red area can target a player before a large hand strikes.

Non-tanks can be knocked over by the attack.

GRAB

A hand can grab a player.

The grabbed player cannot act and takes damage over time while restrained.

POISON GAS

The arena can fill with toxic gas, causing heavy damage over time.

WATER

Water can cover the arena floor.

This slows movement and drains stamina.

LIGHTNING

Lightning applies partial paralysis.

Players periodically become unable to act, which can interrupt actions such as reviving another player.

FLAMETHROWERS

Flamethrowers activate around the edge of the arena.

Their fire sweeps from side to side and deals heavy damage over time.

MULTIPLE TRAPS

Environmental effects can overlap, making the encounter considerably more dangerous.

DISABLING THE TRAPS

Tomb Dwarves appear in chambers around the arena when their corresponding trap activates.

Killing the appropriate dwarf disables that environmental effect.

The dwarf locations are:

Near Right ◆ Water

Near Left ◆ Fire

Far Right ◆ Toxic Gas

Far Left ◆ Lightning

If an environmental effect becomes dangerous, send players to kill the corresponding dwarf.

FINAL BOSS

RAS NSI

SPINNING SWORD

Ras Nsi charges his sword and performs a spinning attack.

Block or dodge the attack.

Being hit applies partial paralysis.

TELEPORT & ARMOR BREAK

Ras Nsi teleports to one of the four corners of the arena and creates a large red area.

Avoid the area.

Being hit applies Armor Break, increasing the damage the player receives.

OVERHEAD SWORD STRIKE

Ras Nsi's sword glows before he performs a powerful overhead strike.

The attack applies a damaging burn and can be lethal to DPS.

The attack is an AoE.

The tank should keep Ras Nsi facing away from the party so DPS remain behind the boss.

TILTING PLATFORM

A massive ghost appears at one of the long ends of the platform.

When the ghost appears, run toward the opposite side.

The ghost pushes down on its side of the platform, causing the entire arena to tilt.

Players who are too close to the ghost can slide off the edge.

SOULMONGER PHASE

White ghostly souls and skeletal spearmen spawn around the arena.

The tank should control the spearmen while DPS immediately focus the souls.

SOULMONGER CHARGE

Every soul that is not destroyed increases the Soulmonger Charge.

After the soul phase, Ras Nsi becomes untargetable and releases a large burst of damage.

If Soulmonger Charge reaches full, the burst wipes the group.

If the charge is not full, the group takes damage and the meter resets to zero.

Soul phases repeat throughout the fight.

SAVE DAMAGE FOR THE SOULS

The souls are the critical damage check.

DPS should keep enough Encounters and Dailies available to destroy them quickly when they appear.

Boss damage matters less than successfully killing the souls before Soulmonger Charge fills.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Enter dangerous ghost corridors first where possible.

During the first boss, the temporary-hit-point curse is well suited to a tank who can intercept incoming orbs.

During Ras Nsi, keep his dangerous frontal attacks facing away from the party.

Control skeletal spearmen during Soulmonger phases so DPS can focus the souls.

HEALER

The zombie-damage curse during the first boss is a suitable option because it reduces boss damage output.

Keep the player carrying the boss-damage curse alive through its damage-over-time effect.

Be prepared for heavy environmental damage during Withers' Control Station, especially when several traps overlap.

DPS

The strongest DPS can take the boss-damage curse during the first encounter.

During Ras Nsi, prioritise the souls immediately.

Save enough burst damage for Soulmonger phases rather than using everything on the boss immediately before the souls appear.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Failing to intercept the first boss's orbs.

Standing in the red cone and being knocked into the outer gutter.

Allowing multiple Withers environmental traps to remain active until their effects overlap.

Standing in Ras Nsi's Armor Break area.

Allowing Ras Nsi's overhead sword strike to hit DPS.

Standing on the wrong side of the arena when the ghost tilts the platform.

DPS attacking spearmen instead of prioritising the souls.

Entering a Soulmonger phase without enough burst damage available.

Allowing Soulmonger Charge to reach full.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Tomb looks like a giant pile of puzzles, traps, ghosts, angry dwarves and questionable architectural decisions, but the boss mechanics are actually fairly clean once you know what matters.

First boss: assign the curses properly and intercept the bloody orbs.

Withers: if the room starts trying to murder everyone, kill the dwarf responsible for that particular flavour of bullshit.

Ras Nsi: keep him facing away, run away from the giant ghost, and when the souls spawn they become everyone's problem immediately.
