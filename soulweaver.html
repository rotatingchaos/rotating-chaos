◆ COMBAT ◆

POWERS & PLAYSTYLE

Soulweaver is proactive. Establish the healing before the damage lands instead of waiting for five health bars to disappear and then screaming.

SS

SOULSTORM

Summons a circle at the target location that heals party members standing within it over time. With Feypact, it fits directly into the build's proactive HoT playstyle.

REV

REVITALIZE

Heals allies at the target location, with magnitude reducing as more targets are affected. Feypact also adds a HoT effect.

POP

PILLAR OF POWER

Creates a conduit at the target location. Party members who enter gain 5% increased damage, making this one of the build's important group-support tools.

SOULSTORM: Put it where the party is actually going to fight, not where they were standing five seconds ago. 😂

CORE FEATS

The HoT setup and Soulweave economy are what make this build work.

FEYPACT

Vampiric Embrace, Revitalize, Soulstorm and Inspirit also apply healing-over-time effects. This is the backbone of the HoT setup.

ESSENCE OF TIME

Soulweave regeneration increases every three seconds and can stack up to four times. Spending Soulweave resets the effect, so don't chuck the entire bar at Dave for losing 3% HP. 😂

FROM THE BRINK

Healing is increased by 15% when healing allies below 25% of their Maximum HP. Useful when somebody genuinely is in trouble.

SOULTHEFT

When struck, regain 25 Soulweave. This can occur once every 10 seconds.

SOULWEAVE MANAGEMENT

The resource bar is there to be managed, not emptied because somebody stubbed their toe.

DON'T OVERHEAL

Existing HoTs can recover smaller amounts of damage. Save direct healing for when the group actually needs it.

PREP FOR MECHANICS

Enter predictable heavy-damage mechanics with enough Soulweave available to respond.

RECOVER AFTER BURST

Once the dangerous damage has passed, allow the resource bar to recover rather than continuing to spam heals into full health bars.

HOW IT WORKS

SOULWEAVER EXPLAINED

Soulweaver is built around maintaining healing-over-time effects, managing Soulweave and responding to incoming damage before the party turns into five corpses arguing about whose fault it was.

Unlike a purely reactive healer, Soulweaver works best when its healing is already established before the damage arrives.

Maintain the group's healing and your resources, then use larger direct recovery when mechanics actually require it rather than panic-spamming everything.

HEALING-OVER-TIME

HEAL BEFORE THEY NEED HEALING

The HoT setup is strongest when applied before predictable incoming damage.

Establish the healing, maintain it and layer direct healing over the top only when necessary.

Soulstorm is central to this style. Position it where the group can actually benefit from it rather than dropping it after everybody has already moved.

RESOURCE

SOULWEAVE MANAGEMENT

Soulweave is not infinite.

Maintain enough resource to answer the next mechanic rather than repeatedly spending it because somebody has taken trivial damage that the existing HoTs will comfortably recover.

Once heavy damage has passed, rebuild resources instead of continuing to dump healing into full health bars.

SUPPORT

HEALING ISN'T THE ONLY JOB

Once the group's healing needs are comfortably under control, Soulweaver can contribute useful party support instead of simply stacking larger green numbers.

Support artifacts, companion effects and group debuffs can help the party kill the boss faster while your normal healing toolkit continues doing its job.

For progression groups or encounters with extremely high incoming damage, lean further into healing. When healing is already comfortable, trade unnecessary overhealing for useful support.

FLEXIBILITY

RECHARGE ◆ AP ◆ SURVIVAL

Increase Recharge where encounter availability provides more practical value than additional raw healing.

Increase Action Point generation when the encounter benefits from more frequent Daily usage.

Defensive stats are not wasted if they keep you alive through mechanics. You cannot heal the party while admiring the floor.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

This is the HoT-style Soulweaver setup: establish the healing before people need it, keep Soulstorm somewhere useful and don't burn the entire Soulweave bar because Dave stood in something stupid for half a second. 😂

The better the group becomes, the less you need to build exclusively for maximum healing. If everybody is already surviving, helping the party kill the boss faster is also healer support.

And if everything suddenly goes completely to shit: forget the elegant support plan, keep the tank alive, recover the group and resume pretending the run was under control the entire time.
