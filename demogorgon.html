DEMOGORGON

Rotating Chaos Neverwinter Trial Guide

Overview Madness Phase One Phase Two Phase Three Role Jobs Common Wipes Chaos Notes

THE TRIAL

OVERVIEW

Demogorgon is a 10-player trial split into three phases.

Phase One focuses on closing Abyssal Tears while one tank keeps Demogorgon occupied.

Phase Two centres on Goristro and correctly directing his charge.

Phase Three is a direct fight against Demogorgon while avoiding dangerous portals and controlling Madness.

GLOBAL MECHANIC

MADNESS & SANITY WELLS

Players build Madness stacks while inside the encounter area.

As Madness increases, players begin taking damage.

At higher stacks, powers can become locked and unusable.

Enter a blue Sanity Well to remove Madness stacks.

Keep an eye on your Madness throughout the trial and cleanse it regularly rather than waiting until your powers are disabled.

PSYCHOTIC BREAK

Psychotic Break freezes the group temporarily.

This is particularly dangerous if Demogorgon is attacking players who cannot move.

FIRST PHASE

ABYSSAL TEARS

Demogorgon is invulnerable during this phase.

One tank should pull Demogorgon away from the group and keep him occupied near the edge of the arena.

PORTAL TYPES

Three sizes of Abyssal Tear can appear: green, blue and large purple.

Prioritise the large purple tears.

Closing a tear causes enemies to spawn from it. Kill those enemies before moving on.

LOW-DAMAGE GROUPS

Stay together when closing portals.

Do not open a second large purple tear until the demons from the first have been dealt with.

Opening too many tears at once can overwhelm the group.

AFTER THE PORTALS

Once the required tears have been dealt with, return to a Sanity Well and control Madness until the phase timer ends.

SECOND PHASE

GORISTRO

Phase Two is a fight against Goristro.

CHARGE

Goristro periodically targets a player with a red arrow.

He then charges directly toward that player.

Use positioning to make Goristro charge into the correct portal.

GOLD PORTALS

Charging Goristro into a gold portal damages him.

This is especially useful for lower-damage groups.

BLACK PORTALS

Do not allow Goristro to charge into a black portal.

A black portal restores a large amount of his health.

SANITY WELL BEAM

A purple beam can target a player with a warning to take shelter in a Sanity Well.

Move into a blue Sanity Well immediately.

Remaining outside causes very heavy damage and can kill the player.

FINAL PHASE

DEMOGORGON

The final phase is a direct fight against Demogorgon.

Stay behind him where possible and focus damage.

MANIACAL LAUGHTER

When Demogorgon laughs maniacally, the group can take heavy damage.

Be ready for incoming damage and avoid standing in additional hazards.

FLOOR PORTALS

Purple portals appear on the floor.

Do not walk into them.

Entering one teleports the player upward before dropping them, causing heavy fall damage.

CONTINUE MANAGING MADNESS

Keep using Sanity Wells when required.

Do not allow Madness to reach the point where your powers become locked during the final burn.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANKS

During Phase One, one tank should keep Demogorgon away from the group while everyone else handles Abyssal Tears.

Keep dangerous boss pressure away from players dealing with portals and Madness.

HEALERS

Be prepared for Madness damage, Psychotic Break and unavoidable group pressure.

Watch players who delay cleansing Madness or fail to reach Sanity Wells.

DPS

Prioritise large purple Abyssal Tears during Phase One.

During Phase Two, help position Goristro toward gold portals and away from black portals.

In Phase Three, stay behind Demogorgon and avoid floor portals.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Allowing Madness to build until powers become disabled.

Opening too many Abyssal Tears at once and overwhelming the group.

Failing to keep Demogorgon away from the Phase One portal team.

Sending Goristro into a black portal and healing him.

Ignoring the Sanity Well warning during Phase Two.

Walking into purple floor portals during the final phase.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Demogorgon is much easier when the group remembers that each phase has one main job.

Phase One: shut the big purple tears and stop opening half the Underdark at once.

Phase Two: gold portal good, black portal very bloody bad.

Phase Three: hit Demogorgon, avoid floor portals and don't let Madness turn your powers into decorative buttons.
