RISE OF TIAMAT

Learn your gem. Cancel your spell. Keep the dragons from turning the arena into soup.

OVERVIEW

RISE OF TIAMAT

Rise of Tiamat is a five-phase trial built around coloured gems, matching spell effects, split-party coordination, dragon-head mechanics and heavy group-wide damage checks.

CORE MECHANIC

THE FIVE GEMS

  • There are five gem colours: Black, Green, Red, Blue and White.
  • Each player should ideally carry a different gem within their party.
  • Gem colours correspond to matching dragon spell effects.
  • When a player receives a spell effect, the matching gem carrier can cancel it.
  • Gem use creates an area. Any player inside that area with the matching spell has the effect removed.

COLOUR SPELLS

SPELL EFFECTS

  • Black: creates Vile Swarm, causing heavy damage over time and dazing players.
  • Green: applies a lighter damage effect and drains Action Points.
  • Red: creates Eruption, leaving a pulsing damaging area on the ground.
  • Blue: creates a shared-damage Hypothermia-style mechanic. Group around the affected player if it is not cancelled.
  • White: creates a large personal area. Other players should move away from the target.
  • Cancelling the spell quickly is always preferable to allowing its full effect to trigger.

PHASE ONE

SEVERIN

  • Watch for rune-like attacks that follow players across the small platform.
  • Move continuously to avoid being struck or knocked toward the centre.
  • Severin uses a frontal cone on the tank.
  • DPS and healers should stay behind him rather than standing with the tank.
  • Every time Severin returns from a dragon phase, he creates a knockback bomb.
  • Move away from the bomb so it does not launch you toward the centre.

PHASE ONE

DRAGON BREATHS

  • Severin periodically disappears and one of Tiamat's dragon heads attacks.
  • These breath attacks cannot be cancelled with gems.
  • The group must survive them through positioning, tank mitigation and healing.
  • Black: one massive heavy hit.
  • Green: heavy damage over time with strong pushback.
  • Red: heavy damage over time with strong pushback.
  • Blue: damage becomes worse when players are tightly grouped.
  • White: pushes players and makes movement slippery.

PHASE TWO

DEFEND THE CLERICS

  • Split into two five-player parties.
  • Each group defends its own cleric.
  • Enemy waves spawn roughly every few seconds and alternate sides.
  • Keep the adds away from the cleric.
  • Damage taken by the cleric reduces their progress bar.
  • The phase ends when both cleric bars are filled.

PHASE TWO

MINI BOSSES

  • Each side also has a dangerous mini boss.
  • Tanks should hold the mini boss away from the cleric and the DPS.
  • DPS should not waste time trying to kill it.
  • Avoid standing in front of the mini boss or inside its red attacks.
  • If the tank dies, the mini boss can quickly kill the rest of the party.

PHASE TWO

CANCEL THE SPELLS

  • Dragon eyes glow before their corresponding spell appears.
  • Use the glowing head colour as an early warning.
  • Players have only a short window to cancel a spell before it triggers.
  • Multiple players can receive the same spell.
  • Group matching spell targets together so one gem activation can clear several at once.
  • Cancelling spells is a top priority.

FLOOR HAZARD

DRAGON BREATH ZONES

  • Dragon heads periodically breathe onto sections of the arena.
  • This creates large persistent red danger zones.
  • Gems do not remove these zones.
  • Stay out of them completely.

PHASE THREE

TIAMAT'S CLAWS

  • Each party attacks one claw.
  • The two claws must die almost simultaneously.
  • Aim to kill them within only a few seconds of each other.
  • If one claw dies too early, it can respawn at full health.
  • Heavy damage over time affects the group during this phase.
  • Healers should enter the phase with strong resources available.
  • Tanks continue controlling the mini bosses away from the DPS.

PHASE FOUR

DRAGON HEADS

  • The two parties split between active dragon heads.
  • Do not leave an active head unattended.
  • Unattended heads create increasing numbers of dangerous red ground attacks.
  • Tanks should stay close to their assigned head.
  • If the tank moves too far away, additional red bombs can appear.
  • Each active head can also trigger its matching coloured spells.
  • The relevant gem carriers must be ready to cancel them.

DRAGON HEADS

RED ATTACKS & BREATH

  • Red ground attacks from the heads can deal lethal damage.
  • Dodge them carefully rather than attempting to tank them.
  • Overlapping red attacks can kill a player instantly.
  • Dragon breath attacks require strong healing and tank mitigation.
  • Continue killing active heads one by one until all five are defeated.

FINAL PHASE

TIAMAT

  • Both parties reunite for the final burn.
  • Two mini bosses spawn again and should be controlled by the tanks.
  • DPS should focus Tiamat rather than the mini bosses.
  • Group-wide damage over time begins and increases as the phase continues.
  • Remaining gem carriers must continue cancelling their assigned spells.
  • Avoid all mini-boss red attacks while maintaining damage on Tiamat.
  • Burn Tiamat before the increasing damage overwhelms the healers.

QUICK REFERENCE

TIAMAT SURVIVAL NOTES

  • Know your gem colour.
  • Matching spell appears → gem carrier cancels it.
  • Dragon eyes glow → matching spell incoming.
  • Black spell → cancel immediately.
  • Blue spell not cancelled → stack and share.
  • White spell → everyone else move away.
  • Red spell → avoid the eruption zone.
  • Dragon breath zones → stay out.
  • Cleric phase → keep adds away from cleric.
  • Mini boss → tank it, don't waste DPS on it.
  • Claws → kill both almost together.
  • Dragon heads → never leave one unattended.
  • Final phase → tanks hold minis, DPS burn Tiamat.
  • Increasing final DoT → finish the bloody dragon quickly.
