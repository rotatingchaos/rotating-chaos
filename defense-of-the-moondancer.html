DEFENSE OF THE MOONDANCER

Rotating Chaos Neverwinter Trial Guide

Overview Phase One Lieutenants Phase Three Air Bleeders Captain Role Jobs Common Wipes Chaos Notes

THE TRIAL

OVERVIEW

Defense of the Moondancer is a timed trial built around preserving the ship's remaining air while clearing enemy waves, handling Astral Elf Lieutenants and destroying Air Bleeders before the group is overwhelmed.

The encounter is divided into five phases, ending with Captain Zoor’Lar. Speed matters throughout because the Air Remaining tracker acts as an overall timer.

FIRST PHASE

OPENING MOB WAVES

Clear the opening waves of Astral Elves as quickly as possible.

The Air Remaining tracker in the top-right of the screen is the group's overall survival timer. If it reaches 0%, the encounter is effectively lost.

Two tanks can split the room, with one covering the entrance side and the other covering the opposite side, collecting enemies as they spawn.

If the group clears this section quickly enough, Artifacts and Dailies can be saved for the Lieutenant phase.

SECOND PHASE

LIEUTENANTS

Six unique Lieutenants exist, but only three appear during this phase. They generally appear in a random order.

Mishandling their mechanics can apply permanent Starfire debuff stacks, increasing the damage taken by affected players.

LIEUTENANT AGENT

Lightning Bomb creates a purple lightning zone. Standing in it when it appears can stun and heavily damage players.

The boss also uses Dancing Lights, Lightning Bolt and Fire Ball against the player holding the highest threat.

LIEUTENANT AURA

Starburn Aura creates a blue sphere around the boss. Players inside take damage over time, but players outside deal significantly reduced damage.

Sun Burst creates a large red area centred on the boss and should be avoided.

Aura also uses Starlight Beam, Lance of Faith and Flame Strike against the highest-threat player.

LIEUTENANT SNIPER

Energy Beam is the major mechanic. The Sniper charges the attack and a glowing wing appears behind him.

The highest-threat player can either break line of sight using a pillar, or the group can stack together and share the damage with mitigation.

The Sniper commonly uses Energy Beam twice in succession before entering a longer cooldown.

LIEUTENANT STRIKER

Lunge must be blocked or the target is dazed for a long period.

Lunge is followed by Thunderous Smash, where the Striker repeatedly pounds the ground and deals heavy area damage for several seconds.

Melee players should move away during the Smash.

LIEUTENANT DOCTOR

Healing Beam connects the Doctor to nearby allies and repeatedly heals them.

The Doctor can restore very large amounts of health to nearby Lieutenants, making him a priority target when grouped with others.

He also uses Sun Burst, Starlight Beam and Lance of Faith.

LIEUTENANT SABOTEUR

Paralysis Trap disables players who enter it and deals heavy damage over time.

Bomb Explosion creates barrels around the arena that can later explode, dealing heavy damage and applying a Starfire debuff.

The Saboteur also uses Whirlwind, Hacking Frenzy and Deft Strike.

CURRENT STRATEGY

Use Artifacts on the first Lieutenant and avoid aggroing the other two immediately.

After the first Lieutenant dies, move to the right side and engage the Lieutenant at the back. Leave the remaining middle-left Lieutenant alone until the second is dead.

Once all three are defeated, clear the remaining enemies and move on. Remember that the air timer is still running throughout.

THIRD PHASE

SECOND MOB PHASE

Clear the next waves of enemies until no more appear and the group can move toward the upper deck of the ship.

Continue moving quickly because the Air Remaining timer does not stop.

FOURTH PHASE

AIR BLEEDERS

Three Air Bleeders must be destroyed before the ship runs out of air.

Air Bleeders lose significantly more progress when three players stand inside their area. With three players, they lose approximately 7% per tick, compared with around 1% when only one player is present.

One Air Bleeder is located in the middle of the ship, one at the front and one at the back.

The remaining Lieutenants also appear during this phase and must have their health reduced enough for them to disappear.

CURRENT STRATEGY

Stay together and move toward the first Air Bleeder without prematurely aggroing the central adds.

Destroy each Air Bleeder with at least three players standing in its area.

Use coordinated Artifact calls to kill the Lieutenants quickly.

The main priority is destroying all three Air Bleeders so the air-loss damage does not begin.

Once the final Air Bleeder is destroyed, another timer begins, so the group must finish the remaining Lieutenants and move to the enemy ship quickly.

FINAL PHASE

CAPTAIN ZOOR’LAR

The final encounter combines Captain Zoor’Lar, all six Lieutenants and additional enemy waves.

HEALTH PHASES

At approximately 80% health, one or two Lieutenants appear.

At 60%, another one or two Lieutenants appear.

At 50%, the Captain becomes immune while mobs spawn and cannons bombard the arena.

Additional Lieutenant waves appear around 40% and 20%.

At approximately 15%, the Captain begins a damage-over-time effect that steadily increases.

TANKBUSTER

Roughly every 35 seconds, the Captain targets the player with the highest threat.

He first raises his sword and performs a lunge. This must be blocked or the target becomes incapacitated.

He then performs Disintegration Blade.

Being struck while incapacitated can result in death.

Surviving the Tankbuster applies a debuff lasting around 40 seconds. Taking another Tankbuster while that debuff is still active results in instant death regardless of mitigation.

The two tanks therefore need to swap responsibility so the same tank does not repeatedly take the Tankbuster.

Disintegration Blade is also a cone attack, so the tank taking it must face away from the rest of the group.

CURRENT STRATEGY

Save Artifacts for the first Lieutenant wave and initially chip the Captain down toward 80%.

Prioritise dangerous Lieutenants, especially the Sniper.

During the Sniper's Energy Beam in this arena, the group can stack together and share the damage.

Tanks should alternate between holding the Captain and handling the Lieutenants/adds, switching after Tankbusters.

Fight the Captain near the edge to avoid accidentally pushing him through health thresholds before the group is ready.

At around 20% health, stop damaging the Captain and clear the remaining Lieutenants and mobs.

Once Artifacts are ready, push the Captain to 15% and burst him down.

The encounter only completes when the Captain and all Lieutenants are dead, so leaving Lieutenants alive during the final damage-over-time phase makes the finish considerably harder.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANKS

Split enemy control during mob phases, position Lieutenants safely and coordinate Captain Zoor’Lar's Tankbuster swaps.

Never point Disintegration Blade through the group.

HEALERS

Be prepared for heavy damage during Lieutenant mechanics, Energy Beam shares and the Captain's final increasing damage-over-time phase.

DPS

Kill priority Lieutenants quickly, preserve air by clearing waves fast and avoid accidentally pushing Captain Zoor’Lar into the next health phase before the group is ready.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Allowing the Air Remaining tracker to reach 0%.

Taking unnecessary Starfire debuff stacks from Lieutenant mechanics.

Destroying Air Bleeders too slowly or splitting the group inefficiently.

Failing to block the Captain's lunge before Disintegration Blade.

The same tank taking consecutive Tankbusters while still carrying the previous debuff.

Pushing the Captain's health too quickly and spawning additional Lieutenants before earlier enemies are under control.

Entering the final 15% damage-over-time phase with Lieutenants still alive.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Moondancer is basically one enormous exercise in not wasting time. Every unnecessary mob chase, missed mechanic and messy Lieutenant pull eats into the same air timer.

The final fight becomes much cleaner when the group controls the Captain's health thresholds instead of tunnelling him and summoning half the Astral Sea onto the deck at once.
