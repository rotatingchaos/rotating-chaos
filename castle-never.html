THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Castle Never introduces the mechanics used by the final boss throughout the dungeon before combining them during the encounter with Orcus.

The major mechanics are player-link beams, Abyssal Tears, charging death spheres, room flips, moving Orbs of Annihilation and heavy unavoidable group damage.

Learning each mechanic on the way through makes the final encounter considerably easier.

EARLY MECHANIC

THE BRIDGE

Large groups of skeletons and demons spawn while the group pushes across the bridge.

Group the enemies together and clear them with AoE damage.

PORTAL ATTACK

A portal at the far end periodically sends a large rectangular damage zone across the bridge.

Move to either side of the bridge to avoid the attack.

PORTAL MECHANIC

ABYSSAL TEARS

Several Abyssal Tears appear around the room.

Interact with a tear to enter it.

Kill all enemies inside the tear and you will be returned to the main room.

Players can enter a tear already occupied by another group member to help them clear it.

All tears must be dealt with before the group can continue.

MINI BOSS

TALGATH THE BEHOLDER

Talgath uses frontal attacks, ground AoEs and a link mechanic between two players.

FRONTAL ATTACK

The Beholder fires a fan-shaped attack in front of itself.

The tank should keep Talgath facing away from the group, while DPS attack from behind where possible.

RED CIRCLES

Red damage circles appear around the arena. Move out before they activate.

PLAYER LINK

Two players can become connected by a beam.

The linked players are gradually pulled toward one another.

If they touch, they are launched into the air and take heavy damage.

If linked, move away from the other linked player and stay separated until the mechanic ends.

DANGEROUS ADD

BAELOR

The Baelor encountered later in the dungeon can deal heavy damage with its sword attacks.

The tank should hold its attention while the group attacks from behind.

Watch its sword animation and dodge the telegraphed attack.

DEATH EXPLOSION

The Baelor explodes when killed.

Move away as it dies to avoid taking damage and being knocked backwards.

IMPORTANT MECHANIC

DEATH SPHERE

A large green sphere appears in the centre of the room while undead enemies move toward it.

If an undead enemy reaches the sphere, it contributes toward charging it.

STOP THE CHARGE

Kill the undead before they reach the centre.

If the sphere reaches maximum charge, it releases an extremely powerful room-wide attack.

After firing, the sphere resets and begins charging again.

Continue killing the required enemies until the phase ends.

MINI BOSS

ILLITHILICH

The Illithilich periodically flips players between the floor and ceiling of the arena.

ROOM FLIP

The room flip is time-based rather than triggered by the boss's remaining health.

The longer the fight lasts, the more room flips the group will face.

SPIKE SQUARES

Watch for red squares underneath your character.

These indicate dangerous spike areas when the room flips.

Move away from the marked squares before the flip to avoid heavy damage.

ADDS

Clear summoned adds when necessary while maintaining damage on the boss.

Faster boss damage means fewer room flips and fewer opportunities for the spikes to kill players.

ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARD

ORBS OF ANNIHILATION

Large green Orbs of Annihilation travel backwards and forwards through the corridors.

These deal extremely heavy damage and should generally be avoided rather than tanked.

WATCH THEIR MOVEMENT

Wait for an orb to pass before moving through its path.

Follow behind it where appropriate rather than attempting to run directly through it.

SAFE AREAS

Some corners and side areas are outside the path of the orbs.

Dangerous enemies can be pulled into these safe areas so the group does not have to fight while simultaneously avoiding the moving spheres.

FINAL BOSS

ORCUS

Orcus remains in the centre of the arena throughout the encounter.

The tank should face him away from the group, while DPS attack from behind.

Orcus combines mechanics already encountered throughout Castle Never.

ROOM-WIDE ATTACK

Orcus periodically stops attacking and raises his arms.

This signals a powerful unavoidable room-wide attack.

Make sure health is high before the damage lands and be ready for the mechanic that follows.

PLAYER LINK

Orcus can connect two players with the same beam used during the Beholder encounter.

Linked players must stay separated.

Do not allow the two linked players to touch.

ABYSSAL TEARS

Portals can spawn around the edge of the arena.

Enter a portal and kill the enemies inside before returning to the main fight.

Orcus gains extremely high defence while the portals are active, making damage against him largely ineffective.

Deal with the portals before returning to boss damage.

DEATH SPHERE

Orcus can leave the arena while a green Death Sphere appears in the centre.

Undead enemies will move toward the sphere and charge it.

Kill them before they reach the centre.

If the sphere fully charges, it releases a powerful room-wide attack and begins charging again.

Once the required enemies have been defeated, the sphere disappears and Orcus returns.

ORBS OF ANNIHILATION

Orcus can summon the same moving green orbs encountered before the boss room.

Watch their paths and stay clear. They deal extremely heavy damage.

RETURNING TO ORCUS

Whenever a special phase ends, allow the tank to regain Orcus's attention first.

DPS should then return behind the boss and continue the burn.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Keep bosses facing away from the group wherever possible.

Control dangerous adds and give DPS access to safer positioning behind enemies.

When Orcus returns after a special mechanic, regain his attention before the group resumes full damage.

HEALER

Be prepared for heavy incoming damage throughout the dungeon.

During Orcus, watch for his arm-raise animation and prepare for the unavoidable room-wide hit.

Players caught by links, spheres or other mechanics may require immediate recovery.

DPS

Use AoE damage on the large groups of enemies throughout the dungeon.

Prioritise enemies attempting to reach the Death Sphere.

Attack bosses from behind where possible and react immediately to player links and environmental hazards.

During Orcus's portal phase, stop wasting your rotation on his enormous defence and deal with the bloody portals. 😂

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Linked players running into each other.

Allowing undead enemies to reach and fully charge the Death Sphere.

Standing on spike squares during the Illithilich room flip.

Attempting to tank moving Orbs of Annihilation.

Standing beside the Baelor when it explodes.

Entering Orcus's room-wide attack with low health.

Ignoring Abyssal Tears while Orcus has massively increased defence.

DPS attacking Orcus before the tank has regained control after a special phase.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Castle Never basically gives you a mechanics exam and then makes Orcus the final paper.

Beam = stay apart. Portal = go inside and murder everything. Death Sphere = stop the adds reaching it. Giant green murder ball = don't fucking touch it.

Orcus raising his arms means everybody is about to get slapped, so healers should be ready and nobody should be wandering around on three HP pretending everything is fine.

Deal with whatever mechanic he throws out, reset your positions and go back to hitting the big bastard. Repeat until considerably less alive.
