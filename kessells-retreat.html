KESSELL'S RETREAT

Rotating Chaos Neverwinter Dungeon Guide

THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Kessell's Retreat is a relatively straightforward dungeon built around controlling groups of enemies before the final encounter against Akar Kessell.

The main dangers are knockback attacks, charging enemies, exploding Black Ice Golems and the large amount of AoE damage during the final boss.

ON THE WAY

DUNGEON MOBS

GROUP THE ENEMIES

Most of the dungeon consists of groups of enemies.

Gather them together where possible so the group can use AoE damage efficiently.

DEATHLOCK WIGHTS

Deathlock Wights can deal significant damage, particularly to lower-geared groups.

Prioritise them if they are causing problems.

LARGE RED CIRCLES

Some enemies use large red-circle attacks that knock players back.

Move out of the marked area before the attack lands.

BEAR CHARGES

Mounted enemies can charge directly at players.

A long, thin red telegraph appears on the floor before the charge.

Dodge out of the line to avoid being knocked down.

BEFORE THE BOSS

BLACK ICE GOLEMS

Black Ice Golems explode when they die.

As a golem reaches death, a red circle appears around it.

Move outside the circle before the explosion.

Being caught inside the explosion can freeze and stun the player.

The same type of golem appears again during the final boss, so continue watching for the death explosion.

FINAL BOSS

AKAR KESSELL

Akar Kessell fights alongside two Beholders and periodically summons additional enemies.

BEHOLDERS

Two Beholders are present during the encounter.

Focus them down early to reduce the amount of pressure on the group.

If a Beholder is reduced to very low health without being killed, it can turn and attack Akar Kessell.

Lower-damage groups can use this mechanic to gain additional damage against the boss.

BLACK & PURPLE ENERGY ORBS

Kessell continually releases black and purple energy orbs around the arena.

Avoid them where possible.

Being hit can temporarily lock some Encounter Powers, preventing the affected player from using them.

The lock only lasts for a short time, but multiple players being affected simultaneously can significantly reduce the group's damage and control.

RED AOES

Large areas of the arena can become covered by red AoE telegraphs.

Keep moving and avoid standing in them.

UNSTABLE GOLEMS

Kessell summons unstable Black Ice Golems during the fight.

These behave like the golems encountered before the boss and explode when they die.

Move away from their red death circles.

Being frozen or stunned by a golem explosion can leave a player trapped inside another red AoE.

ADD WAVES

Additional enemies spawn throughout the encounter.

Deal with dangerous adds quickly so they do not accumulate while the group is handling Kessell's AoEs and energy orbs.

FINAL BURN

Once the Beholders and dangerous adds are controlled, focus damage on Akar Kessell.

Continue avoiding his black and purple energy attacks while burning him down.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Gather dungeon mobs together so the group can burn them efficiently.

During the final boss, control Kessell and incoming adds while keeping dangerous attacks away from the rest of the group where possible.

HEALER

Watch for players caught by knockdowns, freezes or overlapping AoEs.

The final arena can become crowded with hazards, so maintain positioning that keeps the group within healing range.

DPS

Use AoE damage on grouped dungeon mobs.

Prioritise dangerous enemies such as Deathlock Wights where necessary.

During the final boss, deal with the Beholders and adds before concentrating fully on Kessell.

Move away from Black Ice Golems when their death circles appear.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Standing inside large red knockback attacks.

Failing to dodge mounted enemy charges.

Remaining inside a Black Ice Golem's explosion and becoming frozen.

Ignoring the Beholders while they continue attacking the group.

Walking into Kessell's black and purple energy orbs and losing access to Encounter Powers.

Allowing summoned adds to accumulate during the boss fight.

Being frozen by an exploding golem while standing inside another AoE.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Kessell's Retreat isn't complicated. It just contains several different ways of knocking you on your arse.

Group the mobs. Dodge the charges. When a golem develops a massive red circle, stop cuddling it.

At Kessell: sort the Beholders and adds, avoid the purple balls, then introduce the wizard to the combined anger of five guild members.
