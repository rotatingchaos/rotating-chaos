THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Infernal Citadel is built around controlled enemy pulls, environmental hazards, chained players, heavy tank attacks and careful management of the arena floor.

DUNGEON MECHANIC

DEMON POWERS

At the start of the dungeon, each player must choose one of the available demon powers.

The group should normally choose the same power so everyone remains together during the mini-boss section.

One power can break chains and walls quickly.

Another provides protection from poison.

Another can stun and damage certain devils.

DUNGEON HAZARD

HALL OF CHAINS

Chains block paths through the room.

Break only the chains on the safe side.

Do not break chains blocking poison routes.

Opening a poison route releases poison and causes it to spread through the area.

Continue clearing enemies and breaking safe chains until the route opens.

MINI BOSS

DEMON CHALLENGE

The group fights the demon associated with the power chosen earlier.

Heavy red AoEs can deal lethal damage.

Avoid standing in the danger zones.

Some attacks can knock the tank backward and stun them.

Tanks should block or move out where possible.

BOSS ONE

MORZACH

Let the tank engage Morzach first.

DPS approaching before the tank establishes aggro can be killed very quickly.

Morzach uses claw attacks, a forward dash, tail attacks and ranged spine attacks.

When he rises into the air and fires spines, move close underneath him to avoid much of the damage.

GAS SPORE SACKS

Spore sacks periodically appear in the arena.

If left alive, they eventually explode.

The explosion leaves poison gas on the floor.

Too many explosions can cover most of the arena and leave the group with very little safe space.

If damage is low or the fight is taking longer, clear the spore sacks before they explode.

BOSS TWO

ZAPHAEL

Zaphael uses heavy overhead hammer attacks.

Each overhead attack creates a ring of fire around him.

The tank should reposition the boss after each fire ring so melee players can continue attacking safely.

Large red AoEs also appear beneath players.

Move out immediately because remaining inside can kill you.

CHAINS & PILLARS

At approximately 75%, 50%, 25% and very low health, Zaphael phases out.

Two imps spawn and chain two players.

Free the chained players immediately.

Once everyone is free, hide behind the pillars.

Zaphael then releases a massive fire blast.

The pillars block the blast.

Tanks should also use the pillar rather than assuming they can survive the hit.

After the blast, the tank must re-establish aggro quickly.

SMALL FIRE AOES

Small red circles begin targeting players later in the fight.

Move these away from the boss and common movement routes.

Dropping them against the outer wall helps preserve safe fighting space.

Tanks should block Zaphael's overhead swings and use mitigation if necessary.

FINAL BOSS

HELLFIRE ENGINE

The Hellfire Engine progressively destroys the floor.

Each player periodically receives a red fire-bomb AoE.

When the bomb resolves, it damages the floor tile beneath the player.

After two hits, that tile becomes lava and is permanently unsafe.

STACK THE FIRE BOMBS

When all players receive fire bombs, stack together on one tile.

This concentrates the floor damage onto a single tile.

Only one tile is then lost instead of several.

Move systematically across the room as each tile becomes unusable.

Avoid scattering fire bombs randomly and destroying safe space too quickly.

SPIN ATTACK

After the group fire bombs, the Hellfire Engine begins spinning.

Fire sprays from the front during early spins.

Later spins also include side lasers.

Stay behind the boss and rotate around with it.

Keep moving with the safe side until the spin ends.

CHARGE

After spinning, the boss targets one player with a charge.

The targeted player should position carefully and avoid sending the boss through lava.

Poor positioning can make the boss difficult to reach or place players in lethal terrain.

SINGLE FIRE BOMB

One player can receive a solo red fire-bomb AoE.

Take it to the next tile already planned for destruction.

If you cannot reach that tile safely, place it near a wall.

Do not drop it in the middle of the arena.

FIRE ROWS

After the single fire bomb, rows of the floor erupt with fire.

Watch the floor and move into a safe lane.

The encounter then returns to the normal cycle.

EXPLODING VEHICLES

Small mechanical vehicles begin spawning later in the fight.

They explode when destroyed or triggered.

The explosion causes knockback.

Stay away from them when they are about to explode.

Being knocked into lava is usually fatal.

LAST 20%

The final 20% is a damage check.

Save enough burst damage and debuffs to finish the boss inside the available time.

Safe floor space will already be limited, so end the fight before the arena becomes unmanageable.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Engage Morzach before the DPS move in.

Reposition Zaphael after his fire rings so melee players retain safe space.

Use the pillar during Zaphael's massive fire blast.

Re-establish aggro immediately after the blast.

HEALER

Watch for heavy damage during the demon challenge and boss encounters.

During Zaphael, be ready for players repositioning around fire rings and chain phases.

During Hellfire Engine, keep moving with the group as usable floor space decreases.

DPS

Let the tank establish Morzach before engaging.

Clear spore sacks if poison gas is beginning to consume too much of the arena.

Free chained players immediately during Zaphael.

During Hellfire Engine, preserve floor space rather than scattering bombs.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Breaking chains on the poison side of the Hall of Chains.

DPS engaging Morzach before the tank has aggro.

Allowing too many spore sacks to explode and fill the arena with poison.

Failing to free chained players quickly during Zaphael.

Not getting behind a pillar before Zaphael's fire blast.

Dropping small fire circles through the middle of the Zaphael arena.

Spreading Hellfire Engine group bombs across multiple tiles.

Destroying too much floor too early.

Standing in front of the Hellfire Engine during its spin.

Sending the boss through lava during its charge.

Dropping a solo fire bomb in the middle of safe floor.

Being knocked into lava by exploding vehicles.

Reaching the final damage check without enough safe floor or damage remaining.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Everybody takes the same demon power. This is not the moment to be quirky and choose your own adventure.

Poison behind a chain? The chain is doing you a favour. Leave it there.

Zaphael chains people, you free them, then everybody hides behind a pillar. Even the tank. Yes, even you.

Hellfire Engine group bombs: everybody piles onto the same tile. Losing one square is considerably better than turning the entire arena into fucking lava.

Boss starts spinning? Follow its arse. This may not sound like advanced tactical advice, but it is remarkably effective.

Last 20%: you've probably destroyed half the floor already. Stop sightseeing and kill the bastard.
