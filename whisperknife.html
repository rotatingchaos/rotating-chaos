HOW IT WORKS

WHISPERKNIFE EXPLAINED

Whisperknife combines Stealth, positional damage, damage-over-time effects and burst during the party's coordinated support window.

Single target revolves around maintaining Disheartening Strike, keeping Combat Advantage active and combining Stealth with Shadow Strike, Lashing Blade and Impact Shot.

Multi target changes the rhythm completely. Blade Flurry, Smoke Bomb and Path of the Blade take you directly into the enemy pack.

SINGLE TARGET

DISHEARTENING STRIKE

Maintain Disheartening Strike and refresh it roughly every ten seconds.

It's easy to focus on the big encounter hits and forget the maintenance power, but the rest of the burst performs better when its effect is already established.

POSITIONING

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

Flank the boss whenever possible so Combat Advantage remains active.

Dagger Threat works well when normal boss flanking can be maintained.

Swap to Advantageous Position when mechanics or spawned enemies make reliable flanking difficult.

Get into position before entering Stealth. Don't spend half your burst window running around the boss looking for its arse. 😂

STEALTH

SINGLE-TARGET BURST

Enter Stealth and immediately use Shadow Strike. Follow with Lashing Blade and Impact Shot.

Cloud of Steel fills downtime while encounters recover and continues generating Action Points.

Smoke Bomb can replace Impact Shot where the boss encounter includes adds or where extra crowd control and AoE are useful.

MULTI TARGET

BLADE FLURRY

Enter the enemy pack, activate Stealth and use Blade Flurry once.

Follow with Smoke Bomb and Path of the Blade, then keep the second Blade Flurry available until Stealth returns.

Spending both charges immediately loses the stronger interaction available when the next Blade Flurry is paired with Stealth.

BURST

THE ARTIFACT WINDOW

Whisperknife's damage increases dramatically during a coordinated party artifact window.

Enter the call with Combat Advantage active and encounters ready.

Stack the strongest attacks inside the support window rather than pressing everything five seconds before everybody else.

Cavalry's Haste and Tactician's Precision support the full cooldown sequence.

GEAR

SCALED CONTENT & SWAPS

Never assume the newest or highest-item-level piece automatically wins.

Astral Raider's Gloves remain relevant in scaled content and can outperform newer arms after scaling.

Bindings of the Death Pact from Master Shackles of Divinity remain the preferred multi-target arms in this build.

Voidtouched and Xaryxian weapons remain strong alternatives and can perform extremely well in scaled single-target fights.

SURVIVAL

CELESTIAL BOOTS

Celestial boots offer a large damage bonus, but the build records a 20% Maximum HP trade-off.

While learning difficult content, a safer option can produce more actual damage than spending half the fight dead.

The floor has never once appeared on a paingiver chart. 😂

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Whisperknife has a beautifully Rogue approach to multi-target combat: walk directly into the middle of everybody, disappear, then start throwing sharp shit around. 😂

On bosses, don't underestimate Disheartening Strike or Combat Advantage. They're not the flashy bits, but they're part of what makes the flashy bits actually hurt.

And don't miss the artifact call. This is not the toon where everybody presses their artifacts, you notice twelve seconds later and go "ah bollocks, mine too."

That little murder window is where you earn your keep. 😂
