HOW IT WORKS

SENTINEL EXPLAINED

Sentinel is an aggressive tank. Threat generation, Rage, blocking and group support all happen alongside actively attacking the target.

Establish enough Defense, Awareness, Critical Avoidance, Deflect and Maximum Hit Points to survive the content. Once that baseline is secure, additional slots can move toward support and group damage.

Strike and Guard enchantment slots make threat generation considerably easier in Module 33.5, giving experienced Sentinels more freedom to use movement and defensive utility.

THREAT

SENTINEL'S SLASH

Sentinel's Slash is central to the threat loop. Use it before the major threat-generating attacks, particularly during artifact calls.

Sure Strike fills the gaps between encounters and helps maintain sustained single-target threat.

Rage should normally be spent for sustained aggro rather than hoarded indefinitely. Hold it when the next artifact call is only seconds away and doing so improves burst threat.

BURST

TAKEDOWN & ARTIFACT CALLS

Where possible, keep Takedown available as the party approaches the next artifact call.

Fire the mount first, then the artifact. Follow with Sentinel's Slash before dumping the important encounters and Daily into the burst window.

If cooldown support returns the encounters while the group debuffs remain active, use another encounter pass before returning to the normal sustained threat loop.

CLASS FEATURES

THREATENING PRESENCE VS BRAVERY

Threatening Presence is the safer option while learning Sentinel or whenever maintaining aggro is inconsistent.

Once threat is genuinely comfortable, Bravery can replace it for additional movement speed and Deflect.

Don't switch merely because Threatening Presence feels like the beginner option. If you still need the threat, it is doing its bloody job.

COMPANION SUPPORT

SPINED DEVIL & ALTERNATIVES

Spined Devil is the preferred group-damage option where the party already has the offensive percentages it needs.

A companion such as Portobello becomes more attractive where the group relies on those additional offensive stats. Black Death Scorpion is another support option where its positioning requirements can be maintained.

Armor Break, Dulled Senses and Vulnerability are useful dungeon enhancement choices, but coordinate them across the party rather than needlessly duplicating effects.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Sentinel isn't supposed to spend the entire fight hiding behind the block button. You're a Barbarian. Apparently even tanking has to be done aggressively. 😂

The big habit is Sentinel's Slash before the important threat-generating attacks. Get that right and the rest of the rotation becomes considerably easier to control.

And remember the support order: MOUNT BEFORE ARTIFACT.

If your mount finally applies its effect after the DPS have already unloaded half their rotation, congratulations — you've successfully debuffed the boss for the quiet bit. 🤣
