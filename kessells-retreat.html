THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Kessell's Retreat is largely an add-control dungeon, with groups of enemies encountered throughout the route before the final fight against Akar Kessell.

Most enemy groups are best handled by keeping them together and using strong area-of-effect damage.

The main mechanics to watch are enemy knockbacks, charging bears, exploding Black Ice Golems, Kessell's energy attacks and the Beholders in the final encounter.

ON THE WAY

DUNGEON MOBS

The dungeon contains repeated groups of enemies that can become considerably more dangerous if allowed to spread out.

GROUP THE ADDS

Keep enemy groups together wherever possible and burn them down with AoE damage.

LARGE RED CIRCLES

Some enemies use large red-circle attacks.

Move out of these before they activate.

Being hit can knock players back and disrupt positioning.

DEATHLOCK WIGHTS

Deathlock Wights can deal significant damage, particularly to less durable players.

Prioritise them if they are causing problems for the group.

BEAR CHARGES

Enemies mounted on bears can perform charging attacks.

Watch for the long, narrow red telegraph on the ground and dodge away before the charge lands.

Being hit can knock you down.

IMPORTANT ADD

BLACK ICE GOLEMS

Black Ice Golems explode when they die.

DEATH EXPLOSION

When a golem is about to explode, a red danger circle appears around it.

Move outside the circle before the explosion activates.

FREEZE

Players caught inside the explosion can become frozen and temporarily unable to act.

This becomes particularly dangerous during the final boss, where other damaging AoEs may already cover parts of the arena.

FINAL BOSS

AKAR KESSELL

Akar Kessell fights alongside two Beholders and summons additional enemies throughout the encounter.

BEHOLDERS

Two Beholders are present during the fight.

They attack the group and can create additional pressure while Kessell's attacks cover the arena.

Focusing down one Beholder at a time can make the encounter considerably easier.

TURNING THE BEHOLDERS

When a Beholder is reduced to very low health, it can turn and attack Akar Kessell.

Groups that need the additional damage can leave it alive briefly and allow it to attack the boss.

High-damage groups can simply kill the Beholders and continue attacking Kessell.

BLACK & PURPLE ENERGY

Kessell repeatedly releases black and purple energy attacks around the arena.

Avoid these wherever possible.

They can deal significant damage and temporarily disable some encounter powers.

RED AOE

Large areas of the arena can become covered by red AoE attacks.

Keep moving and maintain enough space to avoid overlapping environmental damage.

UNSTABLE GOLEMS

Kessell summons Unstable Golems during the encounter.

These behave like the Black Ice Golems encountered earlier in the dungeon and explode when killed.

Move away from their red death circles before they detonate.

FREEZE + AOE

Getting frozen by an exploding golem can leave you unable to move out of Kessell's other AoE attacks.

Avoiding the golem explosions is therefore particularly important during the boss fight.

FINAL BURN

Once the Beholders and dangerous adds are under control, focus damage on Akar Kessell.

Continue avoiding his black and purple energy attacks until he is defeated.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Group dungeon enemies together so DPS can clear them efficiently with AoE damage.

During the final fight, maintain control of Kessell and dangerous adds while giving the group room to avoid AoEs.

HEALER

Watch players caught by knockbacks, Beholder attacks or Kessell's energy damage.

Be especially alert when a player becomes frozen, as they may be unable to escape incoming AoE damage.

DPS

Group enemies and use AoE damage throughout the dungeon.

Prioritise dangerous Wights and other high-damage enemies when necessary.

During Kessell, deal with Beholders and summoned adds while avoiding the boss's energy attacks.

Do not stand beside a dying Black Ice or Unstable Golem.

It will explode, and no, it does not care how impressive your damage rotation was.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Allowing large enemy groups to spread out instead of controlling them together.

Standing inside large red-circle knockback attacks.

Getting hit by bear charges and being knocked down.

Remaining inside a Black Ice Golem's explosion radius.

Ignoring the Beholders while they overwhelm the group.

Standing in Kessell's black and purple energy attacks.

Getting frozen by an Unstable Golem while standing inside another damaging AoE.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Most of Kessell's Retreat is fairly straightforward: group the angry things together and make the angry things dead.

Bear draws a massive red runway on the floor? Move sideways. Do not wait at the end of it like you're expecting fucking Ryanair.

Dying golem gets a red circle. Leave the red circle. This mechanic has now been explained with almost insulting clarity.

At Kessell, deal with the Beholders, dodge the purple bullshit and don't get frozen inside an AoE.

Congratulations. You now know considerably more than the person who queued in and immediately sprinted directly into everything.
