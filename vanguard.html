HOW IT WORKS

VANGUARD EXPLAINED

Vanguard is the Fighter's dedicated tank path. The build combines strong defensive percentages with deliberate threat generation and group support.

The shield is there to absorb the dangerous attacks, but Vanguard isn't a tank that simply hides behind it. Attacking deliberately is a major part of maintaining enough threat for the DPS to unload safely.

Positioning matters just as much. Keeping the boss facing the correct direction protects the party and makes Combat Advantage easier for everyone else.

THREAT

SINGLE TARGET

Brazen Slash is the normal filler between encounter cycles. Tide of Iron forms an important part of the threat engine and should be used immediately before the heavy threat encounters.

Shield Throw and Anvil of Doom provide concentrated single-target threat. Anvil of Challenge is useful when a boss must be reclaimed immediately or during tank swaps.

Retaliate is another strong threat and damage tool. Use the double Retaliate whenever possible and try to place it inside the strongest section of the party's burst.

SHIELD

STAMINA MANAGEMENT

Steel Recovery restores stamina whenever an encounter or daily is used, helping maintain the shield during sustained fights.

Don't waste stamina blocking harmless attacks simply because the shield exists. Save enough for the attacks that actually matter.

MULTI TARGET

PACK CONTROL

Switch into the multi-target configuration for trash packs and encounters containing several dangerous enemies.

Cleave provides the AoE at-will while Enforced Threat helps establish or reclaim threat across the group. Greater Endurance improves movement while stamina is high, and Steel Recovery remains useful for shield management.

MASTER JOTUNSKAR

FIRST BOSS

Use Cleave instead of Brazen Slash for the first boss.

If a tank-buster removes you from the fight and you return to find the Remorhaz or Basilisks chasing someone else, immediately use Enforced Threat or Anvil of Doom with the threat feature to reclaim them.

FLEXIBILITY

ALTERNATIVES

If survivability is the problem, move companion bonuses, the combat enchantment and gear effects toward maximum hit points and defensive percentages.

If threat is the problem, retain Anvil of Challenge and lean harder into Shield Throw, Anvil of Doom, Retaliate and offensive bonuses.

Once survivability and threat are comfortable, the tank can lean further into support companions, debuff enhancements, mount powers and artifacts to increase total party damage.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Vanguard isn't just "hold shield and hope." The good ones are attacking, blocking, positioning, watching threat, timing support powers and quietly preventing three DPS from making extremely poor life choices. 😂

The Tide of Iron timing is worth practising. If you cancel it too quickly by blocking, the extra threat effect can fail to activate.

And remember: MOUNT BEFORE ARTIFACT. Don't be the bastard whose debuff finally arrives after everybody else's encounters are already on cooldown. 🤣
