ABOUT THIS SITE

Rotating Chaos is a player-run Neverwinter guild and community website.

Some areas of the website are public, while interactive community features are intended for authenticated Rotating Chaos guild members.

We only use information needed to provide and manage those community features.

FIREBASE

Rotating Chaos uses Firebase services to provide guild-member authentication and interactive website features.

Firebase Authentication is used to manage member access, while Firestore is used to store information required by community features on the website.

These features may include Chaos Chat, Events, Crew Profiles, Gallery, From the Chaos, My Chaos and other member areas.

INFORMATION WE MAY STORE

Depending on which parts of the website you use, information stored may include:

  • Your guild name used for login and display.
  • Information required for your authenticated guild-member account.
  • Your guild role or website permission level.
  • Crew profile information you choose to provide.
  • Your profile image URL.
  • Chaos Chat messages and reactions.
  • Event sign-ups and run-sheet assignments.
  • Gallery entries, image links, captions and categories.
  • From the Chaos posts or other member-created content.
  • Merch information created through authorised website features.

PROFILE IMAGES & CLOUDINARY

Guild members can choose to upload a profile image from their device.

Profile images uploaded through the website are hosted using Cloudinary. Once uploaded, the image is stored by Cloudinary and the resulting image URL is saved with the member's Crew Profile.

Members should only upload images they are comfortable using as part of their guild profile and that they have permission to use.

MEMBER-CREATED CONTENT

Some parts of Rotating Chaos allow guild members to create or submit content.

This can include profiles, chat messages, event information, gallery content, posts, images and other community contributions.

Information submitted to shared guild areas may be visible to other authenticated members who have access to that area of the website.

WHO CAN SEE CONTENT

Public-facing pages may contain general information about Rotating Chaos, Neverwinter guides, guild information, events, merchandise and other public content.

Interactive member areas are intended to be restricted to authenticated guild members.

Members should still avoid posting sensitive personal information in chats, profiles, posts or other shared community areas.

YOUR CONTENT

Where the website provides the option, members can edit or remove content they created.

This may include Crew Profiles, event participation, posts, gallery entries and other contributions.

Guild administrators may also moderate, edit or remove content where required for the operation of the guild community.

REMOVAL REQUESTS

If you leave Rotating Chaos or want information associated with your website account reviewed or removed, contact the guild team.

We can review content and account information held within the Rotating Chaos website systems and remove it where appropriate.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Rotating Chaos uses or links to third-party services in order to provide parts of the website.

These may include Firebase, Cloudinary, Discord, merchandise providers and other external websites or services.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, security practices and terms.

EXTERNAL LINKS

The website contains links to external services, including the Rotating Chaos Discord server.

Once you follow an external link, the privacy practices of that service apply.
