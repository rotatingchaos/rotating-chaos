THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Thaumaturge is a magical DPS Wizard built around maintaining Chill, Rimefire Smolder and Arcane Mastery while lining up its hardest encounters with the party's major burst windows.

It also brings excellent group utility. A Thaumaturge can trade a small amount of personal damage for valuable magical and projectile debuffs, making the correct setup depend on both the encounter and whether another Wizard is already covering those effects.

The current build has dedicated single-target, full-debuff, maximum-damage and multi-target configurations, plus a specialised setup for the first boss of Master Jotunskar.

CORE RULE: Keep Chill on the target and Arcane Mastery on yourself. If those stacks disappear, so does a chunk of the reason this build hits so bloody hard.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Thaumaturge wants a balanced offensive spread. Temporary buffs, companion bonuses and group support all affect the final percentages, so build around your actual in-combat values rather than copying somebody else's character sheet exactly.

1 ◆ POWER Core offensive percentage and one of the first stats to establish.
2 ◆ COMBAT ADVANTAGE Extremely important when positioned correctly, particularly in fights where several enemies can be grouped together.
3 ◆ CRITICAL STRIKE Balance with Critical Severity and account for temporary effects before over-investing.
4 ◆ CRITICAL SEVERITY Another major damage percentage. Keep the finished build balanced.
5 ◆ ACCURACY Finish Accuracy after the stronger offensive percentages are in a healthy position.
6 ◆ RECHARGE Valuable for fitting encounters into the rotation, especially now that current weapon choices can provide less Recharge than older setups.
ABILITY SCORES: Intelligence is the primary offensive ability score. Use the remaining points to complement the finished build and its critical or recharge requirements.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments are there to complete the finished stat spread. Changing weapons, rings or party composition can change which enchantments are optimal, so don't treat the exact distribution as permanent.

  • Power where required
  • Combat Advantage
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Accuracy to finish the offensive spread
  • Recharge where needed to smooth the encounter rotation

Armour kits, jewels and other modifications should follow the same principle: use them to fill the ratings the completed build actually lacks.

ARTIFACTS

Thaumaturge is heavily burst-oriented, so the active artifact should be coordinated with the party rather than fired whenever the button lights up.

PRIMARY

Use a current damage or group-debuff artifact appropriate to the party's artifact-call order.

Use current artifacts to complete offensive percentages and gain useful set bonuses without overfilling ratings.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Thaumaturge has several valid configurations. Which one you use depends on the group, the fight and whether another Thaumaturge is already supplying the important Wizard debuffs.

SINGLE TARGET ◆ RECOMMENDED

This is the default boss setup when you're the only Wizard in the group. It sacrifices a small amount of selfish damage in favour of stronger overall party damage.

SWATH OF DESTRUCTION

Recommended when you're the only Wizard. It improves the group's damage against enemies affected by Smolder.

ORB OF IMPOSITION

Slightly stronger selfish option, but usually inferior to Swath when the damage of the whole group is considered.

FULL DEBUFF SETUP

RAY OF ENFEEBLEMENT

Used on Tab when the group needs the full Wizard debuff setup. Particularly strong in magical and projectile-heavy parties.

CRITICAL CONFLAGRATION

Part of the full debuff configuration when another Thaumaturge can take advantage of the support you're providing.

SWATH OF DESTRUCTION

Remains part of the full debuff configuration to improve overall party damage.

FULL DIESEL SETUP

Use this maximum personal-damage configuration only when another Thaumaturge is already running Ray of Enfeeblement, Critical Conflagration and Swath of Destruction.

IMPORTANT: Full Diesel depends on maintaining maximum Arcane Mastery and Chill. Somebody else is doing the support job, so your job is now to make the boss regret loading into the instance. 😂

MJS FIRST BOSS

The specialised Master Jotunskar first-boss setup uses two multi-target powers because the Remorhaz and Basilisks can be grouped close enough for Thaumaturge to damage several targets at once.

ICY TERRAIN

Use early in the sequence. Putting it on Tab makes the fight more comfortable because you don't need to remain permanently in melee range.

FANNING THE FLAME

Use alongside Icy Terrain early to maximise damage across the grouped enemies.

DIRECTED FLAMES

The main driver behind the specialised first-boss setup when the enemies are properly grouped.

RIMEFIRE WEAVING

Works alongside the Smolder and Chill mechanics and supports the multi-target configuration.

MULTI TARGET

For normal packs, switch into the dedicated multi-target loadout and prioritise applying Chill and Smolder across grouped enemies before unloading your larger area attacks.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

COMBAT FLOW

CORE PRIORITIES

  1. MAINTAIN CHILL Always keep at least one Chill stack on the target so Rimefire Smolder remains active.
  2. BUILD ARCANE MASTERY Maintain five stacks of Arcane Mastery to strengthen the encounters that benefit from it.
  3. MAX CHILL WHEN USING CHILLING PRESENCE Maintain six Chill stacks when using Chilling Presence for its maximum damage benefit.
  4. PREPARE THE ARTIFACT CALL Enter the burst window with important encounters available instead of throwing them away seconds before the call.

ARTIFACT CALL

  1. ESTABLISH YOUR STACKS Make sure Chill and Arcane Mastery are already under control.
  2. ARTIFACT / PARTY DEBUFFS Begin the coordinated burst as the group's support effects land.
  3. ENCOUNTER BURST Run through the prepared encounter sequence while the strongest group bonuses are active.
  4. CHILLING CLOUD ◆ CHILLING CLOUD ◆ MAGIC MISSILE When using filler at-wills, this sequence attacks faster than repeatedly alternating the two powers.
  5. SECOND ENCOUNTER PASS Use returning encounters as cooldowns allow. The current Void weapon setup makes fitting the final Repel inside the call much tighter than the older Scintillant setup.

IF A MECHANIC INTERRUPTS YOU

Don't desperately try to restart the entire sequence. Most filler at-wills can be skipped. Keep the important Chill and Arcane effects, use at least one critical at-will where required for your temporary critical bonus, and continue the burst.

MJS FIRST BOSS

  1. ICY TERRAIN Apply it early while the enemies are grouped.
  2. FANNING THE FLAME Follow early to maximise damage across the grouped targets.
  3. GET COMBAT ADVANTAGE Position properly instead of hiding at the entrance if you want the maximum damage ceiling.
  4. MAINTAIN CHILL & ARCANE MASTERY Keep Rimefire Smolder active and Arcane Mastery at five stacks throughout the fight.
  5. REPEAT THROUGH BURST WINDOWS Exploit Directed Flames while the tank keeps the enemies grouped.

OTHER OPTIONS

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Thaumaturge has two jobs: maintain all the clever Wizard shit and then unleash an unreasonable amount of fire and ice when the artifact call starts. 🔥❄️😂

If you're the only Wizard, don't automatically choose the selfish setup. Swath of Destruction improving the other DPS can produce more total group damage than squeezing a little extra damage out of yourself.

And on MJS first boss: get close enough to actually have Combat Advantage. Yes, you can stand miles away near the cleanse and survive. No, the damage meter will not applaud your excellent commitment to personal safety. 🤣
