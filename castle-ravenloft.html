THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Castle Ravenloft is a mechanics-heavy dungeon featuring three major encounters: the Sisters, the Arcolith and Strahd von Zarovich.

Raw damage helps, but several mechanics must still be handled correctly. The key themes are separating enemies, reacting to debuffs, controlling boss positioning and using the Sun Sword correctly during the final fight.

FIRST ENCOUNTER

THE SISTERS

PHASE ONE ◆ FIRST SISTER

Attack the first Sister normally until the Book appears.

PHASE TWO ◆ USE THE BOOK

One player — preferably the lowest DPS — picks up the Book.

Use the Book's lure ability to pull two of the Sisters away from the Sister currently being attacked.

If the Sisters are too close together, the active Sister becomes invulnerable.

PHASE THREE ◆ DESTROY THE RED OBJECT

Once the first Sister dies, a red object appears below the stairs. The group has approximately 10 seconds to destroy it.

The timer begins when the object is first attacked, so allow the group to reach it before starting damage.

PHASE FOUR ◆ SECOND SISTER

Attack the second Sister while another player uses the Book to lure the remaining Sister away.

PHASE FIVE ◆ RED OBJECT AGAIN

After the second Sister dies, destroy the red object again using the same method.

PHASE SIX ◆ FINAL SISTER

Kill the final Sister.

Summoned enemies appear during this phase and can hit extremely hard, particularly if they gain Combat Advantage.

Healers may want to keep a strong daily heal available for this part of the encounter.

CHAINS

The Sister being attacked can chain one player to herself.

While the player is chained, damage dealt to that Sister also damages the chained player.

Stop attacking until the chain ends.

HYPO ARROWS

When Hypo Arrows occur, the group should stack together to share the incoming damage.

Healers should be ready with a large heal.

TANK DEBUFF

Whoever is holding aggro on the active Sister builds debuff stacks. These appear as a red marker beneath the character name.

When the stacks reach 10, the tank must shield until the stacks are removed.

Failing to shield at 10 stacks can kill the tank regardless of their remaining hit points.

DOPPELGANGERS

Doppelgangers can also appear during the Sisters fight. Kill them as they spawn.

SECOND ENCOUNTER

ARCOLITH

PHASE ONE ◆ DAMAGE

Attack the Arcolith and reduce its health normally.

Avoid unnecessary movement and excessive stamina use. The Arcolith reacts to movement and can perform additional actions.

Staying relatively stationary behind it is generally the safest position.

WATCH THE SWORD

The Arcolith has a huge sword swing.

Do not stand inside the sword arc. It hits extremely hard.

PHASE TWO ◆ SPINNING ARCOLITH

The Arcolith periodically becomes immune and begins spinning.

During this phase, the tank must position the Arcolith so that it collides with one of the arena pillars.

Hitting a pillar stops the spin and makes the Arcolith vulnerable again.

REPEAT

The encounter alternates between normal damage and spinning phases until the Arcolith dies.

Historically, allowing the Arcolith to reach a fifth spinning phase resulted in a wipe. Modern damage output means groups will usually kill it long before this becomes an issue.

FINAL ENCOUNTER

STRAHD VON ZAROVICH

One player will take responsibility for the Sun Sword during this fight.

STRAHD'S CURSE

Strahd begins by cursing the Sun Sword.

The group waits near the door for Strahd to approach and attempts to damage him before he can steal one of the party members away.

TELEPORTED PLAYER

If Strahd successfully takes a player, Doppelgangers representing party members appear.

Kill the Doppelgangers quickly to recover the missing player.

If the group takes too long, an Executioner appears and kills the teleported player.

DAMAGE PHASE

Once the Sun Sword is recovered, begin the main damage phase against Strahd.

Similar curse phases occur as his health is reduced, approximately around 25%, 50% and 75% health thresholds.

FINAL SHIELD

At roughly 15–20% health remaining, Strahd pulls the entire party into the centre of the arena.

Listen for the narrator's instruction: “Raise your sword to the sky.”

The Sun Sword carrier must use the Sword's daily ability to create a protective shield around the group.

If the shield is not activated correctly, the group wipes.

Survive the shield phase, finish Strahd and collect your loot.

FINAL FIGHT TOOL

THE SUN SWORD

One player should be assigned to use the Sun Sword during the Strahd fight.

X ◆ ACTION POINT RECHARGE

Recharges Action Points to full for nearby party members.

Y ◆ RUNNING ATTACK

Performs a running attack.

B ◆ HEAVY SWING

Performs a slower, powerful sword attack.

RIGHT TRIGGER ◆ STANDARD ATTACK

Used as the normal attack while carrying the sword.

X DAILY ◆ GROUP SHIELD

Activates the protective shield required during Strahd's final group-wipe mechanic.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

During the Sisters encounter, monitor debuff stacks and shield when they reach 10.

During Arcolith, control its position and direct its spinning phase into a pillar.

HEALER

Be ready for Hypo Arrows during Sisters and save strong healing for heavy group damage.

The final Sister's summoned enemies can be especially dangerous, so keeping a strong healing daily available can help.

DPS

Stop attacking a Sister when another player is chained to her.

During the red-object phases, wait until everyone reaches the target before beginning damage so the 10-second timer is not started early.

Quickly kill Doppelgangers and other priority adds when they appear.

EXTRA CHAOS

ACHIEVEMENTS

THREE GHOSTS

Speak to all three ghosts during the dungeon.

The first is in the hallway before the ballroom containing the Arcolith.

The second is inside the chapel with the vampires, to the right after entering.

The third is on the final stairs before Strahd.

BALLROOM ENEMIES

Allow all five Ballroom Dancers / Suits of Armour to spawn before killing anything.

One method is to let the tank gather them while the healer keeps the tank alive, avoiding damage until all five have spawned.

NIGHT TERROR

Defeat the optional Night Terror boss when it appears.

CHAPEL

Kill all vampires in the chapel before purifying the altar.

COMPLETION

Complete Castle Ravenloft.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Ravenloft is much easier once everyone understands which mechanics should not simply be brute-forced.

The biggest avoidable mistakes are attacking a chained Sister, starting the red-object timer before the group is ready, failing to drive Arcolith into a pillar and missing the Sun Sword shield during Strahd's final phase.

If those four things are handled properly, the run becomes dramatically cleaner.
