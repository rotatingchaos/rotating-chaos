THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Hunter is the Ranger paragon for players who want to make full use of the class's ranged and melee toolkit. The Ranger can switch stance during combat, changing its available powers and effectively giving the toon a second set of encounters.

That gives Hunter considerably more buttons than many DPS classes, but also gives it excellent flexibility. You can attack safely from range, move into melee for another encounter sequence, then switch back out again as powers recover.

Hunter is particularly strong when fighting groups. Its combination of ranged attacks, roots, area damage and stance swapping gives it a much stronger dedicated AoE toolkit than Warden.

THE HUNTER DIFFERENCE: You've basically got two action bars and somebody has decided you should use both of them. Welcome to Ranger. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Hunter is a physical DPS build, so concentrate on the standard offensive percentages while balancing the complete toon rather than individual pieces of equipment.

POWER

Core offensive stat and one of the main targets for the finished build.

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

Extremely valuable when attacking enemies from the correct position relative to your party.

CRITICAL STRIKE

Important for consistent critical hits and should be balanced alongside Critical Severity.

CRITICAL SEVERITY

Improves the value of those critical hits and remains one of the major offensive percentages.

ACCURACY

Useful offensive stat to complete once the major damage priorities are under control.

RECHARGE SPEED

Particularly useful on a power-heavy class where getting the next encounter sequence back sooner keeps the rotation moving.

ABILITY SCORES

Strength supports Hunter's physical damage while Dexterity is a useful secondary choice for Critical Severity and movement.

CHECK IN COMBAT: Your finished percentages matter more than the ratings shown while standing around in Protector's Enclave looking decorative.

LOADOUT

GEAR

Build around current physical DPS equipment with reliable damage bonuses, then use the remaining slots to complete the offensive stat spread.

HEAD

Prioritise useful offensive bonuses that can be maintained in the content you're actually running.

ARMOUR

Look for a strong damage effect supported by ratings the build can actually use.

ARMS

Use strong current damage arms and keep useful scaled-content alternatives available.

FEET

Damage bonuses are preferred when their activation condition is realistic for the fight.

WEAPONS

Use a strong current artifact weapon set appropriate to your progression and content.

RINGS

Excellent slots for correcting offensive stats or adding reliable damage bonuses.

DON'T CHASE ITEM LEVEL: A lower item-level piece with a useful damage effect can outperform something newer whose only contribution is making the number beside your character slightly more impressive.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Use enchantments to finish the stats left short by your gear, companions, insignias and boons.

OFFENSIVE RATINGS

Add Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity or Accuracy according to the finished build.

STRIKE & GUARD

Include the modern Strike and Guard enchantment slots when calculating the complete setup.

REINFORCEMENTS

Use armour kits and jewels to make smaller stat corrections without replacing otherwise excellent gear.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Use a strong current damage or debuff artifact as your active and select secondary artifacts around useful stats and set bonuses.

For bosses, coordinate the artifact with the rest of the party and have both stance rotations ready before the call begins.

PREPARE THE BURST: Artifact, encounters, mount and Daily should all be ready before the call. Hunter has enough buttons already without discovering halfway through that half of them are on cooldown.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Use companions to increase personal damage, fill missing stats and support the group where appropriate.

SINGLE TARGET

Use a strong current boss-damage companion when fighting a single target.

MULTI TARGET

An AoE or grouping companion can be useful when the majority of the fight involves packs.

PLAYER BONUSES

Choose damage bonuses and offensive stats according to what your finished Ranger still needs.

COMPANION GEAR

Use companion equipment as another way to correct ratings without sacrificing stronger player equipment bonuses.

GROUP DEBUFFS

In organised content, coordinate your companion enhancement with the rest of the party rather than stacking unnecessary copies.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Match the mount combat power to the encounter. Use a hard-hitting single-target mount on bosses and a strong AoE mount when clearing groups.

SINGLE TARGET

Use a strong current single-target combat power and place it inside the artifact burst.

MULTI TARGET

Wide-area mount powers gain considerably more value when several targets can be hit at once.

INSIGNIA BONUSES

  • ARTIFICER'S PERSUASION — useful cooldown support around artifact use.
  • TACTICIAN'S PRECISION — useful encounter cooldown support after Daily use.
  • GLADIATOR'S GUILE — useful movement and stamina support.
  • WARLORD'S MOTIVATION — useful when relying on a summoned companion for damage.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

CORE HUNTER MECHANIC

RANGED STANCE

Gives access to the Ranger's bow-based powers and lets you begin encounters safely at range.

MELEE STANCE

Switching stance changes your encounter powers, effectively opening another set of attacks while the ranged versions recover.

STANCE SWAPPING

Hunter gets much of its flexibility from moving deliberately between the two sets rather than remaining permanently in one stance.

SINGLE-TARGET TOOLKIT

AIMED SHOT

Strong ranged at-will for sustained single-target damage when you have time to complete the attack.

HINDING SHOT / HINDING STRIKE

Useful stance pair providing ranged and melee damage while contributing to Hunter's root interactions.

CONSTRICTING ARROW / STEEL BREEZE

Another useful ranged-to-melee encounter pair for keeping the stance rotation moving.

LONGSTRIDER'S SHOT / GUSHING WOUND

Flexible encounter pair that can contribute both ranged utility and melee pressure depending on the setup.

MULTI-TARGET TOOLKIT

CORDON OF ARROWS

Strong area-control option for grouped enemies and one of the powers that gives Hunter its useful AoE identity.

CONSTRICTING ARROW

Useful against groups when combined with Hunter's root-based damage interactions.

HINDING SHOT

Useful ranged encounter that transitions into Hindering Strike when you move into melee stance.

HINDING STRIKE

Strong melee follow-up when enemies are grouped tightly enough to take advantage of it.

FEAT DIRECTION

Hunter builds generally focus on improving the interaction between roots, encounters and stance swapping. Feat choices should support whichever single-target or AoE rotation you're actually using rather than mixing several unrelated playstyles together.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise offensive boon choices and use them to fill gaps in the complete Ranger build.

  • Power
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Combat Advantage
  • Accuracy
  • Forte where useful
  • Recharge Speed
  • Action Point Gain

Movement Speed is also useful for quickly changing position between ranged and melee phases.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

SINGLE TARGET

  1. ESTABLISH COMBAT ADVANTAGE Position properly around the boss before committing the main encounter sequence.
  2. BEGIN AT RANGE Open with the ranged side of your encounter powers.
  3. ARTIFACT CALL Start the coordinated burst with your major powers ready.
  4. USE RANGED ENCOUNTERS Fire the prepared ranged encounter sequence while group debuffs are active.
  5. SWITCH TO MELEE Change stance and immediately gain access to the melee versions of those powers.
  6. USE MELEE ENCOUNTERS Continue the burst with the second encounter set.
  7. MOUNT & DAILY Fit the major burst powers into the remaining artifact window.
  8. RETURN TO RANGE Swap back as powers recover and use your at-will between encounter sequences.

MULTI TARGET

  1. LET THE PACK GROUP Give the tank a moment to gather everything before covering the floor in arrows.
  2. ROOT & CONTROL Use your ranged AoE encounters against the grouped enemies.
  3. CORDON OF ARROWS Place area damage where it can hit the largest part of the pack.
  4. SWITCH STANCE Move into the melee half of the encounter sequence.
  5. HINDING STRIKE Hit the grouped enemies with the melee follow-up.
  6. CONTINUE THE MELEE SET Use the remaining available encounters before switching back.
  7. RETURN TO RANGE Reset position and continue as the ranged powers recover.
DON'T JUST CAMP THE BOW: If your build is designed around stance swapping and you're refusing to swap stance, you've essentially paid for six encounter powers and decided three of them can have the day off. 😂

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

AOE FOCUS

Hunter is the Ranger paragon to favour when strong multi-target damage is required. Build around roots and area encounters when clearing packs rather than forcing the boss loadout into everything.

SINGLE-TARGET FOCUS

For bosses, remove unnecessary pack-control powers and concentrate the loadout around encounters that deal reliable damage to one target.

RANGED SAFETY

You don't need to enter melee simply because another stance exists. Mechanics take priority. Stay at range when moving in would get you killed, then resume the stance rotation when it is safe.

GEAR SWAPS

Keep alternative equipment for scaled content, AoE and bosses. Recheck your stats whenever several pieces are changed together.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Hunter is basically for the person who looked at a normal DPS toon and thought, "This is nice, but could I have twice as many bloody buttons?"

The trick is not trying to play both stances simultaneously in your head. Learn the ranged sequence, learn the melee sequence, then join them together. Once that becomes muscle memory, Hunter starts feeling considerably less like you're operating an aircraft cockpit.
