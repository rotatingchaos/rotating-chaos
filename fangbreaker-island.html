THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Fangbreaker Island combines dangerous trash packs, avalanche hazards, two defence sections and three major boss encounters.

The dungeon punishes poor positioning heavily. Giant attacks, avalanches, icy terrain, explosive runes and boss mechanics can all knock players down or out of position very quickly.

OPENING SECTION

MOUNTAIN CLIMB

GIANT BOULDERS

Giant Beastmasters throw large boulders.

These deal heavy damage and knock players back.

A badly timed hit can knock a player into another attack and lead to a chain of knockdowns.

GIANT CHARGES

Giants can charge in a straight line.

A long red rectangle appears before the charge. Move out of it immediately.

HEAVY SWINGS

Giant hammer and axe attacks are heavily telegraphed.

Watch for the long wind-up and dodge before the swing lands.

AVALANCHES

Avalanches can sweep across the mountain path.

They cause damage and knockback.

Avoid fighting in the middle of multiple giant attacks when an avalanche is about to pass through.

FIRST BOSS

HATI

AVALANCHE COVER

When entering the arena, use the overhang/archway as cover from avalanches.

Hati may leave the arena briefly before another avalanche arrives.

When that happens, return to cover quickly.

JUMP SLAM

Hati can leap into the air and slam down.

A red circle marks the impact area. Move out before the slam lands.

FRONT CONE

Hati has a large frontal cone attack.

Avoid standing in front of the boss.

SPINES

Hati can fire spines forward.

Remaining behind the boss is generally safer for DPS.

TAIL ATTACK

Hati's tail can knock players backward.

Keep enough room behind you so a tail hit does not throw you into another mechanic or off the arena.

POSITIONING

The tank should keep Hati controlled and facing away from the group.

DPS should stay behind where possible while remaining ready to move for avalanches.

DEFENCE SECTIONS

MELTING THE ICE WALLS

The group must defend the NPC while two separate ice walls are melted.

ACTIVATE THE WALL

Someone must interact with the ice wall to begin the defence phase.

TWO SPAWN ROUTES

Enemies spawn from two cave entrances.

Watch both directions and keep the groups controlled.

DO NOT LET WAVES STACK

Kill each wave quickly.

If enemies remain alive when the next wave arrives, they can stack into a much larger and more dangerous pack.

REPEAT

The same mechanic occurs again at the second ice wall.

SECOND BOSS

DRAGON TURTLE

RED AOE

The Dragon Turtle creates very large red AoE areas.

Move out immediately.

BITE

Standing directly in front of the Dragon Turtle can result in heavy bite damage.

WILD SLAM

The Dragon Turtle can raise itself and slam onto the ice.

This grants it a Wild Slam stack.

As these stacks build, the fight becomes increasingly dangerous.

EXPLOSIVE RUNES

Explosive runes drop onto the ice arena.

Move away when they land.

These runes are also used to force the Dragon Turtle back onto the arena when it retreats into the water.

DARKNESS PHASE

The Dragon Turtle retreats into the water and the arena becomes dark.

Locate the turtle by watching for its steam breath or movement in the water.

Strike the turtle with an explosive rune to force it back onto the ice.

Missing the turtle wastes the rune.

STEAM BURN

Steam attacks can apply Steam Burn.

This increases the damage taken from future steam attacks.

Avoid repeated steam hits because the mechanic becomes more dangerous as the fight continues.

DO NOT FALL IN

The arena is slippery.

Falling into the surrounding water kills the player.

BURN WINDOWS

When the Dragon Turtle returns to the arena, maximise damage before it retreats again.

FINAL BOSS

DRUFI

CHARGE

Drufi can charge in a straight line.

A red rectangle marks the path. Move out of it.

BLIZZARD PUSH

Drufi can move to one side of the arena and unleash a blizzard.

The blizzard pushes players toward the opposite wall.

Run toward Drufi and against the push to avoid being forced into the dangerous back wall.

ICICLES

Drufi summons icicles from the ground.

Dodge the initial eruption.

If an icicle traps a player, break that icicle to free them.

If nobody is trapped, do not destroy the icicle.

The group needs those icicles later for protection.

SHARED-DAMAGE ARROW

A player can be targeted with an arrow marker.

The group should stack around that player to share the incoming damage.

WINTER'S DEMISE

When Drufi calls for the group to hide behind an icicle, move behind one immediately.

Failing to use an icicle for cover can wipe the group.

This is why unused icicles should be preserved.

KEEP DRUFI AWAY FROM THE ICICLES

Once icicles are available, position the boss away from them.

This reduces the chance of accidental AoE damage destroying the group's future cover.

SWORD ATTACKS

Drufi's large sword swings are telegraphed.

Stay behind the boss where possible and dodge the attacks.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Keep giant packs controlled during the mountain climb.

Position Hati and Drufi away from the party.

During the defence phases, gather enemy waves so the group can burn them efficiently.

HEALER

Expect heavy damage during giant packs, avalanches and Dragon Turtle steam phases.

During Drufi, keep the group healthy for the shared-damage arrow mechanic.

DPS

Avoid standing in front of Hati and Drufi.

Burn defence waves quickly so enemies do not accumulate.

During Dragon Turtle darkness phases, help use explosive runes to bring the boss back.

During Drufi, do not accidentally destroy useful icicles.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Getting chain-knocked by giant boulders and charges.

Failing to reach cover before Hati's avalanche.

Letting defence-wave enemies stack faster than the group can kill them.

Falling into the water during Dragon Turtle.

Missing explosive-rune shots during the Dragon Turtle darkness phase.

Building too many Steam Burn or Wild Slam effects.

Destroying Drufi's icicles before Winter's Demise.

Failing to stack on the shared-damage arrow.

Failing to hide behind an icicle during Winter's Demise.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Fangbreaker is mostly a test of whether everyone can resist standing exactly where the dungeon is trying to murder them.

Hati: hide from avalanches and stay away from the angry end of the manticore.

Dragon Turtle: don't fall in the sea and don't waste the explosive runes.

Drufi: the icicles are not decorative. Keep them alive unless somebody is trapped inside one, because five minutes later everyone will suddenly be very emotionally attached to them.
