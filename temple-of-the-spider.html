THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Master Temple of the Spider is built around three mechanically demanding boss encounters.

The first boss focuses on controlling barrels, Choking Gas, spider adds, webs and clones.

The second boss revolves around managing Ritual Strength, timed add phases, overlapping red areas and healer-specific mechanics.

The final boss requires the group to rotate line interceptions, break cocoons quickly and manage stacking debuffs while avoiding increasingly dangerous arena mechanics.

FIRST BOSS

WEBS ◆ BARRELS ◆ CLONES

LARGE RED AREAS

Large red areas regularly target players.

Move these away from the group and, most importantly, away from barrels.

A red area hitting a barrel can trigger an explosion and contribute to Choking Gas.

SPIDER ADDS & WEBS

When the boss announces either "Trapped in the web, the prey squirms" or "You are but an insect among spiders", spider adds appear.

The spiders place webs on the ground. Standing in a web causes damage and slows the affected player.

Move out of webs immediately.

Spider adds can deal extremely heavy frontal damage. DPS should avoid standing in front of them while the tank controls aggro.

CLONE PHASE

At approximately 75% health the boss disappears and three clones appear.

The clone phase can occur again later in the encounter, including around 50% health.

Kill the clones quickly. Crowd control and AoE damage are particularly useful here.

EXPLODING BARRELS

Red barrels can appear during the encounter.

When activated, a red circle begins filling around the barrel.

Destroy the barrel before the timer completes.

If the barrel explodes, the group gains a stack of Choking Gas.

Exploding barrels can also trigger nearby barrels, creating a chain reaction across the arena.

CHOKING GAS

Choking Gas causes the entire group to take damage over time regardless of their position.

Additional stacks increase the pressure on the healer.

Prevent stacks by destroying activated barrels and keeping targeted red areas away from them.

TRAPS

Red trap areas can appear during clone phases.

Walking into one causes a long stun and damage over time.

Avoid the trap areas while dealing with the clones and barrels.

SECOND BOSS

RITUAL STRENGTH

WEBS

Webs on the arena apply a Tangled effect.

Tangled slows the player and deals damage. Avoid remaining inside webs where possible.

RED AREAS

Avoid the red areas around the boss.

Being hit can apply a poison damage-over-time effect.

TIMED DEMONS

At approximately 80%, 50% and 20% boss health, demons spawn around the arena.

These adds must be killed within roughly 10 seconds.

Any demon that survives the timer disappears and gives the group a stack of Ritual Strength.

Save burst damage, Dailies and Mount powers for these phases.

RITUAL STRENGTH

Ritual Strength increases the danger of the boss's unavoidable group-wide damage.

Too many stacks can make the periodic Roar lethal.

ROAR

The boss periodically performs an unavoidable group-wide Roar.

Players should be healthy before the Roar lands, particularly if the group has accumulated Ritual Strength.

LARGE LINKED RED AREAS

Players can be targeted with very large red circles connected by a line.

The targeted players must move apart so the circles do not overlap.

Other party members should reposition to stay clear of both areas.

STRANGLE

Below approximately 50% health, the boss can randomly strangle a player and prevent them from moving.

If the restrained player also receives one of the large red circles, the other targeted player must move far enough away to prevent overlap.

FRONTAL RED LINE

A narrow red attack appears in front of the boss.

Non-tanks caught inside can take lethal damage.

Stay away from the boss's frontal attack unless you are the tank.

PURPLE GHOSTS

Purple ghosts can appear as a damage check.

Killing them can reduce Ritual Strength.

HEALER GHOST

A special ghost can appear during the fight with its own health bar.

The healer must actively heal the ghost to full health.

Successfully healing it removes the Ritual Strength gained when it appeared.

If the healer fails, the ghost transforms into a spider and disappears, leaving the Ritual Strength stack active.

Passive healing effects may not heal the ghost. The healer needs to target it with active healing.

FINAL BOSS

COCOONS ◆ LINES ◆ DEBUFFS

COCOON

A player can be trapped inside a cocoon.

The group has approximately 10 seconds to destroy the cocoon and free the trapped player.

Failure to break the cocoon results in the trapped player dying permanently.

Cocoons occur roughly every 30 seconds, although the timing can vary.

DPS should keep enough burst damage available to destroy each cocoon quickly.

INTERCEPTING LINES

Coloured lines connect the boss to players and must be intercepted by party members.

Intercepting a line gives the player a stacking debuff.

The group should rotate who intercepts the lines rather than allowing one player to accumulate every stack.

RED LINE ◆ WORTHY SACRIFICE

Intercepting the red line applies Worthy Sacrifice.

Worthy Sacrifice deals damage over time, with the damage increasing significantly as stacks accumulate.

Tanks are better suited to taking early red-line stacks because they can survive the increasing damage more easily.

GREEN LINE

Intercepting the green line applies a debuff that reduces incoming healing.

The healing reduction becomes increasingly severe with additional stacks.

Tanks should avoid accumulating excessive green stacks because reducing their incoming healing makes tank survival considerably harder.

SHARE THE STACKS

Rotate line interceptions throughout the party.

A useful approach is to allow everyone to take one appropriate stack before beginning a second rotation.

BELOW 50%

Additional mechanics appear once the boss reaches approximately 50% health.

Red areas begin following players around the arena.

These deal extremely heavy damage and can become lethal when combined with damage-over-time debuffs.

PILLARS

Pillar-like attacks appear around the arena during the later phase.

Avoid these alongside the moving red areas and webs.

TELEPORT BURN

When the boss teleports, nearby players can receive a burn effect that deals damage over time for roughly 10 seconds.

Ranged DPS can reduce the risk by maintaining distance when possible.

PRIORITY

When a cocoon appears, freeing the trapped player is the immediate priority.

Continue rotating line interceptions and avoiding arena damage between cocoons.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Control spider adds and keep dangerous frontal attacks facing away from the party.

Control adds during the second boss and keep the group clear of frontal attacks.

During the final boss, tanks are better suited to taking early red-line Worthy Sacrifice stacks.

Avoid excessive green-line stacks because they reduce incoming healing.

HEALER

Choking Gas during the first boss creates increasing group-wide healing pressure.

During the second boss, actively heal the special ghost to full to remove Ritual Strength.

Prepare the group for the unavoidable Roar, especially when Ritual Strength stacks are active.

During the final boss, watch players carrying Worthy Sacrifice because its damage increases with additional stacks.

DPS

Bring enough AoE or crowd control to deal with first-boss clones and spider adds.

Save burst damage for the timed demons during the second boss.

During the final boss, always keep enough damage available for the next cocoon.

A dead boss is lovely. A permanently dead teammate because everyone blew their Encounters five seconds before the cocoon is considerably less lovely.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Dropping first-boss red areas onto barrels and triggering explosions.

Allowing exploding barrels to chain together and building multiple Choking Gas stacks.

Standing in webs or traps.

Failing to control first-boss clones and spider adds.

Failing the second-boss demon damage checks and accumulating too much Ritual Strength.

Overlapping the linked red circles.

The healer failing to fully heal the special ghost.

Standing in the second boss's frontal red attack.

Failing to destroy a final-boss cocoon within the time limit.

Allowing one player to accumulate too many line debuff stacks.

Ignoring later-phase red areas while focusing on the cocoon.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

First boss: red goes away from the barrels. Activated barrel gets murdered before it explodes. Simple enough until five people decide the barrel looks like an excellent meeting point.

Choking Gas hits everybody. You cannot outrun it, hide from it or pretend it's somebody else's problem. Stop creating stacks.

Second boss: the timed demons are the damage check. Save your good shit for them.

Healer ghost appears? DPS leave it alone. The healer actually needs to heal the bloody thing.

Final boss: cocoon beats everything else on the priority list. Boss damage can wait ten seconds. Your trapped teammate cannot.

And rotate the lines. One heroic volunteer collecting every debuff is not teamwork. It's an increasingly elaborate suicide attempt.
