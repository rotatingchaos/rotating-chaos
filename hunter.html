HOW IT WORKS

HUNTER EXPLAINED

Hunter makes full use of the Ranger's ranged and melee toolkit. Switching stance changes the available encounter powers, effectively giving the toon a second encounter bar.

Begin at range, use the ranged side of the toolkit, switch into melee for another encounter sequence and return to range while powers recover.

Hunter is particularly useful against groups because roots, area attacks and stance swapping provide a strong dedicated AoE toolkit.

TOOLKIT

RANGED & MELEE PAIRS

Single-target options include Aimed Shot, Hindering Shot / Hindering Strike, Constricting Arrow / Steel Breeze and Longstrider's Shot / Gushing Wound.

Multi-target play makes strong use of Cordon of Arrows, Constricting Arrow, Hindering Shot and the melee Hindering Strike follow-up.

OTHER OPTIONS

AOE, BOSSES & SAFETY

Hunter is the Ranger paragon to favour when strong multi-target damage is required. Build around roots and area encounters for packs rather than forcing the boss loadout into everything.

For bosses, remove unnecessary pack-control powers and concentrate the loadout around reliable single-target damage.

Mechanics always take priority over stance swapping. If entering melee would get you killed, stay at range and resume the normal rotation when it becomes safe.

EQUIPMENT

DON'T CHASE ITEM LEVEL

Build around current physical DPS equipment with useful damage effects, then use the remaining slots to finish the offensive stat spread.

A lower item-level piece with a genuinely useful damage effect can outperform a newer item whose only achievement is making the number beside your toon bigger.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Hunter is basically for the person who looked at a normal DPS toon and thought, "This is nice, but could I have twice as many bloody buttons?"

Don't try to learn both stances simultaneously. Learn the ranged sequence. Learn the melee sequence. Then bolt the two together.

Once the swaps become muscle memory, Hunter stops feeling quite so much like you're trying to land an aircraft while also shooting things with a bow.
