HOW IT WORKS

HELLBRINGER EXPLAINED

Hellbringer is the Warlock's damage paragon and revolves around magical damage, Curse management and Soul Sparks.

The build changes noticeably between bosses and packs. Multi-target repeatedly applies and consumes Curse, while single-target concentrates damage into one enemy during the group's main burst windows.

Soul Sparks are another major resource. Build them through combat and spend them deliberately for damage and encounter cooldown reduction.

CURRENT OPTIONS

ENDGAME CONFIGURATIONS

Current Module 33.5 options include Laughing Void + Lightning Flash, Laughing Void + Swift Synergy and Scintillant + Swift Synergy.

These are complete build configurations rather than interchangeable individual pieces. Changing the set or bonus changes the stat requirements elsewhere on the toon.

EXTRA SWEATY SHIT

ANIMATION CANCELLING

Hellbringer can cancel the ending animation of certain powers with tactical movement. When performed correctly, the effect still fires while allowing the next action to begin sooner.

Dark Helix is the main power worth learning this with. Faster casts improve damage, Action Point generation and Soul Spark generation.

Hellish Rebuke can also be cancelled quickly when reapplying Curse during tightly optimised boss rotations.

LEARN TO WALK BEFORE DOING INTERPRETIVE DANCE: Get the normal rotation working first. Animation cancelling is only useful when you're still landing everything inside the damage window.

OTHER OPTIONS

STARTER, MIDGAME & SCALED

Hellbringer does not require a finished Master-content account before it becomes playable. Campaign equipment provides a realistic starter route and advanced-content gear can bridge the gap into the endgame setup.

Keep Astral Raider Arms from Wildspace available for scaled content. Their usefulness there can outweigh simply equipping a newer higher-item-level pair.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Hellbringer looks like random purple shit flying everywhere until you understand what the Warlock is actually doing. Then you realise there's an alarming amount of resource management happening underneath all the demonic fireworks.

Learn Curse and Soul Spark management first. Once the normal rotation becomes automatic, start worrying about animation cancelling and squeezing more into the burst.
