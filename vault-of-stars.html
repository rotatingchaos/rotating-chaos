THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Vault of Stars is a mechanics-heavy dungeon built around positioning, add control and recognising visual markers.

Important recurring mechanics include Corpse Flowers, stack and spread markers, light and darkness zones, timed sections and dangerous environmental effects.

Before the first boss, protect the mage while clearing the enemy groups blocking the route.

If Corpse Flowers appear, prioritise them. Leaving them alive allows them to continue spawning more enemies.

FIRST BOSS

ROYAL GARDENER

This encounter contains heavy add pressure and several mechanics involving the Corpse Flowers around the arena.

SMALL RED CIRCLE ◆ DOUBLE ARROW

A player can receive a small red circle with a double-arrow marker.

Move the marker away from the centre and away from the group.

When it activates, the player is briefly stunned and purple mushrooms appear at that location.

The mushrooms create a damaging and slowing area. Dodge away immediately after the stun ends.

UNBRIDLED GROWTH ◆ GREEN LINE

When the boss becomes untargetable, it can move to the side of the arena and create a green line between itself and a Corpse Flower.

A player must stand between the boss and the targeted flower to intercept the line.

If the line reaches the flower, the flower grows larger, produces additional enemies and creates poisonous AoEs.

Failing this mechanic also increases the Corrupted Soil meter.

STUBBORN WEEDS ◆ RED LINE

The boss can attach a wide red line to a player.

Position yourself so a Corpse Flower is directly between you and the boss.

The attack should hit the flower instead of you.

Successfully doing this reduces the size of the flower.

If the player is hit instead, they take heavy damage and the Corrupted Soil meter increases.

VOLATILE CORPSE

A Volatile Corpse can spawn and tether itself to a player with a red line.

The targeted player is slowed while the corpse moves toward them.

Kill the Volatile Corpse before it reaches the targeted player.

If it reaches them, it explodes and increases Corrupted Soil.

CORRUPTED SOIL

Failed mechanics increase the Corrupted Soil meter.

Each stack increases enemy damage and damage resistance.

Prevent the meter from building by completing the mechanics correctly.

DISPLACER BEAST PHASE

At approximately 50% boss health, a Displacer Beast appears and the Royal Gardener becomes untargetable.

The beast jumps onto and stuns a random player.

The Royal Gardener then appears at the outside of the arena and creates a dark red line toward the stunned player.

TANK THE RED LINE

The tank must move between the boss and the stunned player to intercept the line.

The line deals extremely heavy AoE damage, so the tank should intercept it away from the rest of the group.

The healer should keep the stunned player alive while the mechanic is being resolved.

SHAMBLING MOUNDS

After the Displacer Beast dies, the boss returns and the previous mechanics continue.

Shambling Mounds can then begin moving toward Corpse Flowers.

Kill the mounds before they reach the flowers.

If they reach them, they activate and become additional high-health enemies.

BETWEEN BOSSES

DARK FOREST

Follow the moving light through the forest.

Stay inside the illuminated area.

Moving outside the light causes heavy damage over time.

The light periodically stops while enemies spawn. Kill the enemies before continuing with the light.

SECOND BOSS

PRINCE OF FROST

RED ARCS

The boss creates red arc-shaped danger zones around himself.

These become more numerous as his health decreases.

At approximately 75% health there can be two arcs, at 50% three, and near 25% up to four.

Position yourself in the safe gaps between them.

HYPOTHERMIA ◆ STACK

Hypothermia is a shared-damage mechanic.

Stack together to split the incoming damage.

Do not resolve Hypothermia while standing inside one of the boss's red arc areas.

SINGLE RED CIRCLE ◆ DOUBLE ARROW

If one player receives a large red circle with a double arrow, that player must move away from everyone else.

When the circle activates, everyone inside it becomes frozen.

Once the targeted player freezes, the rest of the group must quickly break the ice.

If the targeted player stacks with the party, the entire group can become frozen and wipe.

EVERYONE GETS A CIRCLE ◆ SPREAD

If every player receives a circle without the arrow marker, spread apart.

When the circles activate, they leave damaging areas on the ground.

Move away immediately after they drop.

The circles can overlap slightly to conserve arena space, but do not stack them directly on other players.

FINAL 25%

Below approximately 25% health, the boss performs mechanics more quickly.

Expect faster overlaps and finish the fight cleanly.

TIMED SECTION

THE MAZE

Navigate through three open combat arenas and clear all required enemies in each.

ENCROACHING DARKNESS

A bar called Encroaching Darkness appears beneath the minimap.

This is your time limit.

If the bar empties, the maze fails and the group is returned to the beginning.

CORPSE FLOWERS

Corpse Flowers appear around the outside of the combat arenas.

Kill them quickly or they continue spawning additional enemies.

THIRD ARENA ◆ VOID POOLS

Void pools appear on the ground in the third arena.

Avoid them while clearing the enemies.

FINISHING THE MAZE

After clearing the third arena, kill the three Shambling Mounds before the stairs.

Run up the stairs and interact with the light before the Encroaching Darkness timer expires.

FINAL BOSS

QUEEN'S GUARD

The final encounter consists of three golems fought one after another.

Light and dark areas continuously move around the arena.

Correctly positioning each golem is the central mechanic.

PRESERVED BY DARKNESS ◆ MOVE TO LIGHT

When the golem is Preserved by Darkness, the tank must position it inside a light area.

Only attack once it is correctly positioned in the light.

SHELTERED IN STARLIGHT ◆ MOVE TO DARK

When the golem is Sheltered in Starlight, move it into a dark or void area.

The boss can then be damaged normally.

ATTACKING IN THE WRONG ZONE

Do not attack while the boss is positioned in the wrong type of area.

Your attacks deal no damage to the boss and instead cause damage to you.

Damage-over-time and bleed effects can continue triggering this, so be careful when the required zone changes.

DARK ZONE DAMAGE

Players standing inside a dark zone take damage over time.

DPS should avoid remaining inside it unnecessarily while the tank positions the boss.

HYPOTHERMIA

The encounter also uses a shared-damage Hypothermia mechanic.

Stack together to split the damage when it appears.

THIRD GOLEM ◆ BLACK HOLES

The third golem creates black holes around the arena.

These pull players toward them.

The closer you are to a black hole, the more damage over time you take.

Move away quickly.

The tank must account for the pull while trying to position the boss correctly in the required light or dark zone.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

During the Royal Gardener, intercept the dark red line between the boss and the player stunned by the Displacer Beast.

Take the line away from the rest of the group.

During Queen's Guard, move each golem between light and dark areas according to its current protection mechanic.

Be ready for the third golem's black holes to interfere with your positioning.

HEALER

During the Displacer Beast phase, keep the stunned player alive through the damage-over-time effect.

Be ready for heavy group damage during Hypothermia mechanics.

During Queen's Guard, expect additional damage when players must fight near dark zones or are affected by black holes.

DPS

Prioritise Corpse Flowers, Volatile Corpses, Shambling Mounds and other dangerous adds.

Royal Gardener requires strong add control, so AoE damage is extremely useful.

During Prince of Frost, know the difference between stack and spread markers.

During Queen's Guard, stop attacking immediately if the boss is in the wrong zone.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Ignoring Corpse Flowers and allowing enemy waves to multiply.

Failing Royal Gardener mechanics and building too many Corrupted Soil stacks.

Not intercepting Unbridled Growth before it reaches a flower.

Failing to position a flower between the player and Royal Gardener during Stubborn Weeds.

Allowing a Volatile Corpse to reach its targeted player.

Tank failing to intercept the Displacer Beast red-line attack.

Leaving the illuminated area during the Dark Forest.

Stacking when targeted by Prince of Frost's single double-arrow freeze marker.

Spreading during Hypothermia instead of stacking.

Resolving Hypothermia inside one of Prince of Frost's red arcs.

Running out of time during the maze.

Attacking a Queen's Guard golem while it is positioned in the wrong light or dark zone.

Getting pulled into the third golem's black holes.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Vault of Stars has apparently decided that simply hitting bosses wasn't stressful enough, so you're now doing gardening, traffic management and interior lighting as well.

Royal Gardener: kill the flowers, intercept the green line, use the flowers to block the red line, and don't let Corrupted Soil become everybody's problem.

Prince of Frost: Hypothermia means cuddle. Double-arrow circle means absolutely do NOT cuddle. Please learn the difference before introducing the entire guild to an ice cube.

Queen's Guard: if it wants light, put it in light. If it wants dark, put it in dark. If you're hitting it and you're the one taking damage, congratulations — you've fucked up the lighting.
