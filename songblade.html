SONGBLADE

Songs ◆ Burst ◆ Performance

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Songblade is the Bard's DPS path, combining encounter attacks, Performance songs, party support and extremely strong burst windows. The build rewards preparation far more than simply pressing whatever comes off cooldown first.

Before combat you'll establish your important songs, prepare the buffs needed for the fight and then organise encounters around Activate Exploit and the party's artifact call. Bosses and mob packs use different songs and can also use different encounters.

The result is a DPS toon that can deal serious personal damage while still contributing useful buffs and debuffs to the entire group.

SONGBLADE RULE: The fight starts before you hit the boss. Get the bloody songs up first. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Build toward a balanced offensive spread rather than pouring everything into one percentage. Combat Advantage only pays off when you're actually positioned to receive it.

1 ◆ POWER One of the core offensive percentages and a major part of the Songblade damage setup.
2 ◆ COMBAT ADVANTAGE Extremely strong when you're correctly flanking the target.
3 ◆ CRITICAL STRIKE Build alongside Critical Severity rather than massively over-investing in one and ignoring the other.
4 ◆ CRITICAL SEVERITY Strong offensive percentage, but remember the total percentage ceiling when balancing the final build.
5 ◆ ACCURACY Still useful, but generally the final offensive percentage to finish after the stronger damage stats.
ABILITY SCORES: Strength + Dexterity. If Critical Severity is already pushing beyond what you can use, move some Dexterity into Charisma instead.

LOADOUT

GEAR

Current Songblade gear should maximise the offensive percentages you actually need while maintaining enough item level for endgame content. Don't automatically equip something just because the item level number is bigger.

HEAD

Current DPS headpiece with a useful offensive percentage or reliable damage bonus.

ARMOUR

Prioritise a strong damage effect while balancing Power, Combat Advantage and critical percentages.

ARMS

Use current damage arms appropriate to the content. Scaled content can make lower-item-level damage effects surprisingly valuable.

FEET

Use a strong current damage bonus or whichever piece completes the offensive percentages your build is missing.

WEAPONS

Current artifact weapons are the endgame target. Strong recent alternatives remain perfectly usable while progressing.

NECK & WAIST

Choose a current damage set with a bonus that remains reliable during the content you're actually running.

RINGS

Use rings to finish Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy while gaining useful damage bonuses.

SHIRT & TROUSERS

Prioritise useful offensive effects and use these slots to complete any remaining percentage gaps.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments and modifications are there to finish the build rather than dictate it. Adjust them whenever changing another piece causes one offensive percentage to become overfilled.

  • Combat Advantage where required
  • Accuracy to finish the offensive spread
  • Power where the build is short
  • Critical Severity where useful
  • Recharge or Action Point support if preferred over movement
  • Content-specific overloads can replace general-purpose options

Armour kits and jewels should simply target whatever ratings your particular version of the build still needs.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Songblade damage is heavily concentrated into coordinated burst windows, making the party's artifact call one of the most important parts of the build.

PRIMARY

Use a current support artifact that contributes to the group's enemy debuffs during the coordinated artifact call.

SECONDARIES

Strong current options include Nightflame Censer, Demon Skull, Broken Halo and Bloodthirst Chalice depending on the final stat balance and available set bonuses.

BURST MATTERS: The artifact window is where Songblade damage goes absolutely feral. Going into it with everything on cooldown rather defeats the point. 😂

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

In endgame master content, expect the summoned companion to contribute to the entire group rather than being selected purely for your own personal damage.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Use a strong current DPS-support companion capable of improving party stats, increasing group damage or helping establish Combat Advantage.

ACUTE SENSES

Useful offensive companion bonus and available from several companions.

TAMED RAPTOR

Excellent coordinated Power option when other members of the party are also using it.

NEVERWINTER KNIGHT

Strong general damage companion bonus.

MINSC

Another useful offensive companion bonus for the Songblade loadout.

BATIRI

Useful option where its boss-focused damage bonus applies.

Companion gear should finish whichever offensive ratings are still short after equipment, enchantments and party buffs are accounted for.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mounts contribute damage, cooldown support and additional offensive ratings. For Songblade, cooldown-related insignia bonuses can also be important to fitting the full encounter sequence inside the artifact window.

SINGLE TARGET COMBAT POWER

Strong options include Demonic Gravehound, Bigby's Hand, Grubshank the Burdened, Legendary Giant Toad, Golden Warhorse, Tunnel Vision and Legendary Hellfire Engine.

EQUIP POWER

Ferocity / Providence or Pack Tactics can be used depending on your final build and party setup.

INSIGNIAS

Balance offensive ratings through Skill, Brutality and Dominance rather than blindly copying an exact distribution.

COLLARS

Encounter damage, Critical Severity, movement and stamina utility are all useful choices.

COOLDOWN SUPPORT: Cavalry's Haste and Tactician's Precision are particularly valuable when trying to squeeze the complete burst combination into the artifact window.

COMBAT

POWERS, SONGS & FEATS

Songblade isn't just an encounter rotation. Performance songs form part of the setup and change depending on whether you're fighting a boss or clearing mobs.

CORE SONGS

TAILWIND MAMBO

Activate before combat as part of the standard Songblade setup. Keep it prepared rather than remembering halfway through the fight that you're supposed to be a Bard.

BALLAD OF THE HERO

Primary boss song. Activate after Tailwind when preparing for single-target combat.

BALLAD OF THE WITCH

Swap to Witch when dealing with mob packs and multi-target encounters.

DAILIES

LORE

Boss-focused Daily and an important part of the single-target burst setup. Prepare Battle Research stacks where possible before the fight.

INSPIRATION

Switch toward Inspiration when dealing with mobs alongside the Witch song setup.

MOB ENCOUNTER SWAPS

DUET

Useful encounter swap when moving from the boss setup into multi-target combat.

VOLTI SUBITO

Another strong mob-focused encounter option for the Songblade.

CONTRE

Situational additional encounter choice for mob-heavy content.

ACTIVATE EXPLOIT

Encounter timing should be organised around the Activate Exploit buff. Avoid throwing every encounter away immediately before the buff or artifact window becomes available.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise boons that improve your offensive percentages and bonuses against the enemies you're fighting.

  • Power
  • Combat Advantage
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Accuracy where required
  • Enemy-specific damage bonuses
  • Useful survivability once offensive needs are covered

Blessed boon options are particularly useful when you're regularly running with a healer providing healing-over-time effects.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

COMBAT FLOW

BEFORE A BOSS

  1. TAILWIND MAMBO Establish Tailwind before entering combat.
  2. BALLAD OF THE HERO Follow Tailwind with Hero for the boss setup.
  3. PREPARE BATTLE RESEARCH Where possible, enter the fight with five Battle Research stacks ready for Lore.
  4. PREPARE ACTIVATE EXPLOIT Arrange your encounter cooldowns so the buff isn't wasted.

ARTIFACT CALL

  1. ENTER WITH ENCOUNTERS READY Don't burn the important encounters immediately before the call.
  2. GROUP DEBUFFS Begin the coordinated burst as the party's support artifacts and mount effects land.
  3. LORE Use the boss Daily as part of the burst sequence.
  4. ENCOUNTER BURST Dump the prepared encounter sequence while the group's damage debuffs are active.
  5. COOLDOWN RESET / SECOND PASS With the appropriate cooldown support, squeeze additional encounter uses into the same burst window.
  6. RESET Outside the artifact call, rebuild resources and organise cooldowns for the next burst rather than mindlessly dumping them.

MOBS

  1. TAILWIND MAMBO Keep your pre-combat song setup.
  2. BALLAD OF THE WITCH Use Witch instead of Hero for packs.
  3. INSPIRATION Swap the Daily for multi-target combat.
  4. DUET / VOLTI SUBITO / CONTRE Switch encounters toward the mob-focused setup as required.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

HERO ◆ WITCH

Hero for bosses, Witch for mob-heavy fights. Change the song to suit the encounter instead of forcing one setup everywhere.

LORE ◆ INSPIRATION

Lore is the boss choice; Inspiration is the mob-oriented swap.

DUET / VOLTI / CONTRE

Use these as encounter swaps when the fight needs stronger multi-target coverage.

STAT FLEXIBILITY

Don't panic over being a few percentage points away from somebody else's exact setup. Party buffs and temporary effects constantly alter the final numbers.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Songblade has one of those rotations where somebody watching from the outside thinks you're just running around playing a lute until the artifact call happens and half the boss's health suddenly disappears. 🤣

Preparation is the whole trick. Tailwind, the correct Ballad, Battle Research, Activate Exploit and encounter cooldowns all need to be thought about before the burst begins.

The coordinated artifact window can produce several times your normal damage output, so missing it hurts this build considerably more than slightly imperfect damage between calls.
