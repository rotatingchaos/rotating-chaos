HOW IT WORKS

DREADNOUGHT EXPLAINED

Dreadnought is the Fighter's damage paragon and revolves around heavy physical hits and active Vengeance management.

Blocking through Seethe converts Stamina into Vengeance. This means the shield remains part of your DPS toolkit even though you're not playing the tank paragon.

Prepare Vengeance before the main damage window instead of desperately trying to generate it halfway through one.

LOADOUTS

SINGLE TARGET VS AOE

Don't force the boss setup into every pack. Single target uses Heavy Slash, Commander's Strike, Bull Charge, Anvil of Doom and Mow Down.

Multi-target swaps into Reave, Onslaught, Shield Slam, Tremor and Earthshaker.

SWAPS

GEAR & SURVIVAL

Keep alternative equipment available for scaled content, AoE and boss encounters. One equipment set does not automatically perform best everywhere.

The Fighter's shield also gives Dreadnought more defensive flexibility than many DPS classes. Use it when a mechanic requires it rather than dying because blocking might interrupt your beautiful parse.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Dreadnought looks straightforward until you're juggling Vengeance, positioning, cooldowns, artifact timing and a shield while standing approximately six inches from whatever enormous bastard the dungeon has thrown at you.

Learn the single-target sequence first. Once it becomes automatic, concentrate on preparing Vengeance before calls and keeping your burst properly coordinated.
