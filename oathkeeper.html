PALADIN ◆ HEALER

OATHKEEPER

Healing ◆ Shields ◆ Group Support

Overview Stats Gear Enchantments Artifacts Companions Mounts Powers & Feats Boons Rotation

THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Oathkeeper is the Paladin's healing paragon, specialising in powerful healing backed by enormous protective shields. Instead of simply repairing damage after it happens, a well-played Oathkeeper can give the party a substantial buffer before the next attack even lands.

The build revolves around pushing Power and Outgoing Healing first, then building Critical Severity and Critical Strike to strengthen critical heals and shields. Once your healing is comfortably strong, several pieces can be switched toward group damage support.

That makes Oathkeeper particularly flexible: it can be built for maximum shielding when the group needs safety, or sacrifice some of that frankly unnecessary amount of protection to help the party kill things faster.

THE OATHKEEPER JOB: Heal the damage, shield the next bit and make the health bars look so unnecessarily enormous that the tank starts feeling invincible. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

1 ◆ POWER One of the two main healing priorities. Push Power as high as practical alongside Outgoing Healing.
2 ◆ OUTGOING HEALING Directly improves healing output and therefore the strength of the shields generated from your healing.
3 ◆ CRITICAL SEVERITY Particularly valuable because stronger critical heals mean larger maximum shields. Build this once Power and Outgoing Healing are healthy.
4 ◆ CRITICAL STRIKE Increases the frequency of critical healing. Oathkeeper naturally gains some Critical Strike through Forte, so balance it against Critical Severity rather than blindly stacking it.
5 ◆ RECHARGE / UTILITY Useful after the primary healing percentages are established and can make the overall healing flow more comfortable.
WATCH THE TOTAL PERCENTAGE: Ratings are only the route to the percentage. Don't keep piling rating into a stat that's already where you need it while another important percentage is sitting there starving.

LOADOUT

GEAR

Build the main loadout around healing and shielding first. The Oathkeeper has enough flexibility that several slots can later be switched toward group support once you no longer need every available healing bonus.

HEAD

Prioritise useful healing percentages or reliable support bonuses. Avoid chasing item level where the actual bonus does nothing for the build.

ARMOUR

Use a strong healer armour bonus for maximum shielding, or a group-support piece when the party benefits more from additional utility.

ARMS

Healing-stat arms are the safe option. For organised groups, support alternatives can be used to reduce enemy stats and improve party damage.

FEET

Use the slot to improve whichever core healing percentage is still behind or to gain useful movement and support.

WEAPONS

A healing-focused weapon set works for the standard build. Masterwork-style group-support weapons become attractive in coordinated endgame groups.

NECK & WAIST

Standard healing sets maximise personal output. Damage-debuff sets are a strong alternative when deliberately building for party support.

RINGS

Balance your healing percentages first. Support rings that increase damage taken by the target can replace a healing option when your group doesn't need maximum shields.

ABILITY SCORES

Prioritise Wisdom and Dexterity. If Critical Severity exceeds its useful total, move some Dexterity investment into Charisma.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments should finish the stat balance created by the rest of the build rather than being treated as a completely separate setup.

  • Power where the finished Power percentage needs help
  • Critical Severity for stronger critical healing and shields
  • Critical Strike where required to balance critical healing
  • Awareness or defensive utility where appropriate
  • Outgoing Healing through available bonuses and collars

For weapon modifications, Cure Wounds, Power and Recharge Speed are useful options. Armour kits and jewels should then be adjusted around the exact percentages your own character is missing.

COMBAT ENCHANTMENT: A shield-focused option is ideal for maximum protection. A damage-support alternative can be used in stronger groups, but you're deliberately trading some maximum shielding to get it.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

The primary artifact slot is one of the easiest places for an Oathkeeper to contribute directly to the group's damage. Use a current support artifact and coordinate it with the rest of the party's artifact call.

PRIMARY

Use a strong group-support or enemy-debuff artifact appropriate to the content and the rest of the party.

SECONDARIES

Nightflame Censer, Demon Skull, Bloodthirst Chalice and Portable Spelljammer Detector are examples from the Module 32.5 healing setup. Secondary pieces can be changed to fit your available artifact set and stat requirements.

GROUP SUPPORT: Your artifact isn't decorative jewellery. Drop the bastard with the group's artifact call so the DPS can actually use the debuff. 😂

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Endgame Oathkeepers generally get more value from a summoned group-support companion than from a companion attempting to heal alongside them. Your own healing output absolutely dwarfs companion healing.

SUMMONED

Prefer a current support companion that improves party stats, increases group damage or helps maintain Combat Advantage.

PLAYER BONUSES

Alpha Compy, Tamed Raptor, Wolf, Zariel or Soradiel and Dog are examples of bonuses used to build Power and critical healing stats. Equivalent bonuses can be substituted to fit your build.

COMPANION GEAR

Use companion equipment to complete your healing percentages. Incoming-healing options can also be useful where appropriate.

ENHANCEMENT

A personal healing enhancement is useful while building the toon. Endgame groups may instead want a support enhancement that increases the party's overall damage.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

The mount setup balances healing percentages with utility and group support.

INSIGNIAS

A healing-focused spread can use Brutality, Dominance, Rhythm and Aggression insignias, adjusting the exact mix to match your finished percentages.

INSIGNIA BONUSES

Useful options include healing, survivability and support bonuses. Mender's Covenant, Ally's Resilience, Magistrate's Patience, Master's Cruelty and Survivalist Expertise are among the viable choices.

COLLARS

Outgoing Healing, Critical Severity and Movement Speed are useful options alongside whichever utility collars best suit the content.

COMBAT POWER

Use a group-support mount combat power in organised content and fire it with the party's burst window.

COMBAT

POWERS & PLAYSTYLE

Oathkeeper has a broad healing toolkit, so there isn't one button layout that should be mindlessly used in every encounter. The standard setup handles normal healing, while several powers become excellent swaps for specific mechanics.

CORE HEALING

CURE WOUNDS

Reliable healing tool and a useful option for weapon modifications. Use it for efficient healing when the situation doesn't require a larger emergency response.

DIVINE TOUCH

Particularly useful when players are spread apart and you only need to heal one or two targets at a time. It provides a strong heal and shield without requiring the entire group to stack.

CIRCLE OF DIVINITY

Strong option when the fight allows you to remain positioned around the circle and make full use of its healing support.

BOND OF VIRTUE

Useful alternative when constant movement makes Circle of Divinity awkward or unreliable.

SITUATIONAL POWERS

CLEANSING TOUCH

Keep this in mind whenever the encounter applies dangerous negative effects. Swap it in when a cleanse is required.

BANISHMENT

Excellent utility against groups of enemies where control can prevent damage before you ever need to heal it.

BURNING LIGHT

Another useful control option for keeping packs stunned and reducing incoming group damage.

AURA OF WRATH

Strong damage-support switch for organised groups, increasing Critical Strike for nearby allies.

DAILIES

LAY ON HANDS

Excellent emergency option and often worth holding until a player genuinely needs saving.

SHIELD OF FAITH

Useful when the party is spread out and needs group-wide mitigation rather than a concentrated defensive effect.

SANCTUARY

Useful defensive option when mitigation is required and the group can benefit from its positioning.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Prioritise boons that improve the main healing percentages before spending points on secondary utility.

  • Power
  • Outgoing Healing
  • Critical Severity
  • Critical Strike
  • Recharge and useful healing utility
  • Defensive bonuses after the core healing setup is established

Guild boons should follow the same principle: use them to complete the actual percentages your character needs rather than copying somebody else's setup exactly.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

HEALING FLOW

  1. POSITION FIRST Stay where you can reach the tank and party without forcing yourself into unnecessary mechanics.
  2. ESTABLISH YOUR HEALING AREA Use Circle of Divinity where the fight allows it. Switch to Bond of Virtue when movement makes the circle impractical.
  3. MAINTAIN SHIELDS Keep the important targets protected before heavy incoming damage rather than waiting until everybody is already nearly dead.
  4. USE CURE WOUNDS FOR EFFICIENT HEALING Don't spend expensive healing resources when a smaller heal will comfortably handle the damage.
  5. DIVINE TOUCH WHEN PLAYERS SPREAD Heal and shield individual players when stacking isn't possible.
  6. LAY ON HANDS FOR THE OH-SHIT MOMENT Keep the emergency heal available instead of spending it because somebody's health bar looked mildly untidy.
  7. SUPPORT THE ARTIFACT CALL Fire your support artifact and mount power with the group's burst window once immediate healing is under control.
  8. ADAPT TO THE FIGHT Swap in Cleansing Touch, Banishment, Burning Light or extra mitigation whenever the encounter mechanics demand it.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

Oathkeeper has a surprisingly large amount of room for group-support swaps because maximum healing produces more shielding than many groups actually require.

MAXIMUM HEALING

Keep the full healing-stat setup when progression content or heavy tank damage makes maximum shields valuable.

DAMAGE SUPPORT

A damage-debuff neck, waist and artifact set can replace the personal healing set when the group benefits more from additional boss damage.

MASTERWORK WEAPONS

Strong organised-group option because they provide party damage and damage-resistance support instead of concentrating everything on your own healing.

SUPPORT ARMOUR & RINGS

AP-support armour, enemy-debuff arms and damage-support rings can all be introduced once maximum personal healing is unnecessary.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Oathkeeper is one of those healers where the best upgrade isn't always "MORE HEALING". At some point you've already put enough shield on the tank to protect them from the boss, next Tuesday and possibly a small meteor. 😂

That's when the support options become interesting. If everybody is comfortably surviving, swapping some personal healing for damage debuffs, support companions, Masterwork weapons or Aura of Wrath can contribute considerably more to the run.

Also learn the situational powers. Cleansing Touch, Divine Touch, Banishment and Burning Light aren't necessarily permanent residents on the bar, but knowing when to pull them out is part of actually playing the Paladin rather than just copying a screenshot.
