THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Lair of Lostmauth has three boss encounters.

Most of the danger comes from red telegraphs, lethal lava areas and knockback mechanics.

BOSS ONE

DRAGON SOUL VESSEL

Stay reasonably close to the boss.

If players move too far away, he can throw his spear at random targets.

Avoid the red ground effects.

The boss uses frontal AoE attacks, so DPS should avoid standing directly in front of him.

50% SPLIT

At around 50% health, the rider and drake separate into two targets.

The tank should regain control of the drake immediately.

If one target dies before the other, the survivor becomes stronger.

Keep both under control and finish the remaining target quickly.

DUNGEON HAZARD

FALLING ROCKS & LAVA

Small red areas on the floor mark falling rocks.

Move out before they land.

Lava sections between bosses are lethal or extremely damaging.

Take your time crossing rather than rushing into the lava.

BOSS TWO

TWIN FIRE SCORPIONS

Both Fire Scorpions are active at the same time.

The tank can control both, or one player can kite one while the group kills the other.

DPS should focus one Scorpion at a time.

Avoid all red telegraphs.

LAVA POOLS

Small lava pools on the ground are extremely dangerous and can kill even a tank.

Keep repositioning as the lava reduces safe space.

Once one Scorpion dies, the encounter becomes significantly easier.

FINAL BOSS

LOSTMAUTH

The final fight takes place on a small hexagonal platform.

Stay relatively close to Lostmauth where possible.

FIREBALLS

Fireballs travel across the arena and can knock players backward.

Avoid being pushed into the molten gold around the arena.

MOLTEN FLOOR

Sections of the floor turn red before filling with molten gold.

Move away from any floor section that begins glowing red.

RED SPIKES

At around 50% health, move toward the centre of the arena.

Small red areas appear before spikes erupt from the floor.

Move or dodge before the spikes hit.

The spikes can knock players into the molten gold.

Do not attempt to revive someone standing in molten gold until the dangerous area recedes.

EYE LASER

Lostmauth can target players with an eye-beam attack.

Watch for his eyes glowing before the attack.

Tanks positioned beneath or near his head may be able to intercept the beam.

Healers should watch whoever is targeted, as the attack can hit hard.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Keep the first boss facing away from the rest of the group.

When the rider and drake split, regain control of the drake immediately.

During the Fire Scorpions, control both if possible while the group focuses one.

During Lostmauth, stay aware of the platform edge and knockback mechanics.

HEALER

Watch players hit by knockbacks and heavy boss attacks.

During Lostmauth, pay particular attention to whoever is targeted by the eye laser.

Do not put yourself into molten gold attempting an unsafe revive.

DPS

Avoid standing directly in front of the first boss.

During the Fire Scorpions, focus one target at a time.

Keep enough awareness during Lostmauth to avoid fireballs, molten floor and the spike phase.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Standing in front of the first boss and eating frontal attacks.

Losing control of the drake when the first boss splits.

Allowing the surviving first-boss target to become stronger and then failing to finish it quickly.

Rushing through lava sections between encounters.

Splitting damage across both Fire Scorpions instead of focusing one.

Standing in the Scorpions' lava pools.

Getting knocked off Lostmauth's platform by a fireball or spike.

Failing to move when a floor section turns red.

Attempting to revive someone while they are still standing in molten gold.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

First boss: stay relatively close, don't stand in front, and when he splits, somebody make sure the drake doesn't immediately develop free will.

Two Fire Scorpions does not mean DPS gets to invent two separate fights. Pick one. Murder it. Then murder the other one.

Lava pool? Absolutely fucking not. There is no mechanic hidden inside it. You do not need to investigate.

Lostmauth's platform is surrounded by molten gold. Fireballs knock you backwards. You can probably see where this relationship is going.

At 50%, get toward the centre and dodge the spikes. The edge is currently the least useful place you could possibly choose to stand.
