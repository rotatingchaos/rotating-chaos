THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Pirate King's Retreat is a straightforward dungeon focused primarily on clearing enemy groups and defeating three bosses.

The first two bosses spawn large numbers of adds, while the final Pirate King encounter is a direct boss fight.

DUNGEON ROUTE

PIRATE HIDEOUT

Follow the route through the hideout while clearing enemy groups.

Pull the required wall levers to open the route forward.

Continue clearing enemies until reaching the first boss area.

BOSS ONE

REDOLENT SURGEON

The encounter begins with the boss surrounded by multiple adds.

Use AoE damage to control the large number of enemies in the arena.

Focus damage on the boss while keeping the adds under control.

Additional adds spawn after the boss is defeated.

Clear the remaining enemies before continuing through the dungeon.

DUNGEON ROUTE

ROPE BRIDGES

Continue across the rope bridges while clearing pirate groups.

Follow the route until reaching the hidden doorway.

Activate the secret door to reach the second boss.

BOSS TWO

THICK GRISTLE

Thick Gristle is another add-heavy encounter.

Large numbers of enemies fight alongside the boss.

AoE damage is useful for keeping the arena controlled.

More adds spawn when the boss is defeated.

Clear the remaining enemies before moving toward the final encounter.

FINAL ROUTE

THE PIRATE KING'S SHIP

Continue following the route and clearing enemy groups.

Make your way onto the Pirate King's ship.

Gather the full party before beginning the final encounter.

FINAL BOSS

THE PIRATE KING

Unlike the previous two bosses, the Pirate King does not spawn additional adds.

The encounter is a straightforward single-target fight.

Let the tank establish aggro and focus damage directly on the boss.

Burn the Pirate King down to complete the dungeon.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Gather the large add packs and keep enemies grouped for AoE damage.

Establish aggro on each boss before the group begins burning them.

During the final encounter, keep the Pirate King focused on you while the group attacks.

HEALER

Watch for players taking damage while large enemy groups are active.

Stay with the group while moving through the hideout and across the bridges.

Keep the tank healthy while they control the larger packs.

DPS

Use AoE damage on the large pirate groups and boss adds.

Clear the additional enemies that remain after the first two bosses die.

At the Pirate King, focus damage directly on the boss.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Pulling too many enemy groups without allowing the tank to control them.

Ignoring the large number of adds during the first two boss encounters.

Running ahead of the group and creating multiple separate fights.

Leaving enemies alive after the first or second boss dies.

Starting the Pirate King encounter before the full group reaches the ship.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

This is not one of those dungeons where you need a doctoral thesis to understand the first boss.

There are pirates. Kill the pirates. Occasionally pull a lever. Continue killing pirates.

First boss has adds. Second boss has even more bloody adds. AoE is your friend.

Also, boss dying does not mean the surviving pirates have suddenly reconsidered their career choices. Kill those too.

Pirate King has no adds. Tank gets his attention. Everyone else introduces him to the consequences of piracy.
