DUNGEON MECHANICS

GRAY WOLF DEN

Control the wolves. Avoid the red. Kill the right wolf beside the boss.

OVERVIEW

GRAY WOLF DEN

Gray Wolf Den is heavily populated with wolves and werewolves. Most of the dungeon is straightforward add control, while the final boss has an immunity mechanic that must be handled correctly.

THROUGHOUT THE DUNGEON

WOLVES & WEREWOLVES

  • Enemy groups can deal heavy damage when several wolves or werewolves reach the same player.
  • Tanks should engage first and keep enemy groups together where possible.
  • DPS should avoid rushing ahead alone into large packs.
  • Stormcallers create large lightning areas. Move out of the affected ground before the damage lands.
  • Crowd control and AoE damage are useful throughout the dungeon because of the number of enemy groups.

BOSS ONE

FIRST PACK LEADER

  • The boss summons additional wolves around the outside of the arena.
  • Control the adds as they enter rather than allowing them to overwhelm the group.
  • The boss can jump around the arena, so be ready to reposition when he moves.
  • Maintain pressure on the boss while clearing adds as necessary.

BOSS TWO

PACK MASTER

  • The Pack Master also summons wolves throughout the fight.
  • Keep the wolves grouped and burn them down with AoE damage.
  • The boss uses standard melee attacks and can jump around the arena.
  • Stay aware of positioning and do not allow the adds to pile onto lower-defence players.

FINAL BOSS

ETHRANIEV MARROWSLAKE

  • Ethraniev uses sword attacks and large red AoE zones.
  • Move out of the red areas before they activate.
  • She can jump toward random players, so be ready to reposition when targeted.
  • Additional enemies spawn during the encounter. Kill them before they begin to build up.

KEY MECHANIC

IMMUNITY & BARROW SPINE WOLF

  • During the fight, Ethraniev grows larger and becomes immune to damage.
  • When this happens, a Barrow Spine Wolf spawns.
  • The wolf must be killed directly beside Ethraniev.
  • Killing the wolf close enough to the boss breaks her immunity.
  • If the wolf dies too far away, the mechanic fails and the group must wait for another wolf to spawn.
  • Repeat this mechanic every time Ethraniev becomes immune.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

  • Engage enemy packs first and keep wolves grouped together.
  • During the final boss, keep Ethraniev controlled and bring her near the Barrow Spine Wolf when the immunity phase begins.

HEALER

  • Stay positioned where the group can remain inside your healing range during heavy add phases.
  • Watch players who are surrounded by wolves or caught by the boss's direct attacks.

DPS

  • Clear wolves and werewolves quickly when they spawn.
  • During the final boss, do not kill the Barrow Spine Wolf until it is beside Ethraniev.
  • Once the wolf breaks her immunity, immediately return damage to the boss.

COMMON WIPES

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

  • DPS running ahead and being overwhelmed by wolf packs.
  • Standing inside Stormcaller lightning zones.
  • Ignoring summoned wolves during boss encounters.
  • Standing inside Ethraniev's red AoEs.
  • Killing the Barrow Spine Wolf too far away from Ethraniev.
  • Repeatedly failing the immunity mechanic and leaving the boss permanently untargetable.

QUICK REFERENCE

WOLF DEN SURVIVAL NOTES

  • Tank goes first.
  • Lightning ground → move.
  • Wolves spawn → AoE them down.
  • Red area → get out.
  • Final boss grows and becomes immune → stop hitting her.
  • Barrow Spine Wolf appears → bring it beside the boss.
  • Kill wolf beside boss → immunity breaks.
  • Wolf dies too far away → congratulations, you're doing it again.
