HOW IT WORKS

OATHKEEPER EXPLAINED

Oathkeeper combines strong healing with protective shields. Rather than simply restoring health after damage lands, the Paladin can provide a substantial protective buffer before the next attack.

Build Power and Outgoing Healing first, then strengthen critical healing with Critical Severity and Critical Strike. Stronger critical heals also mean larger shields.

Once healing and shielding comfortably exceed the encounter's requirements, some personal healing can be traded for group-support effects.

CORE TOOLKIT

CURE WOUNDS, DIVINE TOUCH & HEALING AREA

Cure Wounds is the efficient general healing tool. Don't spend expensive resources when a smaller heal comfortably handles the damage.

Divine Touch is particularly useful when players are spread out and only one or two targets need immediate healing and shielding.

Circle of Divinity works well where positioning is predictable. Bond of Virtue becomes more practical when constant movement makes the circle unreliable.

SWAPS

LEARN THE SITUATIONAL POWERS

Cleansing Touch belongs on the bar whenever dangerous negative effects need removing.

Banishment and Burning Light provide useful control against enemy packs, reducing incoming damage before it ever needs healing.

Aura of Wrath is a damage-support option for organised groups when additional personal healing isn't required.

SUPPORT

WHEN MORE HEALING STOPS HELPING

Maximum healing isn't automatically the strongest group setup. Once the party is comfortably surviving, another healing bonus may contribute less than a useful damage debuff or party-support effect.

Support neck and waist sets, support armor and rings, group-support companions, weapons, artifacts and mount powers can all become worthwhile alternatives.

Keep the full healing setup for progression or heavy incoming damage. Switch toward support when the group genuinely doesn't need maximum shields.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Oathkeeper is one of those healers where eventually "MORE HEALING" stops being the answer.

At some point you've already put enough shield on the tank to protect them from the boss, next Tuesday and possibly a small meteor. 😂

That's when support becomes interesting. If nobody is dying, help the group kill the bastard faster.

And learn the swaps. Cleansing Touch, Divine Touch, Banishment and Burning Light don't need permanent residency on the bar to be extremely useful when the encounter actually calls for them.
