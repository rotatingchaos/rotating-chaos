SHACKLES OF DIVINITY

Rotating Chaos Neverwinter Trial Guide

Overview Core Mechanics Phase One Rune Phase Phase Two Phase Three Phase Four Role Jobs Common Wipes Chaos Notes

THE TRIAL

OVERVIEW

Shackles of Divinity is a 10-player trial centred around a highly predictable sequence of mechanics.

The main encounter against Valkariel is divided into four phases. Between the first three phases, the group drops to the lower platform to fight three Zulkirs and complete a rune mechanic before returning to Valkariel.

The rune mechanic is used in Advanced and Master. Normal does not require the rune matching mechanic.

Valkariel follows a set mechanic rotation during each phase. Learning that sequence makes the encounter considerably easier because the group can position for mechanics before they happen.

KNOW THESE FIRST

CORE VALKARIEL MECHANICS

SCREAM

Valkariel covers most of the arena in red, leaving only a narrow safe area around the outside edge.

Move to the outside of the platform. Remaining inside causes heavy damage over time and can easily kill non-tank players.

SIDE SWORD STRIKES

Valkariel strikes one side of the platform with her sword.

Watch her positioning and move away from the side she is about to hit.

MOVE CLOSE

For one sword sweep, players need to move close to Valkariel.

Players standing too far away can be hit and thrown from the platform.

MOVE FAR

The opposite sword sweep requires players to move away from Valkariel.

Remaining close can cause heavy damage and knockback.

TANKBUSTERS

Both tanks receive very large red skull areas.

Tanks need to position these away from the rest of the group and leave enough space between each other to avoid overlapping the attacks.

A common strategy is for the tanks to remain around the middle while the rest of the group positions toward one side.

BALLS

Vortex-like balls appear around the arena and can remain active for some time.

These are particularly dangerous because standing between a ball and Valkariel can cause the player to take Combat Advantage damage.

This can dramatically increase incoming damage and turn otherwise survivable mechanics into one-shots.

Destroy the balls quickly where possible, or position so that a ball is not directly behind you while Valkariel is in front.

FIVE RAYS

Five red ray telegraphs appear across the arena. Move into the safe spaces between them.

TEN RAYS

A larger pattern of red rays covers the arena. Identify a safe section and move out of the telegraphs.

SPREAD

Multiple players receive individual red areas.

Spread away from each other so the areas do not overlap.

LARGE BALLS

Large red areas appear around players during later phases.

Move away from them rather than allowing the group to become boxed in.

HYPO

Hypo is a shared damage-soak mechanic marked by arrows.

Group together to share the incoming damage.

Coordinating an Artifact call during Hypo can reduce the boss's damage and make the soak easier to survive.

VALKARIEL

PHASE ONE

Valkariel begins the encounter with Scream.

From there, she follows a fixed rotation involving sword strikes, close/far positioning, Tankbusters, balls, five rays, ten rays and eventually Spread.

Groups with strong damage may push the boss before seeing every mechanic.

If Valkariel is not pushed to the phase threshold, the mechanic sequence begins repeating from the start.

Once the threshold is reached, Valkariel drives her sword into the platform and the group begins taking damage over time.

Jump down to the lower platform to begin the Zulkir phase.

ADVANCED ◆ MASTER

ZULKIRS & RUNES

Three Zulkir minibosses must be defeated on the lower platform.

Do not stand on the platform runes while fighting the Zulkirs. Activating them at the wrong time can throw players from the platform.

KILL ALL THREE ZULKIRS

Each defeated Zulkir reveals a symbol in the air.

Each symbol corresponds to one of the runes positioned around the platform.

Three assigned players should memorise the corresponding runes.

ACTIVATE THE RUNES

After all three Zulkirs are dead, the assigned players stand on their matching runes.

The three runes do not need to be activated at exactly the same moment.

Remain on the rune until the game displays the warning to seek cover and brace against Valkariel's Chaotic Judgment.

CHAOTIC JUDGMENT

Valkariel strikes the Mythallar and releases a huge burst.

Hide behind the available screens to survive the explosion.

In Master, damage over time during this phase can become severe. Healers need to remain close enough to support the rune players.

MOBS

Additional mobs continue spawning until the Mythallar detonates.

Tanks need to keep them under control while the rune mechanic is completed.

Tanks and a healer are often good choices for rune assignments because they are more capable of surviving enemies around the runes.

RETURN TO VALKARIEL

After the explosion, use the upward launch points to return to the upper platform.

Aim the camera toward the next platform while jumping to make the route easier to follow.

VALKARIEL

PHASE TWO

Valkariel returns with another fixed mechanic rotation.

The phase begins with sword positioning followed by a Tankbuster.

Phase Two introduces two important mechanics: Large Balls and Hypo.

Large Balls create large red areas that need to be moved away from.

Hypo requires the group to stack together and share the damage.

Spread mechanics also occur during this phase, so players need to recognise when the group should stack and when it should separate.

Balls, rays, Scream, Tankbusters and sword mechanics continue throughout.

If damage is low, the rotation repeats.

At approximately 50% health, Valkariel phases again and the group returns to the lower platform for another Zulkir and rune sequence.

VALKARIEL

PHASE THREE

Return to the upper platform after completing the second rune phase.

Phase Three uses the same established mechanics: far and close sword positioning, Hypo, rays, Tankbusters, Scream, balls and Spread.

There are no major new mechanics here. The challenge is recognising the established rotation and positioning early.

At approximately 25% health, Valkariel phases for the third time.

Drop to the lower platform, defeat the three Zulkirs, complete the rune mechanic, survive Chaotic Judgment and return to the top.

FINAL BURN

PHASE FOUR

Phase Four is the final Valkariel phase.

Her mechanic rotation remains similar to Phase Three, but the group now takes increasingly heavy damage over time.

Healing pressure becomes extremely high, particularly on Master.

The source guide notes that Master groups commonly use three healers for this phase unless two exceptionally strong healers can manage the incoming damage.

BALL CONTROL MATTERS

Destroying or safely positioning around the vortex balls becomes particularly important.

Taking Combat Advantage damage from a badly positioned ball while the group is already suffering heavy damage over time can easily kill players.

AVOID EVERYTHING YOU CAN

Healers are already dealing with substantial unavoidable damage.

Every avoidable ray, red area, sword strike or ball hit adds unnecessary pressure and can turn the final phase into a wipe.

Continue following the known rotation until Valkariel is defeated.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANKS

Position Tankbusters away from the group and from each other.

Control spawning mobs during the rune phases.

Tanks are strong choices for rune assignments because they can survive enemies that remain active around the platform.

HEALERS

Keep rune players alive during the lower-platform phases.

Prepare for heavy group damage during Hypo and extremely high sustained damage during Phase Four.

Master places considerably greater pressure on healers during the final burn.

DPS

Learn Valkariel's rotation so you can move before mechanics land rather than reacting after the arena turns red.

Kill vortex balls quickly where possible and avoid creating Combat Advantage against yourself.

Stack for Hypo, spread for Spread and stop donating your health bar to avoidable red telegraphs.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Standing in Scream instead of moving to the outside edge.

Moving the wrong direction during close/far sword mechanics and being knocked from the platform.

Tankbusters overlapping the group or each other.

Leaving vortex balls in positions that give Valkariel Combat Advantage against players.

Spreading during Hypo instead of stacking.

Stacking during Spread instead of separating.

Standing on runes too early during the Zulkir phase.

Failing to activate the correct three runes on Advanced or Master.

Failing to hide behind cover during Chaotic Judgment.

Taking unnecessary avoidable damage during the Phase Four heal check.

DIFFICULTY DIFFERENCES

NORMAL ◆ ADVANCED ◆ MASTER

Normal does not use the rune-matching mechanic during the lower-platform Zulkir phases.

Advanced introduces the rune mechanic and requires three players to correctly identify and activate the matching runes.

Master uses the same fundamental encounter structure but applies much greater damage pressure, particularly during the rune phases and final Valkariel phase.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Shackles looks far more complicated than it actually is because Valkariel throws a lot of mechanics at the arena.

The trick is that she isn't improvising. Her mechanics follow a set rotation. Once the group learns what comes next, most of the fight becomes positioning rather than panic.

The biggest rule is simple: close means close, far means far, Hypo means cuddle, Spread means absolutely stop cuddling, and red still means get the hell out of it.
