THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Master Dread Sanctum contains three major boss encounters.

Crystals are a recurring mechanic throughout the dungeon. They provide the buff needed to damage enemies protected by otherwise impenetrable barriers.

The final encounter adds coordinated Demogorgon kills, individual portal transfers, safe-corner identification and a final timed damage check.

FIRST BOSS

GRAF HARROW

Graf Harrow deals heavy melee damage.

The tank should maintain control of the boss while the group attacks from safer positions.

BARRIER

During the encounter, Graf Harrow becomes protected by a barrier and incoming damage becomes immune.

CRYSTALS

When the barrier activates, move next to one of the crystals around the arena.

Wait for the crystal to burst.

The burst grants a buff that allows the player to damage Graf Harrow through his barrier.

If your attacks begin showing as immune again, return to a crystal and refresh the buff.

SECOND BOSS

MIND FLAYER

The Mind Flayer uses the same crystal-and-barrier mechanic introduced during Graf Harrow.

STARTING SHIELD

The boss begins protected by a shield.

Use a crystal immediately to gain the buff required to damage through the barrier.

REFLECTED DAMAGE

Do not continue attacking while the boss is immune.

Damage dealt while the immunity is active is reflected back onto the attacker.

PLATFORM PHASE

At certain health thresholds, players are transported onto a series of platforms.

Move across the platforms toward the centre.

GREEN GOO

Avoid falling into the green liquid surrounding the platforms.

It deals heavy damage.

RED PUSH AREAS

Red areas appear on the platforms.

Move out of them before they activate or they can push you from the platform.

RETURN PORTAL

Reach the central platform and enter the portal to return to the boss arena.

The boss remains immune briefly after players return.

Refresh your crystal buff and wait until the immunity ends before attacking.

The platform phase repeats later in the fight.

PARANOID DELUSIONS

Paranoid Delusions can spawn during the encounter.

Deal with them while continuing to manage the boss's barrier and platform phases.

FINAL BOSS

MIND FLAYER DRAGON

The final boss has several phases and requires considerably more coordination than the previous encounters.

OPENING PHASE

Damage the dragon while managing the additional enemies appearing around the arena.

Tentacles and Paranoid Delusions can spawn during this phase.

Move away from dangerous tentacle attacks rather than remaining beside them unnecessarily.

DRAGON BITE

The dragon's bite can deal heavy damage, particularly to DPS.

It can also apply a strong damage-over-time effect.

DPS should avoid standing directly in front of the dragon.

INTERMISSION

Once the dragon reaches the required health threshold, it flies away and the encounter moves into the Demogorgon portal phase.

COORDINATION CHECK

DEMOGORGONS & PORTALS

This phase requires the group to kill the Demogorgons one at a time.

KILL ONE AT A TIME

Do not kill multiple Demogorgons simultaneously.

When a Demogorgon dies, the player closest to it receives a special buff.

BUFFED PLAYER

The player who receives the buff must immediately move to the portal in the centre.

Only the player carrying the buff can use the portal.

WAIT BEFORE THE NEXT KILL

Wait until the buffed player has completely entered the portal before killing the next Demogorgon.

Then kill the next one, allow the next buffed player through, and repeat.

DO NOT DOUBLE KILL

If two Demogorgons are killed too quickly, only one player can use the portal.

The other player can become trapped and die.

FIND THE SAFE CORNER

Before leaving the main arena, identify which corner contains multiple green waterfalls.

That is the safe corner for the incoming wipe mechanic.

The other corners only have a single green waterfall and are not safe.

AFTER USING THE PORTAL

Once you enter the central portal, move immediately toward the safe corner identified earlier.

The entire group must reach the correct safe area before the phase resolves.

Failing to reach the safe corner results in death.

PHASE END

Remain in the safe corner until the arena-wide damage occurs.

Once that hit has resolved, it is safe to leave and continue damaging the dragon.

SECOND DEMOGORGON PHASE

The entire Demogorgon sequence repeats later in the fight.

The safe corner can change, so identify the group of green waterfalls again.

Do not assume the previous safe corner remains correct.

DEMOGORGON ADDS

Demogorgons can also spawn in the main dragon arena between intermissions.

These enemies deal very heavy damage and should be killed quickly.

Tentacles and Paranoid Delusions can also continue appearing, making later phases increasingly chaotic.

LAST DAMAGE CHECK

DRAGON ASPECT & BRAIN

Once the dragon reaches approximately its final health threshold, the group is transported to the last section.

DRAGON ASPECT

Kill the illusion or aspect of the dragon.

CRYSTAL

After the aspect dies, move to a crystal and allow it to burst.

This grants the buff needed for the final target.

THE BRAIN

Use the crystal buff to attack the brain.

This is a timed damage check.

If the group fails to destroy the brain quickly enough, the entire party wipes.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Maintain control of Graf Harrow and the Mind Flayer during their damage phases.

During the final boss, keep the dragon facing away from DPS where possible so its heavy bite attacks do not hit the group.

Be ready to control dangerous Demogorgon adds between intermissions.

HEALER

Watch for heavy melee and dragon damage, particularly damage-over-time effects from the dragon's bite.

During platform sections, maintain awareness of players taking damage from the environment.

Keep the group healthy during the increasingly chaotic later dragon phases.

DPS

Stop attacking whenever a boss becomes immune and refresh the crystal buff.

During the Demogorgon phase, control your damage and kill them one at a time.

Do not kill the next Demogorgon until the previous buffed player has entered the portal.

Save enough damage for the final brain burn.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Continuing to attack an immune boss and taking reflected damage.

Forgetting to refresh the crystal buff.

Falling from the platforms during the Mind Flayer encounter.

Standing in the green liquid during the platform phase.

DPS standing in front of the dragon and taking its bite.

Killing multiple Demogorgons before the previous player has used the central portal.

Forgetting which corner contains the multiple green waterfalls.

Running to the previous safe corner without checking whether it changed.

Allowing Demogorgon adds, Paranoid Delusions and tentacles to overwhelm the group.

Failing the final brain damage check.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

The first two bosses are basically teaching you one important lesson: shiny crystal good, attacking immune things bad.

The dragon is where everyone actually has to start communicating.

Demogorgons: ONE. AT. A. TIME.

Kill one, let the glowing person disappear through the middle portal, then murder the next one. This is not the moment for someone to demonstrate their magnificent AoE.

And before going through the portal, remember which corner has the ridiculous amount of green waterfalls. That's your new favourite corner.
