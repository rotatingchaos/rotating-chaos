◆ DEFENSIVE STATS ◆

STAT PRIORITY

Build the core defensive percentages, then move excess ratings into block, utility and group support.

MAXIMUM HP

More health means a larger safety buffer and, importantly for Paladin, more effective block.

DEFENSE

Core damage mitigation. Easy to overcap, so don't keep throwing Defense at the build once it no longer needs it.

AWARENESS

Major defensive priority and heavily supported by the recommended M33 equipment.

CRITICAL AVOIDANCE

Reduces the danger from enemy critical hits and remains an important tank percentage.

DEFLECT

Useful additional mitigation once the main defensive percentages are healthy.

DEFLECT SEVERITY

Improves the value of successful deflects.

STAMINA REGEN

Becomes increasingly useful once Awareness is already capped. More stamina means more block.

THE BASIC IDEA

JUSTICAR EXPLAINED

Justicar is built around controlling threat, managing block and surviving the large attacks that would turn everybody else into decorative paste.

The general build is intentionally flexible. Start with enough personal survivability to tank the content comfortably, then convert unnecessary defensive excess into party support.

BLOCK

HP IS PART OF YOUR DEFENCE

Maximum HP matters for more than simply making the health bar bigger. Paladin's block scales with that health pool, which is why this setup invests heavily into HP.

Stamina regeneration becomes particularly useful once Awareness is already capped because it lets you recover your block more quickly between attacks.

THREAT

MAKE THE BOSS HATE YOU

Divine Champion is central to the Justicar's threat generation.

Use your normal attacks and encounters to maintain control, then increase effective damage during the group's artifact call to make sure high-output DPS don't rip the boss away from you.

If threat is becoming a problem, change the powers. Oath Strike, Sacred Weapon, Smite and Aura of Valor all give you ways to lean harder into threat.

GROUP SUPPORT

WHEN SURVIVAL IS ALREADY EASY

Once you can comfortably survive the encounter and threat is stable, the build can move toward helping everybody else do more damage.

Masterwork weapons, party-support companions, support enhancements, alternative neck/waist sets and support auras can all contribute.

But don't sacrifice the fundamentals just to make a spreadsheet look prettier. A dead tank provides approximately zero fucking group support.

ABILITY SCORES

CONSTITUTION FIRST

Constitution is the core ability-score investment for the tank setup.

The secondary choice can move between Charisma, Intelligence or Strength depending on what the finished version is trying to achieve.

DEATH & REVIVE

REVIVE SICKNESS MATTERS

Tanks are hit particularly hard by losing Maximum HP after a death because that health also supports their ability to absorb damage.

The guild revive-sickness reduction boon is therefore extremely useful on Justicar.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Your first responsibility is not dying. Your second is making sure the boss is trying to kill you instead of everyone else.

Once both of those things are comfortably sorted, congratulations — now you get to become a support character while simultaneously being punched in the face. 😂

If a DPS complains that you're using extra threat tools while they're repeatedly turning the threat bubble orange, feel free to point out the incredibly sophisticated tanking technique known as “stop stealing my fucking boss.”
