SOUL HARVEST

Rotating Chaos Neverwinter Trial Guide

Overview Phase One Phase Two Biome Mechanics Supports Phase Three Final Boss Escape Szass Tam Standard Chaos Notes

THE TRIAL

OVERVIEW

Soul Harvest is a mechanically heavy 10-player trial built around three elemental biomes: Fae, Fire and Ice.

The group is split into two teams of five and sent to two randomly selected biomes. Each side must control its boss, perform biome-specific mechanics, support a healer-and-tank maze run, and complete a coordinated soul swap before the trial can progress.

Later phases reunite the group for the final boss, phantom biome bosses and a timed escape sequence.

FIRST PHASE

RANDOM BIOME SPLIT

The group is randomly divided into two parties of five.

The game attempts to ensure each side has at least one tank and one healer.

Each party is then sent to two of the three possible biomes: Fae, Fire or Ice.

The goal is to reduce each biome boss to 50% health.

Once a boss reaches 50%, it becomes invulnerable and Phase Two begins.

The two groups do not need to hit 50% at the exact same moment.

SECOND PHASE

STUN TIMER & SOUL SWAP

Once the biome bosses lock at 50%, they begin siphoning essence toward Szass Tam.

A countdown appears on the right side of the screen. If it reaches zero, the trial wipes.

The displayed value is not seconds. Divide it by two to estimate the actual remaining time.

Phase Two happens in two parts simultaneously: DPS perform biome mechanics to extend the timer, while tanks and healers obtain buffs and travel through the death biome maze.

DPS JOB

BIOME STUN MECHANICS

DPS should first help kill the green and blue support ghosts, then kill red Soul of the Vanguard ghosts to gain their own special buff.

The red buff reduces incoming damage and provides healing over time, which is critical while supports are away.

FAE BIOME

Kill a red Soul of the Vanguard to gain the red buff.

Stand inside one of the purple pools while the buff is active. This gradually grows a plant.

After roughly 30 seconds, a small creature spawns and moves toward the boss. When it reaches the boss, the trial timer increases.

Each DPS should use a separate pool because multiple players in the same pool do not speed the mechanic up.

Ideally two DPS begin growing plants immediately while one helps the supports obtain their green and blue ghost buffs.

FIRE BIOME

Kill mobs while the red Soul of the Vanguard buff is active.

Mobs drop soul currency that is shared between nearby DPS.

Deposit collected currency at the circular rune beneath the burning tree in the centre of the biome to increase the timer.

Currency is held individually, so each player needs to deposit their own.

ICE BIOME

Find the currently active stone pillar marked by a beacon.

Stand inside its yellow barrier to increase the trial timer and prevent freezing.

More players inside the barrier increases the timer faster.

Active pillars change throughout the phase, so the group must move together from pillar to pillar.

Players continuously gain Frostbite stacks. Staying close to another player grants warmth and protects against Frostbite.

Too many Frostbite stacks will freeze a player in place.

SUPPORT JOB

HEALER & TANK SOUL SWAP

Two support ghosts spawn: Soul of the Devout in green and Soul of the Sentinel in blue.

It does not matter who kills them, provided the tank and healer are present in the biome when they die.

SOUL OF THE DEVOUT

The healer receives the green buff.

This allows the healer to transport their biome's soul through the maze and deposit it at the biome occupied by the other team.

The healer also gains improved visibility of maze traps.

SOUL OF THE SENTINEL

The tank receives the blue buff.

This creates a protective bubble that helps the tank and nearby allies survive the death biome's damage-over-time effect and improves visibility.

MAZE TRAVERSAL

The healer should lead through the maze because their green buff highlights traps.

The tank follows closely, using the protective bubble to keep both players alive.

Avoid mobs where possible and avoid the wandering Tenebraloth.

Maze paths are randomised. The correct path should allow traps to be avoided rather than requiring players to brute-force through them.

THE SWAP

Both support teams should communicate before entering their destination biome.

Once both sides are ready, the tanks hard-taunt their respective bosses.

The healers then enter the green circle under the boss and deposit the souls.

Once both deposits are complete, the wipe timer ends and the bosses become active again.

THIRD PHASE

FINISH THE BIOME BOSSES

After the soul swap, portals briefly appear between the two active biomes.

These can be used to rearrange players if required.

Both biome bosses are now vulnerable again and should be killed.

Once both bosses are dead, portals appear that take both groups to the final boss arena.

Tanks and healers should enter before DPS where possible.

PHASES FOUR TO SIX

FINAL BOSS & PHANTOMS

PHASE FOUR

The full group reunites.

One tank can hold the boss while the second tank controls additional mobs.

Reduce the boss to 50% health, where it locks again.

PHASE FIVE - PHANTOM BOSSES

Phantasmal versions of the two previously defeated biome bosses appear.

At the same time, the biome's stun mechanic becomes active again.

DPS must keep extending the timer while the group kills both phantoms.

Decide in advance which phantom the group will kill first.

One tank should isolate the priority phantom while the second controls the other phantom and any additional enemies.

Once both phantoms are dead, the countdown stops.

ICE PHANTOM WARNING

If the final biome is Ice, the phantoms may target the active stun-mechanic pillar directly.

DPS using the pillar need to move when these attacks arrive.

Tanks may temporarily lose effective threat control while the phantoms focus on the pillar.

PHASE SIX

Finish the final boss.

Before the boss dies, organise two balanced groups where possible: one tank, one healer and three DPS per party.

This prepares everyone for the final escape phase.

FINAL PHASE

SOUL DELIVERY & ESCAPE

The group ends up in the Ice biome and the final timed sequence begins.

Split into the two planned five-player parties.

Kill the green and blue support ghosts again to obtain healer and tank buffs.

The two parties must then transport empowered souls through the death biome maze.

One team travels to Fae and the other to Fire.

Both healers deposit their empowered soul in the green circles where the biome bosses previously stood.

FIVE-MINUTE TIMER

The guide states the group has five minutes to complete the maze, deposit both empowered souls, return through the maze and reach the final gate.

Bell tolls occur approximately every 25 seconds. The twelfth toll means the group wipes.

BRIDGE ESCAPE

After both souls are deposited, regroup at the bridge gate.

Tanks should lead the group, collecting enemies ahead.

Healers keep the party alive while DPS control and kill enemies.

Moving forward after Szass Tam's meteor showers can reduce unnecessary deaths.

REQUIRED SURVIVORS

On Master, at least nine players must survive to the end of the bridge sequence.

Advanced requires six surviving players.

Standard requires three surviving players.

Reach the final circular platform before time expires to complete Soul Harvest.

GLOBAL MECHANICS

SZASS TAM'S EDICTS & POWERS

Szass Tam can interfere throughout the trial with random edicts and powers.

On Master these can begin immediately. On Advanced they begin once the first biome bosses reach 50%. Standard does not use his edicts.

CHAOS

Temporarily disables a random category of player powers.

This can affect Dailies, Encounters or At-Wills. Tanks do not have their Encounters locked out.

DECAY

Reduces stamina regeneration. Tanks should be especially careful during this effect.

OPPRESSION

Increases player ability cooldown times.

REGRESSION

Disables Artifacts, Mount powers and Utility slots.

This can seriously disrupt Artifact-call timing.

WRATH

At-Will powers damage the player while active.

DARK STAR

Creates large purple zones that inflict the death-biome status effect, reducing visibility and dealing damage over time.

Avoid these zones.

DISRUPT LIFE

Reduces maximum player health significantly.

Tanks need to be especially careful with Tankbusters while this is active.

FRIGHTENING GAZE

Significantly reduces player movement speed.

METEOR

Large AoE circles appear and deal very heavy damage.

Avoid them where possible rather than relying on immunity frames.

RTQ VERSION

STANDARD SOUL HARVEST

Standard Soul Harvest is heavily simplified.

The entire group remains together rather than splitting into two teams.

There is no death-biome maze, stun mechanic or soul-swap sequence.

The group defeats each biome boss in sequence using portals to move between them.

After the final boss, everyone enters the bridge escape together.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Letting the Phase Two timer reach zero.

DPS failing to keep the biome stun mechanic active while supports are in the maze.

Supports dying in the death biome or depositing souls at different times.

Losing control of phantom bosses during Phase Five.

Failing to complete the final soul delivery within the five-minute limit.

Arriving at the final bridge platform with too few surviving players.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Soul Harvest looks terrifying because there are a ridiculous number of moving parts, but the fight becomes much easier once everyone knows which mechanic actually belongs to their role.

DPS keep the clock alive. Supports perform the soul swap. Tanks control positioning. Everyone survives the final bridge. Do those four things and the wall of mechanics becomes considerably less horrifying.
