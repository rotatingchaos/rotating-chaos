THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Arbiter is the damage-focused Cleric paragon and one of the more demanding DPS paths to play well. It rewards good timing, encounter management and careful setup with extremely strong burst damage.

The heart of the build is managing Divinity and Perfect Balance while building powerful Prophecy of Doom windows. Arbiter is not a button-mashing build: the order and timing of your encounters matter.

Once everything lines up, however, Arbiter has one of the highest damage ceilings available to a Cleric and can produce some seriously nasty burst.

PLAYSTYLE: Build your balance, manage Divinity, prepare Prophecy of Doom and unload your encounters inside the damage window.

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Arbiter wants its offensive stats pushed hard while maintaining enough recharge speed to keep Prophecy of Doom rotations flowing. Don't chase one number at the expense of the entire build.

POWER

Primary damage stat. Aim to keep this at or around the effective cap once all combat buffs are active.

CRITICAL STRIKE

Important for maintaining consistent damage during both sustained combat and burst windows.

CRITICAL SEVERITY

A major part of Arbiter's burst potential. The current setup can reach the 120% ceiling without relying on a Critical Severity mount collar.

COMBAT ADVANTAGE

Extremely valuable in group content. Positioning still matters: having the stat is no bloody use if you aren't actually gaining Combat Advantage.

ACCURACY

Fill this after the core offensive priorities. The current build has room to improve Accuracy without sacrificing its main damage setup.

RECHARGE SPEED

Particularly important for Arbiter because shorter cooldowns help keep Prophecy of Doom rotations aligned with group artifact calls.

ABILITY SCORES

The Module 33.5 setup uses a Dexterity and Intelligence focused spread, finishing at:

STR 19 CON 13 DEX 27 INT 32 WIS 19 CHA 16

LOADOUT

GEAR

This is an endgame Module 33.5 setup built around maximising Arbiter's burst while keeping enough recharge to maintain its sustained Prophecy of Doom rotations.

WEAPONS

Use the strongest current Cleric DPS weapon set available to you, prioritising the set bonus and offensive stats that best support your finished stat balance.

NECK & WAIST

The Evolving Form set is the preferred Module 33.5 option. It provides a modest damage increase over the older Scintillant set.

RINGS

Choose current damage rings that complete your stat requirements rather than blindly copying a ring because somebody else's build uses it.

ARMOUR

Prioritise current Cleric DPS pieces with useful offensive bonuses. Item level alone does not automatically make a piece better for the build.

IMPORTANT: Moving from Scintillant to Evolving Form removes a significant source of recharge speed. Make that recharge back elsewhere or your sustained Prophecy of Doom timing becomes noticeably tighter.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Build enchantments around the stats you are actually missing rather than treating every slot as fixed. The finished build should be balanced as a whole.

Module 33.5 allows a Strike enchantment and a Guard enchantment to be equipped together. This is especially useful for Arbiter's multi-target setup.

GUARD ENCHANTMENT

Fluid Aurora is particularly valuable for multi-target combat because the extra Divinity allows Arbiter to keep encounters flowing without constantly stopping to rebuild Divinity.

BONUS ENCHANTMENT

Recharge is valuable here. The current setup recovers part of the recharge lost when moving away from the Scintillant set through its enchantment and other supporting bonuses.

REINFORCEMENTS

Use reinforcement kits and jewels to finish the offensive stats your character is short on rather than automatically stacking the same stat in every slot.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

Arbiter's artifact setup is built around creating a coordinated burst window rather than simply firing an artifact whenever it lights up. Your artifact call should be combined with Prophecy of Doom, your daily, mount power and encounter sequence.

The Module 33.5 setup uses the Evolving Form artifact set alongside supporting artifacts chosen to complete the required stats and group damage setup.

CHAOS RULE: An artifact call is a damage window. Treat the whole sequence as one rotation instead of pressing everything in whatever order your thumb finds first.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Arbiter benefits heavily from companions that improve both personal damage and the damage output of the whole group.

RAPTOR'S INSTINCTS

A strong offensive companion bonus that scales with other party members using the same bonus.

BATIRI'S WISDOM

A useful offensive option for boss-focused damage.

GOLDEN CAT'S INSTINCTS

Another offensive option used to help complete the finished stat setup.

NEVERWINTER KNIGHT'S DISCIPLINE

Strong general offensive support for the finished DPS setup.

COMPANION ENHANCEMENTS

In dungeon content, group debuff enhancements are preferred over selfish personal bonuses. Useful options include:

  • Armor Break — enemy defence debuff.
  • Dulled Senses — enemy Awareness debuff.
  • Vulnerability — enemy Critical Avoidance debuff.

Ideally these debuffs are spread across the group so that the party benefits from all three rather than everybody stacking the same enhancement.

GROUP DAMAGE > PERSONAL PURPLES. If you're running coordinated dungeon content, helping the whole team hit harder can produce more overall damage than squeezing another personal stat bonus onto your own sheet.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mounts are doing several jobs in this build: supplying combat power, filling stats, supporting recharge and contributing insignia bonuses.

EQUIP & COMBAT POWERS

SWIFTSTRIDE

Used as the mount equip power in the current setup for additional movement speed.

INFERNAL POUNCE

Strong single-target mount combat power and the primary combat option in the current setup.

OTHER ST OPTIONS

Tunnel Vision and Giant Toad Tongue Lash are among the useful alternatives when you need another strong single-target combat power.

INSIGNIA BONUSES

  • Cavalry's Haste — using a mount combat power reduces encounter cooldowns.
  • Gladiator's Guile — movement speed while stamina is high and stamina regeneration while it is low.
  • Guardian's Spirit — defensive support while blocking.
  • Tactician's Precision — using a Daily reduces encounter cooldowns.
  • Warlord's Motivation — increases summoned companion damage.

MOUNT COLLARS

The current build uses mount collars to provide a mixture of damage, movement, recharge and stamina support. Wayfaring Crescent is particularly useful because its recharge bonus helps compensate for recharge lost elsewhere in the Module 33.5 setup.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Arbiter can switch between its single-target and multi-target setups particularly easily because both setups use the same Class Features and Feats. That means you can swap the required powers without needing to run back to a campfire and change the entire loadout.

SINGLE TARGET

The single-target setup revolves around Prophecy of Doom, Forgemaster's Flame and Daunting Light, with Flame Strike forming part of the main artifact-call burst sequence.

Lance of Faith is also important between encounter casts. During sustained rotations it can be held while waiting for Perfect Balance to activate, adding extra damage to the following Forgemaster's Flame.

MULTI-TARGET

The multi-target setup retains the same feats and class features, making it easy to switch between ST and AoE. Fluid Aurora supplies enough additional Divinity for encounters to be used aggressively throughout the multi-target rotation.

In ideal conditions this removes the need to consume Divinity purely to trigger Perfect Balance during the AoE rotation, although game lag can affect how smoothly the sequence behaves.

MJS FIRST BOSS

The first boss of Master Jotunskarr uses a specialised variation. Searing Javelin replaces Forgemaster's Flame while the overall rotation remains similar.

Artifact calls should be aimed at the Remorhaz. Outside the artifact call, Prophecy of Doom is better placed on a Basilisk during the sustained portion of the fight because a submerged Remorhaz can cause the Prophecy explosion to miss its damage.

KEY FEATS

DOOMSAYER

Applying a negative condition increases your damage for a short period, rewarding the debuff-heavy Arbiter playstyle.

DIVINE EQUILIBRIUM

Rewards keeping Divinity high, making Divinity management part of both Arbiter's damage and rotation planning.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Boons should support the same overall goal as the rest of the build: maximise useful offensive stats first, then use remaining points for survivability and utility.

Prioritise damage-focused boon choices that contribute to Power, Critical Strike, Critical Severity, Combat Advantage and Accuracy, adjusting individual selections when your character is already overcapped in a particular stat.

DON'T COPY A STAT BLINDLY: If a boon pushes a stat miles beyond what you can actually use, move the point somewhere that benefits your own toon.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

This is where Arbiter earns its reputation. The build is capable of huge burst, but only if Prophecy of Doom, Perfect Balance, Divinity, artifact calls and encounter timing are working together.

ARTIFACT CALL ROTATION

  1. Flame Strike Start at a 2-2 balance count.
  2. Prophecy of Doom Set up the burst window.
  3. Artifact Trigger the coordinated artifact call.
  4. Forgemaster's Flame Move the balance count to 3-2.
  5. Daunting Light Move to 3-3.
  6. Forgemaster's Flame Move to 4-3.
  7. Consume roughly half Divinity Use your Divinity mechanic to prepare the next sequence.
  8. Mount Combat Power Continue the burst while managing Divinity.
  9. Daunting Light Move to 4-4.
  10. Perfect Balance Reset to full Divinity and a 0-0 balance count.
  11. Forgemaster's Flame 1-0.
  12. Daunting Light 1-1.
  13. Forgemaster's Flame 2-1.
  14. Daunting Light 2-2.
  15. Forgemaster's Flame 3-2.
  16. Daunting Light Finish at 3-3 and let Prophecy of Doom go boom.

SUSTAINED ROTATION

Aim for roughly three Prophecy of Doom rotations per minute. Sustained PoD rotations should ideally begin at a 1-1 balance count, while artifact-call PoD rotations begin at 2-2.

A clean Prophecy of Doom window can fit around 10 encounter casts. While waiting for Perfect Balance during sustained damage, hold Lance of Faith to add extra damage to the next Forgemaster's Flame.

THE GOAL: Don't just make Prophecy of Doom explode. Pack as much damage as possible inside the window before it does.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

This is an endgame setup, not a shopping list you must complete before you're allowed to play Arbiter. Use the strongest pieces available to you while building towards the finished stat balance.

EVOLVING FORM VS SCINTILLANT

Evolving Form is the preferred Module 33.5 set for the higher damage ceiling. Scintillant remains useful while progressing, particularly because of its recharge contribution.

DEXTERITY VS STRENGTH

A Strength-heavy variation can produce a small damage increase, but sacrifices recharge speed. That recharge then needs to be recovered elsewhere through options such as another Tactician's Precision or an alternative cooldown bonus.

The trade-off is a tighter sustained rotation with less room for lag, delayed casts or late artifact calls. Dexterity therefore gives the build a little more breathing room.

COMPANION DEBUFF SWAPS

Armor Break, Dulled Senses and Vulnerability can be distributed differently depending on what the rest of the party is already running. Coordinate rather than duplicating bonuses unnecessarily.
