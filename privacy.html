WHAT THIS SITE USES

Rotating Chaos uses Firebase services to provide guild-member login and interactive community features.

This includes authentication and database storage used by features such as Chaos Chat, Events, Crew Profiles, Gallery, From the Chaos and My Chaos.

WHAT MAY BE STORED

  • Guild name used for site login and display.
  • Firebase authentication account information.
  • Chaos Chat messages and reactions.
  • Event sign-ups and run-sheet assignments.
  • Crew profile details voluntarily added by members.
  • Gallery image links, captions and categories.
  • From the Chaos posts.
  • Merch listings created through the site.

IMAGES AND LINKS

The site may display externally hosted images or links supplied by guild members. Those files may be hosted by third-party services rather than directly by Rotating Chaos.

WHO CAN SEE GUILD CONTENT

Most interactive community areas are restricted to authenticated guild members. Public-facing pages may still contain general guild information and links.

MEMBER CONTROL

Where supported, members can edit or remove content they created, including profiles, posts, gallery entries and event sign-ups.

Guild administrators may also moderate content where required once administrator permissions are enabled.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Rotating Chaos may link to services such as Discord, Firebase, image-hosting services and future merch providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

QUESTIONS

If you have a question about information displayed on the site, contact the Rotating Chaos guild team through Discord.
