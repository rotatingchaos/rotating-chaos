HOW IT WORKS

THAUMATURGE EXPLAINED

Thaumaturge is built around maintaining Chill, Rimefire Smolder and Arcane Mastery while lining up its hardest encounters with the party's burst windows.

It can also trade some personal damage for valuable magical and projectile debuffs, meaning the correct setup depends on both the fight and the other Wizards in the party.

That is why this build has several configurations instead of pretending one loadout magically works perfectly everywhere.

SINGLE TARGET

RECOMMENDED SETUP

When you're the only Wizard, Swath of Destruction is the recommended class feature.

It sacrifices a small amount of selfish damage but improves party damage against enemies affected by Smolder.

Orb of Imposition is a slightly stronger selfish option, but becomes more attractive when the relevant support is already covered elsewhere.

SUPPORT

FULL DEBUFF

The full debuff configuration uses Ray of Enfeeblement on Tab alongside Critical Conflagration and Swath of Destruction.

It is particularly useful for magical and projectile-heavy parties and when another Thaumaturge can exploit the support you're supplying.

PERSONAL DAMAGE

FULL DIESEL

Full Diesel is the maximum personal-damage configuration.

Only use it when another Thaumaturge is already covering Ray of Enfeeblement, Critical Conflagration and Swath of Destruction.

It depends heavily on maintaining maximum Arcane Mastery and Chill. If the stacks fall off, so does the reason for using the setup.

MASTER JOTUNSKAR

FIRST BOSS

The specialised first-boss setup uses Icy Terrain and Fanning the Flame because the Remorhaz and Basilisks can be grouped closely enough to damage multiple targets.

Icy Terrain can be placed on Tab to make the fight more comfortable without requiring permanent melee positioning.

Directed Flames is a major driver of this setup while the enemies remain properly grouped, with Rimefire Weaving supporting the Chill and Smolder mechanics.

ROTATION

WHEN MECHANICS RUIN YOUR BEAUTIFUL PLAN

Don't desperately restart the entire rotation because a mechanic interrupted it.

Most filler at-wills can be skipped if necessary. Preserve the important Chill and Arcane effects, maintain any required temporary critical bonus and continue the burst.

The current Void weapon setup provides less Recharge than the older Scintillant setup, so fitting the final returning encounter inside the call can be tighter.

MULTI TARGET

PACKS

For normal enemy packs, use the dedicated multi-target loadout rather than forcing the boss setup into everything.

Apply Chill and Smolder across the grouped enemies, maintain the important Wizard mechanics and then unload the larger area attacks.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Thaumaturge has two jobs: maintain all the clever Wizard shit and then unleash an unreasonable amount of fire and ice when the artifact call starts. 🔥❄️😂

If you're the only Wizard, don't automatically choose the selfish setup. Improving the other DPS can produce more total group damage than squeezing a little more damage out of yourself.

And on MJS first boss: get close enough to actually have Combat Advantage. Standing three counties away from the boss is very safe but somewhat inconvenient for the whole doing-damage part. 🤣
