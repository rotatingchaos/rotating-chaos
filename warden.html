THE BUILD

OVERVIEW

Warden is the Ranger's aggressive melee-focused DPS path, combining fast encounter use, stance mechanics and strong single-target damage. It rewards players who stay close to the fight, manage cooldowns cleanly and keep their attacks flowing instead of standing twenty feet away pretending they brought a bow for a reason.

The current Module 33.5 setup is particularly suited to boss fights. Warden's single-target powers are strong enough that the same core setup can also handle hybrid encounters containing smaller enemies alongside the boss.

Its weakness is dedicated AoE. Warden's multi-target powers simply don't compete as well, so Ranger players wanting maximum performance against large groups should keep a Hunter loadout available.

WARDEN IN ONE SENTENCE: Get close, hit everything rapidly, abuse your short cooldowns and occasionally remember that technically you're carrying a bow. 😂

PRIORITIES

STAT PRIORITIES

Warden follows the normal endgame DPS principle: maximise useful offensive percentages without wasting rating by pushing stats beyond their effective caps.

1 ◆ POWER Core damage stat and one of the first offensive percentages to establish.
2 ◆ COMBAT ADVANTAGE Extremely valuable when properly positioned. Warden already wants to fight in melee range, so maintaining Combat Advantage should be part of normal positioning.
3 ◆ CRITICAL STRIKE Build toward the useful cap while accounting for buffs and effects that activate during combat.
4 ◆ CRITICAL SEVERITY Works alongside Critical Strike and remains one of the major offensive priorities.
5 ◆ ACCURACY Finish Accuracy after the stronger offensive percentages are comfortably established.
DON'T BUILD FROM THE CHARACTER SHEET ALONE: Check your percentages in actual combat with food, companion bonuses and party buffs active. Pretty red numbers that are already capped aren't helping anyone.

LOADOUT

GEAR

The Module 33.5 Warden setup is built around current endgame damage gear with particular attention paid to balancing offensive percentages rather than blindly equipping the highest item-level piece in every slot.

HEAD

Use a strong current DPS headpiece that supplies a reliable damage bonus or whichever offensive percentage your final build still needs.

ARMOUR

Prioritise consistent offensive effects that remain active during boss fights rather than bonuses that look impressive but rarely trigger.

ARMS

The correct arms can change with scaled content. Don't ignore lower-item-level pieces whose damage bonus can outperform raw item level after scaling.

FEET

Use the slot to strengthen damage or finish an offensive stat without pushing another percentage beyond its useful limit.

WEAPONS

Current artifact weapons remain the endgame target. Weapon modifications should support your main powers and whatever offensive percentage is still missing.

RINGS

The current MJS Warden configuration uses the Arcane ring rather than the Wrath option, allowing the rest of the build to balance its offensive percentages more effectively.

MJS HEAD SWAP

The Combat Advantage headpiece from MSOD is used instead of the AJS legendary helmet in the current MJS configuration.

SHIRT & TROUSERS

Finish whichever offensive ratings are still short and favour useful damage bonuses over empty item-level inflation.

ENHANCEMENTS

ENCHANTMENTS & REINFORCEMENTS

Enchantments should complete the stat spread created by your gear, companions and mount bonuses. There is no benefit in copying an enchantment layout exactly if your character already receives those percentages somewhere else.

OFFENSIVE ENCHANTMENTS

Fill Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy according to whichever percentage is lowest once the full build is active.

COMBAT ENCHANTMENT

Use the current damage enchantment that best complements the rest of your setup and content rather than treating one option as mandatory for every loadout.

REINFORCEMENTS

Use armour and jewellery reinforcements to correct the final offensive stat balance instead of overcapping an already completed percentage.

POWER

ARTIFACTS

The active artifact should line up with the party's coordinated artifact call. Damage dealers benefit far more from everyone stacking compatible buffs and debuffs together than from five people randomly firing shiny objects whenever they remember they own them.

PRIMARY ARTIFACT

Use the offensive artifact assigned to you by the group and fire it as part of the coordinated burst window.

SECONDARY ARTIFACTS

Choose secondary artifacts for their offensive stats and useful set bonuses, balancing Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy.

YOUR CREW

COMPANIONS

Endgame Warden companion choices should maximise personal damage while still respecting the party's support setup. Some bonuses become substantially stronger when multiple players coordinate them.

SUMMONED COMPANION

Use a current high-performing damage or group-support companion appropriate to the content and the rest of the party.

OFFENSIVE BONUSES

Prioritise strong damage bonuses first, then use remaining slots to complete any offensive percentages still below target.

COMPANION ENHANCEMENT

Coordinate debuff enhancements with the group. Armor Break, Dulled Senses and Vulnerability are most useful when different party members cover different effects rather than everybody bringing the same one.

COMPANION GEAR

Use companion equipment to finish Combat Advantage and other offensive ratings while avoiding unnecessary overcaps.

RIDE INTO CHAOS

MOUNTS

Mounts provide another substantial chunk of Warden's offensive setup. Combat powers should contribute to burst damage or the group's debuffs, while insignias and equip powers complete the stat spread.

COMBAT POWER

Use a strong current offensive mount power and coordinate its use with the party's artifact call.

EQUIP POWER

Select whichever offensive percentage provides the most value to your actual build rather than automatically copying another Ranger's choice.

INSIGNIAS

Balance offensive insignias around Power, Combat Advantage, Critical Strike, Critical Severity and Accuracy.

COLLARS

Encounter damage, Critical Severity and useful movement or utility collars are strong choices depending on the final setup.

COMBAT

POWERS & FEATS

Warden's strength comes from its melee encounters and rapid cycling. The current Module 33.5 setup uses the same single-target power configuration for MJS first boss because the encounter mixes boss damage with nearby Remorhaz and Basilisks.

CORE COMBAT

HINDERING STRIKE

One of Warden's strongest melee encounters and an important part of the current single-target setup.

MELEE ENCOUNTERS

Keep encounters cycling aggressively. Warden performs best when you're actively using its short cooldowns rather than sitting on available powers.

STANCE MECHANIC

Ranger can move between ranged and melee powers, but this Warden setup is built primarily around extracting maximum value from the melee side.

AT-WILLS

Use at-wills as filler between encounter cycles rather than allowing gaps in damage while waiting for cooldowns.

FEATS & CLASS FEATURES

Build around the Warden features that strengthen melee combat, encounter cycling and sustained single-target damage. The goal is a smooth rotation where each power contributes to the next cycle instead of treating encounters as isolated buttons.

AOE WARNING: Warden's AoE options are not its strong point. If you're running content dominated by large trash packs, swap to Hunter rather than trying to force Warden into doing a job the other paragon does better.

PROGRESSION

BOONS

Build campaign boons around offensive performance first, filling the percentages that provide the most value to the final Warden setup.

  • Power
  • Combat Advantage
  • Critical Strike
  • Critical Severity
  • Accuracy
  • Recharge Speed where useful
  • Action Point Gain where useful
  • Movement or defensive utility after offensive priorities are covered

As with enchantments, boon points can move as your equipment changes. Use them to fix the build you actually have rather than preserving a screenshot from six gear upgrades ago.

PUTTING IT TOGETHER

ROTATION

Warden is a rhythm build rather than a character where you wait around for one enormous button. Keep encounters moving, maintain Combat Advantage and place your strongest damage inside coordinated party burst windows.

GENERAL SINGLE TARGET FLOW

  1. GET INTO MELEE RANGE Warden wants to be close enough to use its strongest melee powers consistently.
  2. ESTABLISH COMBAT ADVANTAGE Position properly before committing your major encounters.
  3. OPEN YOUR ENCOUNTER CYCLE Start cycling the melee encounters rather than holding powers unnecessarily.
  4. HINDERING STRIKE Keep this high-value encounter working inside the main damage cycle.
  5. AT-WILL FILLER Fill the small gaps while encounters recover.
  6. REPEAT THE CYCLE Warden benefits from maintaining pressure. Don't allow the rotation to stall while usable encounters sit glowing on the bar.

ARTIFACT CALL

  1. POSITION FIRST Make sure you're in melee range and have Combat Advantage before the call begins.
  2. ACTIVATE GROUP BURST Fire your assigned mount and artifact at the correct point in the coordinated call.
  3. FRONT-LOAD STRONG ENCOUNTERS Put the hardest-hitting melee encounters into the period where the boss has the greatest number of group debuffs active.
  4. KEEP CYCLING Continue using encounters as they recover rather than treating the artifact activation as the end of the rotation.

MJS FIRST BOSS

Keep the single-target Warden setup. The Remorhaz and Basilisks spend enough time close to the boss that two of the multi-target-capable powers still gain value without sacrificing the stronger single-target configuration.

OTHER OPTIONS

ALTERNATIVES & SWAPS

DEDICATED AOE

Swap to Hunter. Seriously. Warden's AoE kit isn't where the paragon shines and Ranger already has a better option sitting right there.

MJS

Use the current single-target setup with the Arcane ring and Combat Advantage MSOD headpiece for the specialised MJS configuration.

STAT SWAPS

Change enchantments, insignias, companion gear and secondary artifacts whenever a new item pushes one offensive percentage beyond its useful target.

PROGRESSION

If you don't own the endgame pieces yet, use the strongest available damage gear while maintaining a sensible offensive stat balance. The rotation matters considerably more than owning every perfect shiny thing immediately.

FROM THE GUILD

CHAOS NOTES

Warden is for Rangers who looked at the bow, looked at the enemy and decided, "Nah, I'm going over there personally." 😂

Don't get obsessed with making one loadout do absolutely everything. Warden is excellent where it is excellent: strong melee and single-target damage. For rooms containing seventeen angry bastards, you've got Hunter. Use the bloody loadout system. 🤣

The other big trap is sitting on encounters waiting for the "perfect" moment. Outside the coordinated burst window, keep the rotation moving. Warden likes pressure, rhythm and uptime — not standing around admiring its cooldown icons.
