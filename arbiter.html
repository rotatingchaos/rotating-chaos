HOW IT WORKS

ARBITER EXPLAINED

Arbiter is the damage-focused Cleric paragon and one of the more demanding DPS paths to play well. It rewards encounter management, timing and careful setup with extremely strong burst damage.

The heart of the build is managing Divinity and Perfect Balance while building powerful Prophecy of Doom windows. The order and timing of your encounters matter.

During sustained rotations, Lance of Faith can be held while waiting for Perfect Balance to activate, adding extra damage to the following Forgemaster's Flame.

ALTERNATIVES

SWAPS & TRADE-OFFS

EVOLVING FORM VS SCINTILLANT

Evolving Form is the preferred Module 33.5 set for the higher damage ceiling. Scintillant remains useful while progressing, particularly because of its recharge contribution.

DEXTERITY VS STRENGTH

A Strength-heavy variation can produce a small damage increase but sacrifices recharge speed. That recharge then needs to be recovered elsewhere through options such as another Tactician's Precision or an alternative cooldown bonus.

Dexterity gives the build more breathing room for lag, delayed casts and late artifact calls.

COMPANION DEBUFF SWAPS

Armor Break, Dulled Senses and Vulnerability can be distributed differently depending on what the rest of the party is already running. Coordinate rather than duplicating them unnecessarily.
