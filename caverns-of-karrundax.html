CAVERNS OF KARRUNDAX

Fight through the fiery caverns and bring down Karrundax.

OVERVIEW

CAVERNS OF KARRUNDAX

Caverns of Karrundax takes the group through Mount Hotenow and its red dragon forces before a final battle against Karrundax.

DUNGEON

THROUGH THE CAVERNS

  • Much of the dungeon consists of groups of enemies positioned throughout the caverns.
  • Mounts can be used through large sections of the dungeon.
  • Groups can move past some enemy packs, but enemies may continue chasing players who have not escaped them.
  • Keep the group together when moving through the dungeon so players are not left behind dealing with enemies alone.

FINAL BOSS

KARRUNDAX

  • Karrundax is a red dragon and uses heavy fire attacks throughout the encounter.
  • Watch the arena carefully for fire and damaging areas appearing around the group.
  • Move out of dangerous areas rather than remaining stationary while attacking.
  • Karrundax periodically takes flight, temporarily moving away from the group.
  • Be ready to reposition when Karrundax lands and continue attacking once he becomes available again.
  • Smaller red dragon enemies can also appear during the encounter and should be dealt with while maintaining awareness of Karrundax.

QUICK REFERENCE

DON'T BECOME DRAGON FOOD

  • Stay together while moving through the caverns.
  • Don't leave slower group members trapped with enemy packs.
  • Avoid Karrundax's fire attacks.
  • Reposition when the dragon takes flight and lands.
  • Deal with additional enemies while keeping track of Karrundax.
