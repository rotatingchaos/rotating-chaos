THE DUNGEON

OVERVIEW

Lair of the Mad Dragon is built around controlled boss damage, priority adds and positioning.

The first two bosses punish groups that burn the boss too quickly without handling spawned enemies.

The final dragon requires good positioning, recognition of floor patterns and careful management of poison areas.

BOSS ONE

M'RAEL

IMPS

M'rael summons Imps at specific health thresholds.

Imps are priority targets and should be killed immediately.

Different Imps can buff enemy damage, create fire pools or heal nearby enemies.

Do not burn M'rael through multiple health thresholds too quickly or several Imps can become active at once.

Control boss damage and switch to the Imps whenever they spawn.

RED AREAS

Red areas regularly appear on the floor.

Move out immediately.

Being hit applies a debuff that increases incoming damage.

CONE ATTACK

M'rael uses a large cone attack.

Move out of the cone early because the hit can land sooner than the visual suggests.

The cone can kill almost any player, including the tank.

Tanks should position M'rael so the cone is not aimed toward DPS or spawned Imps.

SOAK ATTACK

M'rael can temporarily disappear and return with a greyed health bar.

This signals a heavy soak attack.

The tank should move close to the boss and take the mechanic.

DPS should stay clear rather than entering the soak area.

On Master difficulty, additional larger fiends also spawn alongside the regular adds.

BOSS TWO

SUMMONERS & WARLOCKS

SUMMONERS

The second boss summons Warlocks and Summoners at health thresholds.

Summoners are the highest priority target.

If left alive, Summoners begin spawning Hounds.

Hounds are extremely dangerous and can quickly cause a wipe.

Crowd control Summoners immediately and kill them before they can summon Hounds.

WARLOCKS

Warlocks increase enemy damage resistance.

They are lower priority than Summoners, but should still be killed before additional waves cause too much resistance to build.

ADD WAVES

Above 50% boss health, waves contain one Summoner and one Warlock.

At 50% and below, waves begin containing two Summoners.

Below approximately 20%, waves occur more frequently.

Late waves can contain two Summoners and two Warlocks.

Slow boss damage if necessary so the group can clear each wave before triggering the next.

Enter each health threshold with crowd control and burst damage ready.

FLAME PILLARS

Flame Pillars can target players standing too far away from the boss.

The affected area is larger than it appears.

Being hit applies heavy damage over time.

Keep the group close to the boss whenever possible.

Ranged companions or effects positioned far away can also trigger Flame Pillars.

The damage-over-time effect can be cleansed.

PUSH MECHANIC

The boss creates a red area before pushing players away.

Move or dodge out before the push lands.

A second outer red area follows and deals damage over time.

Avoid the outer danger zone and move back in once it clears.

MULTI-TARGET BUSTER

The boss regularly uses a heavy attack on the tank.

The attack can also hit nearby players.

Tanks should position away from DPS before the attack lands.

DPS should remain behind or beside the boss rather than standing with the tank.

FINAL BOSS

GREEN DRAGON

The dragon uses standard dragon mechanics, including Roar, Wing attacks, Bite and poison pools.

Avoid standing directly in front of the dragon.

Avoid persistent poison areas on the ground.

Tanks should position the dragon near the edge of the arena where possible.

RED TEARS

Tear-like markings appear on the ground in different formations.

These markings become damaging red areas moments later.

Do not wait for the red telegraph before moving.

Learn the pattern and move into a safe gap as soon as the tears appear.

Several different formations can occur.

LEAP & POISON BREATH

The dragon leaps toward or behind the tank.

After landing, it uses a dangerous poison breath attack.

The tank must reposition quickly so the breath is aimed away from the group.

Fighting near the edge allows the tank to direct the breath safely toward the wall or away from the party.

Failing to reposition can cause the breath to hit the DPS behind the dragon.

POISON POOLS

Red circles appear beneath players before poison pools form.

Move out immediately.

The resulting poison can be difficult to see once the red marker disappears.

Remember where each pool was placed and avoid returning to those areas.

Standing in the poison applies additional damage and can increase incoming damage.

FINAL DAMAGE CHECK

Near the end of the fight, the dragon deals repeated group-wide burst damage.

Each burst applies a debuff that causes later hits to deal more damage.

Healers and tanks should prepare mitigation.

Burn the remaining boss health before the repeated damage overwhelms the group.

KNOW YOUR JOB

TANK ◆ HEALER ◆ DPS

TANK

Keep M'rael's cone facing away from the group.

When M'rael returns with a grey health bar, move close and take the soak.

During the second boss, stay away from DPS when the tank buster is incoming.

Position the dragon near the edge and turn its poison breath away from the group after each leap.

HEALER

Watch players hit by M'rael's red areas because they take increased incoming damage.

Cleanse Flame Pillar damage-over-time during the second boss where possible.

During the final damage check, prepare group healing and mitigation for the repeated bursts.

DPS

Stop attacking M'rael and kill Imps immediately when they spawn.

During the second boss, Summoners are priority number one.

Crowd control and kill them before they summon Hounds.

Kill Warlocks after the Summoners.

During the dragon, recognise the tear patterns early and move before they become red.

WHAT USUALLY GOES WRONG

COMMON WIPES

Burning M'rael through several Imp thresholds at once.

Ignoring Imps while they buff, heal or create additional hazards.

Moving too late for M'rael's cone attack.

DPS entering the tank's soak mechanic.

Failing to crowd control and kill Summoners quickly.

Allowing Summoners to begin spawning Hounds.

Burning the second boss through several add thresholds before previous waves are dead.

Standing too far away and triggering Flame Pillars.

Standing beside the tank during the multi-target buster.

Waiting for the dragon's tear patterns to become red before moving.

The tank failing to turn the poison breath away after the dragon leaps.

Forgetting where invisible poison pools were dropped.

Failing to finish the dragon during the escalating final damage check.

CHAOS NOTES

M'rael spawns Imps at health thresholds. If you keep nuking the boss, you can spawn several at once. So occasionally the correct DPS rotation is apparently "stop fucking DPSing."

Second boss: Summoner first. Warlock second. Boss afterwards. Hounds everywhere means somebody has already fucked up step one.

Stay reasonably close to the second boss. Apparently wandering off to Narnia summons pillars of fire. Neverwinter has opinions about ranged players.

Dragon tears appear before the actual red danger zone. That is your warning. Waiting until the floor turns red is basically waiting for written confirmation that you're about to get twatted.

Dragon jumps behind the tank? Tank turns it away. Everyone else remains safely at the non-poison-breath end of the dragon.
